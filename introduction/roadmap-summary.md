# 🗺️ Roadmap

**Pixel Perfect Update - June 2026**

Our Pixel Journey, now over **1050 days strong** since launching on WAX in 2023 with Pixal PFPs minted in Spring 2024, continues as a beacon of educational Web3 exploration. We've evolved our phasing from rigid yearly cycles to **flexible, milestone-driven Seasons** to better reflect real progress and community needs.

### **Extended Season 1 – Foundational Mastery on WAX (2023–mid 2026+)**

The bedrock of everything. Focused on building deep roots in the WAX ecosystem while developing our powerful **PxPackages** framework.

#### Key Achievements & Developments:
- **PXJ Tokenomics**: Yearly minting at New Year’s Eve — ~10% of remaining unminted supply allocated to **dao.pxj, team.pxj, dev.pxj, defi.pxj, craft.pxj** pools. This sustainable model supports ongoing rewards, staking, and crafting.
- **Pixal PFPs & Crafting Economy**: 10k collection with robust recycling/blending mechanics.
- **PxPackages Development (Late 2025 – mid 2026)**: Intensive Alpha experimentation with miniDApps, followed by a comprehensive rebuild using shared modular functionality. This powers future scalability and our PxLearn Portal.
- Community governance via Rock Council, leaderboards, and educational content.

#### **Why This Matters**:
Every step teaches real Web3 skills — wallets, marketplaces, DeFi, NFT utility — while building lasting value.

### **Future Milestone Phases**

- **Multi-Chain Expansion**: Bridges, new ecosystems, enhanced PxPackages utility.
- **PxLearn Portal Launch**: Comprehensive educational hub drawing from our GitBook.
- **Advanced DeFi, Gaming & Community Tools**: Powered by our refined PxPackages.

Join us as we continue this epic, pixelish adventure! Together we've achieved so much — and the brightest pixels are ahead. 🚀🧩
