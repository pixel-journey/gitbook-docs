### The Evolution of Our Phased Journey

Pixel Journey began with the vision of structured annual "Seasons" to guide our growth. However, as with many ambitious projects in Web3, real-world development has taught us that deep, high-quality progress often requires more time and iteration than a rigid yearly calendar allows.

**Season 1** has served as our extended foundational phase on the **WAX blockchain**, spanning nearly three years (launching in early 2024 and continuing as our core focus through mid-2026 and beyond as needed). This longer runway has enabled us to build a truly solid, sustainable base rather than rushing features.

A particularly intensive period of development occurred from **late 2025 through the first half of 2026**. During this time, we dedicated significant resources to creating and refining our **PxPackages** framework. This involved:

- Developing multiple **Alpha Experimentation miniDApps** to test real-world concepts, user experiences, technical integrations, and mechanics in a live environment.
- Conducting thorough research, iterative testing, community feedback loops, and performance analysis.
- Ultimately rebuilding and consolidating the learnings into a much stronger, modular, and shared functionality layer powered by PxPackages.

This work has dramatically improved the scalability, maintainability, and future-readiness of our entire ecosystem. It represents one of the most important technical maturation steps in Season 1 and sets a powerful foundation for everything that follows.

We have also established a clear and consistent **PXJ token release mechanism**: At each **New Year's Eve**, we mint approximately **10% of the remaining unminted PXJ supply**. These newly minted tokens are allocated across our five core pools — **dao.pxj**, **team.pxj**, **dev.pxj**, **defi.pxj**, and **craft.pxj**. This yearly cadence has been our practice for the past two years and will continue, providing predictable, sustainable support for governance, team operations, development, DeFi utility, and crafting economies.

Our overall journey mechanics emphasize **sustainability, balanced tokenomics (burn/faucet dynamics), long-term value creation, and genuine community ownership**. Education and making Web3 accessible, fun, and rewarding remain at the heart of everything we do.

### Season 1 – WAX Foundation & Core Ecosystem (Extended Foundational Phase)

This extended phase has been all about building the bedrock of Pixel Journey on WAX. Key achievements and focus areas include:

#### PXJ Token & Liquidity

- **PXJ Token Launch & Ongoing Releases**: Our native utility token launched on WAX with initial liquidity on platforms like Alcor and TacoSwap. The token supports staking, rewards, crafting, DeFi mechanics, and governance.
- **Yearly Supply Releases**: As described above, we release ~10% of remaining supply each New Year's Eve into the five core allocation pools. This replaces the original per-season model with a more predictable and sustainable yearly rhythm that has proven effective.
- **Liquidity & Rewards**: Significant portions of early allocations supported LP staking rewards and NFT/collectible incentives to bootstrap the economy.

#### Community Growth & Partnerships

- Building a vibrant, engaged community through Discord, events, and collaborations.
- Establishing strong partnerships within the WAX ecosystem and beyond.
- Creating roles, levels, and lounges that reward participation and contribution.

#### PFP Collection & Pixel Recycling

- **Pixals PFP Collection**: Successfully launched our flagship **10,000-piece Pixal PFP collection** in early 2024 on NFTHive, giving holders ownership, utility, and a stake in the journey.
- **Pixel Recycling & Crafting**: Innovative mechanics allowing up to 80% of Pixals to be burned for PixelPacks, which can then be blended and crafted into new collectibles using NeftyBlocks and NFTHive tools. This creates ongoing engagement and a circular economy.

#### Upgrading, Staking & Collectibles

- Advanced blending contracts for pixel upgrades and sophisticated collectible crafting.
- Randomized airdrops (pets, rocks, etc.) tied to PFP traits.
- Staking mechanics for collectibles and PXJ, with rewards governed in part by the community through PixelDAO/WaxRocks.

#### Governance & Community Involvement

- Formation of the **WaxRocks Council** (early PixelDAO structure): A community advisory board empowering members to vote on key decisions.
- NFT and token holder roles that grant real influence and perks in Discord and project direction.

#### PxPackages Development & Technical Maturation (Late 2025 – Mid 2026 Focus)

As highlighted earlier, this has been a cornerstone of the latter half of Season 1. We moved from initial Alpha miniDApp experiments to a complete rebuild using shared PxPackages functionality. This has strengthened the entire backend, improved developer experience, and prepared us for smoother multi-chain and multi-feature expansions.

#### Milestone Events & dApp Evolution

- Ongoing blending and crafting events that bring the community together.
- Progressive dApp improvements and the major technical consolidation via PxPackages.
- Continued focus on education, tools, and onboarding resources to help new explorers join the journey.

Our Season 1 work has been deliberate and thorough. We prioritized quality, sustainability, and real utility over rushing to the next phase.

### Future Phases – Flexible, Milestone-Driven Growth

Rather than locking ourselves into strict yearly Seasons, we are evolving toward a more **adaptive, milestone-based approach** to phases. Each new phase will be triggered by meaningful achievements — such as completing core infrastructure, launching major new features or collections, successful multi-chain integrations, or reaching community and utility milestones.

This flexibility allows every phase to fully mature, reduces pressure for artificial deadlines, and keeps the focus on delivering real value.

#### Next Phase Vision (Multi-Chain Expansion & Enhanced Experiences)

Building on the strong WAX foundation and PxPackages, the next major phase will focus on:

- **Multi-Chain Expansion**: Bringing Pixel Journey experiences, collectibles, and utility to additional blockchains (starting with strong bridges to Polygon, Ethereum, and potentially others).
- **New NFT Collections & Mechanics**: Fresh PFP series or themed collections with new traits, utilities, and integration with PxPackages.
- **Enhanced dApps & User Experience**: Leveraging the matured PxPackages for more intuitive, powerful, and feature-rich applications.
- **Deeper Economy & DeFi**: Expanded crafting, staking, and DeFi opportunities across pools and chains.
- **Continued Education & Onboarding**: More guides, interactive learning, and tools to welcome newcomers.
- **Community Events & Surprises**: Giveaways, competitions, collaborative drops, and exciting reveals in Web3 gaming, memes, and cross-chain play.

Community input will continue to shape priorities through Discord contribution channels and governance mechanisms.
