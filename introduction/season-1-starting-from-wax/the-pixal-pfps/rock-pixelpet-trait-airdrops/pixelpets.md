# 🐇 PixelPets

### Pixel Pet Completist Discord Role

Complete your Pixal PixelPet collection (getting one of each Pixal PFPs Pixel Pet traits), and honeycomb will automatically grant you the Pixel Pet Completist role on Discord!

This role grants you:

* Occasional extra airdrops on the Discord
* An extra Discord XP Leveling Boost
* Additional Voting power in our WaxRock Council (if you own a WaxRock for access), or in our community wide votes
* Access to our "MEGA-DROPS" channel (alongside Ingredient Completists, Pixal Stackers of +50 Pixals, or WaxRock holders)



### Next planned utility

PixelPets will function as small characters to utilize in a planned mini-game (ETA for release towards the end of '25), with more info to come. Before then they will be airdropped as separate NFTs to their Pixal Holders.
