Our KING of Pixals meme, was created on the 12th of December within the first hour of the wax.fun platform, as the 5th meme of the platform.

As per the initial Wax.fun meme configurations, our KING initiated its launch from a $3250 market cap rate from where piga.pxj as the creator bought \~2.5% supply for approximately 2500WAX.

Within the first few days our meme clearly stood out as being backed by the most active project/community, here with our Pixel Journey, and reached a market cap of $10000 within less than 36 hours.

Within a week (on the 6th day), we reached a market cap above $22000 which granted our KING the new title, as the first meme ever, to become the "King of Wax" to receive a spotlight on the wax.fun platforms frontpage!
