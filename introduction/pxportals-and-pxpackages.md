## PxPackages — The Modular Foundation

Behind the portals sits **PxPackages** — our modular, reusable framework for building mini-dApps and experiences. Think composable building blocks that power everything from simple tools to complex portals. This is the technical backbone that lets us deliver state-of-the-art, maintainable, and extensible experiences at scale.

More details and technical deep-dives will drop as the portals launch. For now, know that everything is being built with the same care you'd expect from someone who's lived the full journey: network engineering precision, photographic eye for visuals, UX design obsession for flow and clarity, and Web3 educator passion for making complex things simple and empowering.
