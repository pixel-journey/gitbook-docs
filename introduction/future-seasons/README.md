# 🌌 Future Seasons

Season 1 has barely begun, all about the Wax blockchain and ecosystem. As part of Season 1, we have esthablished our first parts of the Pixel Journey:

* PXJ and our Pixel Economy/DeFi on the Wax Blockchain
* Pixal PFPs
* Pixel Recycling
* Collectibles and token staking platforms
* Blending and collectibles crafting utility
* PXJ/KING DeFi volume based burn positions
* NFT/Token holder roles to reflect ownership in our Discord Community (and grand perks/PixelDAO voting weight!)
