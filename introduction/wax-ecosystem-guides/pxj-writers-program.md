# PXJ Writers Program — Earn PXJ by Creating Educational Content

The **PXJ Writers Program** is our way of rewarding community members who help create and improve high-quality educational content for the Pixel Journey and the wider WAX/Web3 ecosystem. It empowers creators, educators, and enthusiasts to share their knowledge while earning $PXJ rewards through community and governance support.

You can unlock the program by reaching **Level 20** in our Discord (very achievable with regular participation, even faster with XP boosts from holding Pixals or boosting the server).

## Why It Matters for Pixel Journey Users

- **Build the Ecosystem**: Your contributions directly improve resources that help new explorers, collectors, creators, and partners understand and engage with Pixel Journey and WAX.
- **Earn While Contributing**: Get rewarded in $PXJ for valuable content that aligns with our educational mission.
- **Community Recognition**: Approved work is integrated into official docs, shared on socials, or used in Discord — giving you visibility and impact.
- **Skill Development**: Hone your writing, research, and Web3 knowledge in a supportive environment.
- **Long-Term Value**: High-quality content helps grow the community, which benefits everyone involved in the Pixel Journey economy and culture.

## How It Works

1. **Unlock Access**: Reach Level 20 in Discord to join the dedicated **Writers Lounge**.
2. **Create Content**: Write original guides, threads, articles, lore, tutorials, or ecosystem overviews related to Pixel Journey, WAX, or relevant Web3 topics.
3. **Submit for Review**: Post in the Writers Lounge for community feedback and approval.
4. **Integration & Proposal**: Once approved, content may be integrated into our official docs or used elsewhere. We then submit a proposal to the **WaxRocks Council / PixelDAO** (or relevant governance) to reward contributors with $PXJ.
5. **Receive Rewards**: Approved contributions are rewarded based on impact, depth, and alignment with Pixel Journey goals.

## Example Rewards (Approximate Guidelines)

Rewards can vary based on quality, effort, impact, and community feedback. They may be split for collaborative work:

- Original thread or social media post about Pixel Journey or WAX topics → ~5,000 PXJ
- In-depth article or guide covering aspects of the project or ecosystem → ~25,000–50,000 PXJ
- Writing or significantly updating an official docs page → ~5,000–15,000 PXJ
- Chapter or section of Pixel Journey lore → up to ~25,000 PXJ
- Linking to our docs from your own publications or external content → ~500 PXJ per link
- Ecosystem guides or overviews that mention or link to Pixel Journey → up to 10,000 PXJ
- Major bugfixes, substantial updates, or improvements to existing content → Smaller reward (amount depends on impact and effort)

These are guidelines — actual rewards depend on proposal approval and community input.

## Best Practices for Success

- **Original & Valuable**: Focus on unique insights, clear explanations, visuals (where helpful), and practical value for readers.
- **On-Topic**: Tie content to Pixel Journey, WAX tools, NFTs, DeFi, education, or community building.
- **Quality Over Quantity**: Well-researched, well-structured pieces with good readability tend to perform better.
- **Engage Community**: Seek feedback in the Writers Lounge before final submission.
- **Follow Guidelines**: Check the Writers Lounge pinned messages for current submission formats and topics of interest.
- **Be Consistent**: Regular contributions build your reputation and may lead to more opportunities.

## Safety, DYOR & Community Notes

- This is a community-driven program — all rewards go through transparent governance proposals.
- Legitimate Pixel Journey team members will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys, or sensitive info.
- Always verify official links and Discord channels.
- If something seems off (e.g., unsolicited DMs about rewards), report it in Discord.

## How to Get Started

1. Level up to 20 in Discord (participate in chats, hold Pixals for XP boosts, boost the server if possible).
2. Join the Writers Lounge channel.
3. Read the pinned guidelines and examples.
4. Start drafting your first piece — even a short thread on a topic you're passionate about counts!
5. Submit for review and iterate based on feedback.

This program is one of the ways we give back to the community while building better resources for everyone. Your knowledge and writing skills directly help new explorers and strengthen our shared journey!
