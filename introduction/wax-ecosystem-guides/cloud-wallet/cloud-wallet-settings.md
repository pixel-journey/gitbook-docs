# Cloud Wallet Settings

The Settings section in Cloud Wallet lets you manage important account options, including:

- Security settings
- Account claim / key export
- Notification preferences
- Linked social accounts
- General account information

Access it from the menu in your Cloud Wallet dashboard. It's a good idea to review your settings periodically, especially after claiming keys or making changes to your account.

For the most up-to-date options, simply log into your Cloud Wallet and explore the Settings area directly.
