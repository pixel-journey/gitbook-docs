# Claiming your Cloud Wallet account keys

{% embed url="https://www.mycloudwallet.com/settings/account-claim" %}
[https://www.mycloudwallet.com/settings/account-claim](https://www.mycloudwallet.com/settings/account-claim)
{% endembed %}

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Claiming your private keys from Cloud Wallet gives you more control and flexibility. You can import your account into other wallets (like Anchor) if you ever want to.

There are two options:

- **Soft Claim**: Get your keys while still keeping full use of Cloud Wallet.
- **Hard Claim**: Get your keys and remove Cloud Wallet's permissions (more advanced — only do this if you're comfortable managing keys yourself).

**Why claim keys?**
- Backup your account independently
- Use your account in other WAX wallets and dApps
- Greater long-term control

Follow the on-screen process on the linked page. Always store your keys securely and never share them with anyone.
