# WAX.FUN

### WAX.FUN Introduction

WAX.FUN is in many ways similar to the very popular "pumpdotfun" platform from the Solana network

* All tokens launched on WAX.FUN have a token supply of 1 Billion Tokens (1 000 000 000)
* A meme launch allows for 80% of the Total Token Supply (800M Tokens) are sold on a bonding curve starting at a $1250 market cap rate, and completing the distribution curve once the meme hits a $22000 market cap valuation
* The "King Of Wax" title can be claimed only once by each meme, the first time they pass $9k market cap
* The bonding curves are unique from meme to meme, and subject to being influenced by the rate of $WAXP at the time of their meme creation (a meme created in a low $WAXP value moment will require a lot more WAXP to reach it's bonding target, than a meme that is created in a moment where WAXP is much higher)
* Token transfers for memes in their launching/bonding progress are locked till they complete the bonding curve and are released to Alcor Exchange
* Upon bonding of a meme, all the liquidity raised on the WAX.FUN platform is paired up with the remaining 20% of total supply (after 80% of total supply has been sold along the curve)
* A token is fully distributed/circulating once the meme reaches graduation, and is released
* Memes can have the same name/ticker on the WAX.FUN platform; the first will be created under the alpha.waxfun token contract, with the second of the same ticker being created under beta.waxfun and so forth
* There is a 1% Buy and Sell platform fee that goes to main.waxfun after each trade on the WAX.FUN platform (till the bonding goal is reached, and the LP/swap functionality transfers into Alcor .. for future trades to burn into the renounced liquidity, or other liquidity providers)
* Platform offers easy Transaction History, and Holders distribution overviews
* Each meme has a comment thread feed, that anyone logged into the platform can write up to 5 comments on each memes pages (so be careful not to waste your messages!)
* The frontpage offers easy sorting of different options to either find the latest featured/actively traded memes, the most recently traded, the highest market cap memes and .. more!
