# Uncut — Social Platform for NFT Collectors & Creators (2026)

**Uncut** is a social platform built specifically for NFT collectors and creators. It integrates directly with WAX (and supports other chains) and allows you to showcase your collection, share drops and updates, connect with the community, and engage in a more visual, NFT-focused way than general social media.

It functions similarly to Twitter/X or Instagram but is tailored for the NFT space, with easy ways to display your Pixals, other holdings, and creations. You can access Uncut directly from Cloud Wallet (via WAX Social features) or through their main site.

## Why Uncut Matters for Pixel Journey Users

- **Showcase Your Collection**: Easily display and share your Pixals, crafted items, and other NFTs in a dedicated community.
- **Community Connection**: Engage with like-minded collectors, creators, and projects in an NFT-centric environment.
- **Share Drops & Updates**: Post about new mints, events, or Pixel Journey activities to reach an engaged audience.
- **Cross-Chain Potential**: While focused on WAX integration, it supports visibility across ecosystems.
- **Visual & Engaging**: Better suited for NFTs than general platforms, making it easier to highlight traits, rarity, or stories behind your pieces.

**Pixel Journey Perspective**: Uncut is a great supplementary tool for sharing your Pixel Journey progress, connecting with the community, and amplifying drops or educational content. It complements AtomicHub, F12Key, and other tools by providing a social layer to your collection and activities.

## Getting Started on Uncut

1. Visit the official site or access via Cloud Wallet's WAX Social features.
2. Connect your Anchor Wallet (recommended) or Cloud Wallet.
3. Set up your profile and link your WAX collections for easy showcasing.
4. Start posting, following other collectors/creators, and engaging with NFT-focused content.
5. Use it to share your Pixals, lore, or journey updates with the community.
