# WaxLabs Guide - 2026

**Overview**: WAX Labs is the official funding and grant platform for the WAX ecosystem. It supports developers, creators, and community projects building on WAX, including tools, games, dApps, and Pixel Journey initiatives.

## Getting Started
1. Visit [waxlabs.io](https://waxlabs.io/).
2. Connect your Cloud or Anchor Wallet.
3. Browse active proposals or submit your own.

## How to Submit a Proposal
- Detailed plan, milestones, budget in WAXP.
- Community voting via WAXDAO or governance.
- Examples: F12Key, new NFT tools, PXJ features.

## Key Features
- Transparent funding process.
- Proposal tracking and voting.
- Successful projects: marketplaces, explorers, games.

## PXJ Tips
- Propose tools for Pixal minting, recycling, or community events.
- Track funded PXJ-related projects.

## Best Practices
- Research similar proposals.
- Engage community for feedback.
- Deliver on milestones for future funding.
