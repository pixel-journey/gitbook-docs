# WaxLabs — Official WAX Grants & Funding Platform (2026)

**WaxLabs** is the official grant and funding platform for the WAX blockchain ecosystem. It supports developers, creators, projects, and community initiatives building on WAX — including tools, games, dApps, marketplaces, explorers, and Pixel Journey-related features. Funded projects often receive WAXP grants through a transparent proposal and voting process via WAXDAO.

## Why WaxLabs Matters for Pixel Journey Users

- **Ecosystem Support**: Many funded projects directly benefit Pixel Journey users, such as new tools for minting, recycling, trading, or community events.
- **Proposal Opportunities**: Active community members can submit proposals for PXJ enhancements, new features, or educational tools.
- **Transparency & Governance**: Proposals are voted on by the community, ensuring alignment with ecosystem needs.
- **Backup & Innovation**: Successful grants often lead to reliable alternatives during platform maintenance (e.g., new explorers or marketplaces).
- **Community Impact**: Supports the growth of the broader WAX and Antelope ecosystem that Pixel Journey is built on.

**Pixel Journey Perspective**: WaxLabs is a key resource for anyone looking to contribute to or benefit from WAX innovations. Propose tools that enhance Pixal minting, crafting, or economy participation, or track funded projects that improve your experience as a collector or creator.

## Getting Started on WaxLabs

1. Visit the official site: [waxlabs.io](https://waxlabs.io/).
2. Connect your Anchor Wallet (recommended) or Cloud Wallet.
3. Browse active or past proposals to see examples and funded projects.
4. Review the proposal guidelines and submission process.
5. If submitting, prepare a detailed plan with milestones, budget in WAXP, and community impact.

## Safety, DYOR & Best Practices

- Always use the official site (waxlabs.io) — bookmark it.
- Research similar past proposals before submitting to understand what succeeds.
- Engage the community (Discord, forums) for feedback on your ideas.
- Deliver on milestones if funded to build credibility for future proposals.
- Be cautious of unofficial sites or scams claiming to be affiliated with WaxLabs.
- DYOR on any project before interacting or investing time/resources.
