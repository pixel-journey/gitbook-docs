# Listing a single NFT

To list a single NFT on Neftyblocks:

1. Go to your inventory and find the NFT you want to sell.
2. Click the **"List on market"** button.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (119).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This opens the listing overlay where you can:

- Enter a price in **WAX** or **USD** (USD listings automatically adjust to the current WAX price).
- See useful market history and recent sales for that template.
- View the fee breakdown (Collection royalty + Marketplace fee + Tokenomics fee).
- Use the quick % buttons to price relative to the current floor.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Tip:** Some NFTs have mutable attributes, and certain mint numbers (like #1, #42, or low serials) can be more valuable. Always do your own research (DYOR) before listing.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (118).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can also view an interactive price history graph to help decide on a fair price.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (122).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once you're happy with the price, review the net amount you'll receive after fees and confirm the transaction in your wallet.

If successful, your NFT will appear on the market!
