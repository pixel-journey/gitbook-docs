# Bulk listing NFTs

Neftyblocks supports bulk listing, which is very useful when selling multiple Pixals or other assets at once.

### How to Bulk List

1. In your inventory, select the NFTs you want to list by clicking the checkboxes.
2. Click the **"Bulk actions"** button and choose **"List on market"**.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (125).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You then have two options:

#### By Assets (Individual Pricing)

You can set a custom price for each selected NFT. You can also use the quick pricing tools at the bottom to apply percentage-based pricing to all selected items at once.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (127).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Note:** If listing more than ~10 NFTs, you may need to sign multiple transactions (often in batches of 10).

#### By Templates

This option groups identical templates together. You set one price per template, and it applies to all selected copies of that template.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (130).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
You can freely switch between the **Assets** and **Templates** tabs while setting prices. However, if you accidentally close the window before confirming, you will lose all progress.
{% endhint %}

Once all prices are set, confirm the transactions in your wallet to complete the bulk listing.
