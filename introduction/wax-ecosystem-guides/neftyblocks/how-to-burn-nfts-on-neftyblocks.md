# How to burn NFTs on Neftyblocks

{% hint style="danger" %}
**BEWARE: Burning NFTs is permanent and irreversible.**

Most burning happens automatically inside blends or other mechanics. It is very rare that you need to manually burn an NFT from your inventory.

When an NFT is burned, the original creator gets their RAM back — **not you** as the current holder. Burning does **not** free up RAM in your own account unless you created the NFT.

**Only burn an NFT if you have a clear and specific reason.** It is almost never recommended otherwise.
{% endhint %}

### How to Burn an NFT

1. Go to your inventory on Neftyblocks.
2. Click the three-dot menu on the NFT you want to burn.
3. Select **"Burn NFT"** and confirm the transaction in your wallet.
