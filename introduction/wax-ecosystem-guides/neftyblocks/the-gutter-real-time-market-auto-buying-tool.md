# The Gutter (real-time market/auto-buying tool)

The Gutter is a powerful real-time market tool on Neftyblocks that helps you automatically snipe or buy NFTs based on custom rules and filters.

It is especially useful for collectors who want to stay competitive on fast-moving drops or floor prices without having to watch the market 24/7.

### Key Features

- Real-time monitoring of listings
- Custom buying rules and price thresholds
- Auto-buying / sniping functionality
- Useful for both casual collectors and serious traders

You can access The Gutter from the Neftyblocks interface (usually under the marketplace or tools section).

**Note:** Use with caution and set reasonable parameters, as auto-buying can lead to unexpected purchases if filters are too broad.

For the most up-to-date instructions, check the official NeftyBlocks documentation or ask in their Discord.
