# Managing your Collection

The NB Creator tools allow collection owners to manage various aspects of their project on Neftyblocks, including:

- Viewing and updating collection settings
- Managing staking levels and perks
- Overseeing drops and blends
- Handling ads and promotional banners
- Accessing analytics and supporter data

Access these tools from the Creator Hub in your Neftyblocks account. Higher staking levels unlock additional management features and priority support.

This is especially useful for any collection involved with the Pixel Journey ecosystem.
