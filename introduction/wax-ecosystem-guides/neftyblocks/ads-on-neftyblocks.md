# Ads on Neftyblocks

For the most up-to-date information, always check the official NeftyBlocks documentation:

[https://docs.neftyblocks.com/banners.html](https://docs.neftyblocks.com/banners.html)

### Overview

Collections can advertise on NeftyBlocks using banners (left and right positions). Banners are booked by burning $NEFTY (not buying).

You can manage banners from the Creator Hub → **Collection Banners**.

Key points:
- Choose left or right position
- Provide a URL (can open in new tab)
- Upload desktop and mobile images (preview available)
- Select the time frame (from short durations up to 5 days)
- Add optional notes

**Important:** Banners are stored permanently on the blockchain via smart contract. Do not include any private or personal data.

### Refunds

You can only get a refund if you delete the banner at least **48 hours** before it starts.

### Pricing & Discounts

Pricing is based on duration (unit price for <12h, dozen price for 12-24h, bulk for 24h+).

**Staking Level Discounts** (on $NEFTY burned):
- Level 1: 5% discount
- Level 2: 15% discount
- Level 3: 35% discount

Higher staking also unlocks better visibility (Nefty Royals) and other perks.
