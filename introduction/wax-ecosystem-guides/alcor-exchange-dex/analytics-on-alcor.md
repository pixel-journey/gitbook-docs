# 📊 Analytics on Alcor — Track Your DeFi Performance Like a Pro

Knowledge is power in Web3! Alcor provides built-in analytics to help you monitor your trades, liquidity positions, farms, and overall portfolio health.

## What Analytics Does Alcor Offer?
- **Portfolio Overview**: See all your tokens, LP positions, staked assets, and estimated value in one dashboard.
- **Trade History**: Full history of swaps, spot orders, and transfers.
- **Farm Performance**: Track rewards earned, APR history, and impermanent loss estimates.
- **Pool Analytics**: Depth charts, volume, liquidity, and fee data for every market.
- **ROI Calculators**: Tools to project earnings from providing liquidity or staking.

## How to Access & Use
1. Connect your wallet on [alcor.exchange](https://alcor.exchange/)
2. Go to **Portfolio**, **Analytics**, or **Dashboard** tab
3. Explore charts, tables, and export options
4. Use filters for specific time periods or assets (great for tax tracking!)
