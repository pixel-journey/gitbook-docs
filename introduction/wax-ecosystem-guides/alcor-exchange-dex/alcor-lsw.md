# 💧 Alcor LSW (Liquid Staking Wax)

Pixel Pioneers, meet one of the smartest ways to stake on WAX: **Alcor Liquid Staking Wax (LSW)**!

## What is LSW?
Traditional staking locks your WAX and you can't use it elsewhere. LSW lets you **stake your WAX** to earn staking rewards **while keeping a liquid staked token** you can trade, provide liquidity with, or use in DeFi — all without unstaking delays.

You receive a liquid staking derivative (like stWAX or similar) that represents your staked position + accrued rewards.

## How to Use Alcor LSW
1. Head to [alcor.exchange](https://alcor.exchange/)
2. Connect wallet
3. Find the **LSW** or Liquid Staking section
4. Stake your WAX → receive liquid staked tokens
5. Use those tokens in pools, farms, or trade them
6. Unstake anytime (with possible cooldown) to get back original WAX + rewards

## Benefits for Pixel Journey Holders
- Earn staking yields on your WAX bag
- Keep capital working in other ecosystem activities (trade for more Pixals, LP for $PXJ farms, etc.)
- Great for long-term holders who still want flexibility

## Risks & Best Practices
- Smart contract risk (use official Alcor)
- Price of liquid token can fluctuate vs native WAX
- Always check current APY and pool health
