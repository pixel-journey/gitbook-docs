# 🌽 Alcor Farms — Earn While You Provide Liquidity

Ahoy Pixel Liquidity Providers! One of the best ways to earn passive rewards on WAX is by providing liquidity (LP) on **Alcor Exchange Farms**.

## What are Alcor Farms?
Alcor Farms let you stake your LP tokens (from Alcor spot or swap pools) to earn extra yield in various tokens — often project tokens, $WAX, or even ecosystem gems like $PXJ in partnered farms!

This is **DeFi 101 on WAX**: You add liquidity to a pair (e.g. PXJ/WAX), receive LP tokens, then stake those LP tokens in the farm to harvest rewards.

## How to Get Started on Alcor Farms
1. Go to [alcor.exchange](https://alcor.exchange/)
2. Connect your **Cloud Wallet** or **Anchor Wallet**
3. Navigate to **Farms** or **Pools** section
4. Find active farms (filter by APR, your tokens, or "Pixel Journey" related if available)
5. Add liquidity to the underlying pool if you don't have LP tokens yet
6. Stake your LP tokens in the farm
7. Harvest rewards periodically (claim & compound!)

## Key Features & Tips
- **High APR Farms**: Watch for new farms with boosted rewards (often from project incentives).
- **Impermanent Loss (IL)**: Understand the risk when providing liquidity to volatile pairs.
- **Auto-compound**: Some farms or tools help automate harvesting.
- **WAX Resources**: Make sure you have enough CPU/NET for transactions.

## PXJ-Specific Opportunities
- Look for $PXJ paired farms or ecosystem incentive programs.
- Stake LP from our community pools to support the Pixel Journey economy while earning.
- Many Pixel holders use farms to grow their bags for more Pixal mints, recycling, or staking.

## Best Practices from 1050+ Days of Pixeling
- Start small and learn the UI.
- Track your positions in Alcor portfolio tools or external trackers.
- Diversify across a few farms.
- Always DYOR — check farm end dates and token utility.
