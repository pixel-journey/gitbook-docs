# WAX Blockchain Resources and Gas Fees

WAX doesn't use traditional gas fees. Instead, it uses three resources: **CPU**, **NET**, and **RAM**.

**CPU & NET**
- Used to process and send transactions.
- These resources are consumed when you make transactions.
- They naturally regenerate over time and are fully replenished after ~24 hours.

**RAM**
- Stores your account's data on the blockchain.
- RAM does **not** regenerate automatically.
- You can buy more RAM when needed, or sell excess RAM.

Monitor your resources regularly to avoid transaction issues. For a deeper explanation, read this guide:
