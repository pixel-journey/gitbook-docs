# Unstaking for WAX Blockchain Resources

**To unstake your WAXP:**

1. Go to the WAX Block Explorer: [https://waxblock.io/wallet/resources/unstake](https://waxblock.io/wallet/resources/unstake)
2. Follow the on-screen instructions.

**Important things to know:**

- After unstaking, it takes approximately **72 hours** for your WAXP to become available again.
- If you unstake more while already waiting for a previous refund, the 72-hour timer restarts.
- The system will prioritize re-staking from any pending refund balance first.

**After the 72 hours have passed but you still haven't received your WAXP:**

Visit this page to manually claim your unstaked tokens: [https://waxblock.io/wallet/resources/refund](https://waxblock.io/wallet/resources/refund)

Follow the prompts to complete the refund process.
