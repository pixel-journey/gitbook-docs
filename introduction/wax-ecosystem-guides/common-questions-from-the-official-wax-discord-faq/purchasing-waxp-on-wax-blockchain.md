# Purchasing WAXP on WAX Blockchain

**To buy WAXP:**

1. In WAX Cloud Wallet, click the **"Buy WAXP"** buttons on either panel.
2. You’ll see a list of available services.
3. Choose one, or explore more options here: [https://go.wax.io/Markets](https://go.wax.io/Markets)

**Important note:** WAXP is the utility token that powers the WAX blockchain and NFT ecosystem. It is **not** an investment. Always do your own research (DYOR) and only use funds you can afford to use for transactions and resources.
