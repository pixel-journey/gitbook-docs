# Staking for WAX Blockchain Resources

**For Cloud Wallet users:**
1. Go to My Cloud Wallet and click the **"Resources"** button on the right panel.
2. Choose the resource you want to increase (CPU, NET, or RAM).
3. Enter how much WAXP you want to stake or spend.

**For all users:**
You can also manage resources directly on a block explorer:
- Stake CPU/NET: [https://waxblock.io/wallet/resources](https://waxblock.io/wallet/resources)
- Buy RAM: [https://waxblock.io/wallet/ram](https://waxblock.io/wallet/ram)

Always make sure you have enough WAXP available and regularly check your resource levels so transactions go smoothly.
