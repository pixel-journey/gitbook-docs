# Anchor Desktop App

**Anchor Desktop App** is the full-featured desktop version of Anchor Wallet.

It is widely used by WAX power users, developers, and collectors who want maximum control and security.

**Key features:**
- Advanced key and account management
- Seamless dApp integration
- Multi-account and multi-chain support
- Detailed transaction signing and history
- Resource management tools

Download the desktop app from the official Anchor website and import your existing WAX accounts (or create new ones).

Many people use Anchor Desktop alongside Cloud Wallet depending on the task. It's especially useful for complex transactions, managing multiple accounts, or when you want the highest level of control over your keys.
