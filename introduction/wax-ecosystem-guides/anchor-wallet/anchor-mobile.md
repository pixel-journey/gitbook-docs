# Anchor Mobile

**Anchor Mobile** brings the power and security of Anchor Wallet to your phone.

It allows you to easily manage your WAX accounts, sign transactions for dApps, and stay connected while on the go.

**Key features:**
- Secure key management
- Easy dApp signing
- Multi-account support
- Push notifications for transactions

Download Anchor Mobile from the official sources (App Store or Google Play) and pair it with your existing accounts or create new ones.

It's a great companion to the desktop version and Cloud Wallet.
