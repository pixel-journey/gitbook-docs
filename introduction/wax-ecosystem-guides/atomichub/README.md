# AtomicHub - Primary NFT Marketplace on WAX (2026 Update)

**Overview**: AtomicHub remains a cornerstone of the WAX ecosystem for creating, buying, selling, and trading NFTs. Despite occasional maintenance, it’s the highest volume marketplace with robust creator tools.

## Getting Started
1. Visit [wax.atomichub.io](https://wax.atomichub.io/).
2. Connect your Cloud Wallet or Anchor Wallet.
3. Explore collections, open packs, or search assets.

## Key Features (2026)
- Cross-chain NFT support (WAX + others).
- Advanced search and filters.
- Creator tools for minting collections and packs.
- Inventory management and transfers.
- Analytics and sales history.

## Step-by-Step Tutorials
- **Buying NFTs**: Browse, filter by price/rarity, purchase with WAXP.
- **Selling/Minting**: List assets from inventory or create new ones.
- **Pack Opening**: Participate in drops and unbox.
- **Profile & Collections**: Manage your creator profile.

## PXJ Integration
- Ideal for Pixal minting, trading, recycling, and event participation.
- Use during high activity for fast transactions (power up resources first).

## Safety & Best Practices
- Verify collection contracts before large buys.
- Use official links only.
- Monitor CPU/NET resources.
- Consider alternatives like F12Key or Neftyblocks during downtime.

## Common Issues
- Platform maintenance: Switch to Neftyblocks or F12Key.
- High fees during congestion: Power up wallet resources.
