# 🏆 What is the goal?

Pixel Journey exists to create something meaningful, fun, and lasting in Web3. Here’s what we’re aiming for:

- **A welcoming community** where everyone can have fun, connect, and feel at home.
- **Real friendships and collaboration** — meeting new people and building together along this shared journey.
- **Safe, guided education** — helping newcomers (and experienced explorers) learn about NFTs, crypto, blockchains, and tools in a clear, low-pressure way (always with proper DYOR).
- **Showcasing the best of each ecosystem** — highlighting the amazing (and often surprisingly accessible) features across the chains we journey through.
- **Connecting WAX with the wider Web3 world** — building bridges between communities, projects, and opportunities.
- **Elevating pixel art and collectibles** — making JPEGs and pixels more valuable, useful, and enjoyable for everyone.
- **New opportunities for creators** — giving artists and projects fresh ways to reach engaged audiences through our collections, pixelated themes, and Pixel economy.
- **Great collecting experiences** — helping collectors discover neat, epic, and meaningful things to own and enjoy.
- **Sustainable, long-term design** — building mechanics and systems that are balanced, stable, and built to stand the test of time — and hopefully inspiring other projects along the way.

**TL;DR:** To Pixel and Journey all the things — together! 🥳👾

We’re not just building a project. We’re building a shared adventure. Your participation, feedback, and energy make it all possible.
