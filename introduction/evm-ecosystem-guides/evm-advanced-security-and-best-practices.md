# EVM Advanced Security & Best Practices — Protect Your Assets Across Chains

**Security on EVM chains** requires vigilance due to the prevalence of smart contract risks, phishing, and complex interactions. This guide covers advanced best practices, common pitfalls, and tools to stay safe while exploring Ethereum, Base, Polygon, and other EVM ecosystems. Tailored for Pixel Journey users bridging from WAX or experimenting with new opportunities.

## Core Security Principles

### Wallet & Key Management
- Use hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor) for significant holdings.
- Enable multi-sig or smart contract wallets (e.g., Gnosis Safe) for team or high-value assets.
- Never share seed phrases or private keys. Use password managers for dApp logins.
- Regularly review and revoke connected dApp approvals (use tools like Revoke.cash or DeBank).

### Transaction Safety
- Always simulate transactions before signing (Rabby or MetaMask with simulation enabled).
- Verify contract addresses on explorers (Etherscan, Basescan) before interacting.
- Check for known vulnerabilities using tools like DeFiSafety or RugDoc.
- Start with small test transactions on new protocols or bridges.

### Phishing & Scam Awareness
- Bookmark official sites; beware of fake support DMs or links.
- Use browser extensions like Wallet Guard or Scam Sniffer.
- Verify URLs and contract sources on Etherscan.
- Be cautious with airdrops or "free" claims — many are phishing vectors.

## Advanced Best Practices

### Bridge & Cross-Chain Security
- Use official or audited bridges (e.g., official Polygon bridge, Hop, Across).
- Test with small amounts first; understand the bridge's security model (e.g., optimistic vs. zk).
- Monitor bridged assets on both chains via explorers.
- Be aware of bridge-specific risks like delays or exploits.

### DeFi & NFT Interactions
- DYOR on protocols: check audits, TVL, team transparency, and community feedback.
- Use portfolio trackers (DeBank, Zapper) to monitor positions and risks.
- For NFTs: Verify collections on official marketplaces; watch for copycats.
- Enable transaction previews and gas optimization to avoid mistakes.

### Multi-Chain Management
- Use chain-specific explorers for verification.
- Keep separate wallets or accounts for different activities (e.g., trading vs. long-term holding).
- Regularly audit connected dApps and revoke unnecessary permissions.
- Combine with WAX tools (e.g., Anchor with hardware) for cross-chain security.
