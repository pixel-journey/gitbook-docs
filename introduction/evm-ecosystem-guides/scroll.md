# Scroll — zkEVM L2 Prioritizing Security, Compatibility & Developer Experience

**Scroll** is a zkEVM Layer 2 focused on delivering strong security guarantees, excellent Ethereum compatibility (bytecode-level), and an outstanding developer experience. It aims to make zero-knowledge technology accessible while maintaining high performance and low costs, with an active community and grant programs supporting ecosystem growth.

Scroll stands out for its commitment to security and seamless integration with existing Ethereum tooling.

## Why Scroll Matters for Pixel Journey Users

- **Strong Security & zkEVM**: Validity proofs provide robust security for on-chain activities and asset protection.
- **Developer-Friendly**: Bytecode compatibility means most Ethereum dApps and tools work with minimal changes.
- **Growing Ecosystem**: Active in DeFi, NFTs, and infrastructure with community support and grants.
- **Cost-Effective Scaling**: Low fees combined with zk-security make it practical for frequent interactions.

**Pixel Journey Perspective**: Scroll offers a secure, compatible environment for users who appreciate zk-technology and want smooth Ethereum tool integration. It complements WAX by providing a reliable L2 for bridging, DeFi exploration, or NFT activities with strong security foundations.

1. **Wallet Setup**:

2. **Get ETH for Gas & Bridging**:

   - Supported exchanges for direct access

3. **Key Explorers & Tools**:

4. **First Actions**:
