# NFT Marketplaces on EVM Chains — Buy, Sell & Discover Digital Collectibles

**NFT Marketplaces** on EVM chains offer diverse platforms for trading digital art, collectibles, gaming items, and more. While OpenSea remains the largest, specialized platforms like Blur have gained traction for traders.

## Top Recommended Marketplaces

### OpenSea
- The largest and most established NFT marketplace across EVM chains.
- Supports Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, and many others.
- User-friendly for beginners with strong search, collections, and profile features.
- Includes auctions, offers, and bundle options.

### Blur
- Popular among active traders for its advanced tools, low fees, and fast execution.
- Strong on Ethereum and expanding to other chains.
- Excellent for sniping and high-volume trading.
- Often offers incentives or rewards for users.

### Other Notable Platforms
- Magic Eden (supports select EVM chains alongside Solana).
- Chain-specific or emerging platforms on Base, Polygon, or zkSync.

## Getting Started
1. Connect your wallet (ensure you're on the correct network).
2. Browse collections or search for specific NFTs.
3. Review listings, rarity, and provenance where available.
4. Make offers, buy now, or participate in auctions.
5. Manage your inventory and listings directly in the marketplace or via wallet tools.

## Safety & DYOR
- Verify collection contracts and creator authenticity (check official links, Discord, Twitter).
- Be cautious of fake collections or phishing sites — always use bookmarked official URLs.
- Review royalty settings and creator fees.
- Start with smaller purchases when exploring new collections or chains.
- Use portfolio trackers to monitor your holdings across marketplaces.
