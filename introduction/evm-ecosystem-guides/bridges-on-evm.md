# Bridges on EVM Chains — Secure Cross-Chain Transfers for Pixel Journey Users

Bridges allow you to move assets (tokens, NFTs) between different blockchains. On EVM, they are essential for accessing liquidity, cheaper fees on L2s, or specific ecosystems. However, bridges are one of the highest-risk areas in Web3 — many exploits have targeted bridge contracts.

## Why Bridges Matter for Pixel Journey

- Move Pixals or $PXJ-related assets to EVM L2s for cheaper trading, new NFT communities, or DeFi yields.
- Access deep liquidity on Ethereum or specialized apps on Base, Arbitrum, etc.
- Experiment with multi-chain strategies while keeping your core collection on WAX.

## Top Recommended Bridges

### Official Chain Bridges (Safest Starting Point)
- **Base Bridge** (bridge.base.org) — Simple, official for Base.
- **Arbitrum Bridge** — Official for Arbitrum.
- **Optimism Bridge** — Official for Optimism.
- **Polygon Bridge** — For Polygon PoS.

These are generally more audited and lower risk than third-party bridges.

### Multi-Chain Bridges
- **Across** — Fast, optimistic bridge with good UX and security track record.
- **Hop Protocol** — Excellent for stablecoins and ETH across many L2s.
- **LayerZero** powered bridges (e.g., Stargate) — For broader cross-chain, but understand the security model.

### NFT Bridges
- Some marketplaces or dedicated NFT bridges (e.g., via Wormhole or official chain tools) for moving collectibles. Always verify support for your specific NFT standard.

## Getting Started with Bridging
1. Choose the source and destination chain.
2. Use the **official bridge** for that chain whenever possible.
3. Connect your wallet on both sides if needed.
4. Approve and send — note any lock-up or challenge periods (especially on optimistic rollups like Arbitrum/Optimism, which can take ~7 days for withdrawals in some cases).
5. Verify the transaction on explorers for both chains.

## Critical Safety Warnings
- **Bridges are high-risk**: Many major hacks have targeted bridges. Only bridge what you can afford to lose and start with tiny test amounts.
- Use **official or well-audited bridges** only. Avoid random links or new/unverified bridges.
- Double-check contract addresses and URLs.
- Understand the bridge type: Optimistic (challenge period), zk (faster but complex), or liquidity-based.
- For NFTs: Not all bridges support every standard; test with low-value items first.
- Never approve unlimited spending unless necessary, and revoke after use.

## Pixel Journey Best Practices
- Bridge small amounts first to test the flow and timing.
- For frequent moves, prefer L2s like Base or Arbitrum for low fees.
- Keep track of bridged assets in portfolio tools (DeBank, Zapper).
- Combine with the Wallets on EVM and DEXes guides for full workflow.
- Remember: WAX remains your primary home — use EVM for exploration and opportunities.

**Pro Tip**: The official chain bridges are usually the safest entry point. For multi-hop or advanced needs, Across or Hop are popular among experienced users. Always research the specific bridge's security model and recent audits.
