# Base — Coinbase's User-Friendly L2 for Everyday Web3

**Base** is a Layer 2 blockchain built by Coinbase on the Optimism OP Stack. It inherits Ethereum's security while offering dramatically lower fees, faster transactions, and a seamless onboarding experience — especially for users coming from traditional finance or new to crypto.

Base has quickly become one of the most popular and accessible EVM chains for NFTs, DeFi, social apps, and gaming. Its tight integration with Coinbase makes on-ramping (buying crypto with fiat) incredibly easy.

## Why Base Stands Out for Pixel Journey Users

- **Beginner-Friendly Onboarding**: Coinbase account holders can bridge directly with low friction.
- **Low Fees + High Speed**: Perfect for frequent interactions, minting, swapping, or experimenting without worrying about gas costs.
- **Growing NFT & Creator Scene**: Strong ecosystem for digital collectibles and pixel-style art experiments.
- **DeFi & Yield Opportunities**: Solid liquidity on major protocols with reasonable costs.
- **Bridge-Friendly**: Easy to move assets from Ethereum mainnet or other chains.

**Pixel Journey Perspective**: Base is an excellent "second home" chain for many WAX users who want cheap, fast transactions while staying in a trusted, well-supported environment. Great for testing new tools or bridging smaller Pixal-related assets.

## Getting Started on Base (Step-by-Step)

1. **Wallet**:
   - MetaMask or Rabby (add Base network easily via chainlist.org or wallet prompts)
   - Coinbase Wallet for seamless Coinbase integration

2. **Get Funds on Base**:
   - Bridge from Ethereum using the official Base Bridge (bridge.base.org)
   - On-ramp directly via Coinbase (easiest for fiat)
   - Use third-party bridges like Across or official ones for other chains

3. **Key Tools & Explorers**:
   - [basescan.org](https://basescan.org) — Official block explorer
   - [debank.com](https://debank.com) or Zapper for portfolio tracking
   - Gas is usually pennies — monitor via basescan gas tracker

4. **First Actions**:
   - Swap on Uniswap or Aerodrome (popular DEXes)
   - Mint or trade NFTs on platforms like Zora, OpenSea (Base), or friend.tech-style apps
   - Explore social/farcaster integrations

## Safety, DYOR & Best Practices on Base

- **Bridge Carefully**: Official Base Bridge is recommended. Understand any withdrawal delays (though Base is generally fast).
- **Token Approvals**: Regularly revoke unnecessary approvals.
- **Scam Tokens & Contracts**: Base has seen rapid growth — many new tokens. DYOR, check contract age/holders on Basescan, and avoid hype without research.
- **Fiat On-Ramp Safety**: Use official Coinbase flows when possible.
- **General EVM Hygiene**: Same rules as Ethereum — simulate txs, start small, bookmark official sites.
