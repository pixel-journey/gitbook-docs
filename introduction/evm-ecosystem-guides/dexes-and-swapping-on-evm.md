# DEXes & Swapping on EVM Chains — Trade Efficiently Across Ecosystems

**Decentralized Exchanges (DEXes)** allow you to swap tokens directly from your wallet without intermediaries. On EVM chains, Uniswap is the dominant protocol, with excellent liquidity and support across most networks.

## Top Recommended DEXes

### Uniswap
- The gold standard for EVM swapping and liquidity provision.
- Available on Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, and many other chains.
- Supports limit orders, concentrated liquidity, and advanced features.
- User-friendly interface with strong security track record.

### Chain-Specific DEXes
- **PancakeSwap** on BNB Chain — High volume, low fees, great for BNB ecosystem tokens.
- **QuickSwap** on Polygon — Popular for fast, cheap swaps on Polygon.
- **Velodrome** on Optimism — Strong for Optimism and Superchain liquidity.
- Others like Trader Joe (Avalanche), SyncSwap (zkSync) offer specialized liquidity.

## Getting Started with Swapping
1. Connect your wallet (Rabby or MetaMask recommended).
2. Select the source and destination tokens.
3. Review the estimated output, price impact, and fees.
4. Approve the token (if first time) and confirm the swap.
5. Monitor the transaction on the chain explorer.

## Providing Liquidity
- Add liquidity to pools to earn trading fees and rewards.
- Be aware of **impermanent loss** — the risk that your deposited assets change in value relative to holding them.
- Start small and choose stable or correlated pairs when learning.

## Safety & DYOR
- Always verify the correct token contract addresses (use explorers or official sources).
- Check liquidity depth and price impact for large swaps.
- Use reputable DEX frontends — bookmark official sites.
- Enable transaction simulation in your wallet to preview outcomes.
- DYOR on new tokens and avoid hype without research.
