# Ethereum — The Foundational L1 of the EVM World

**Ethereum** is the original smart contract blockchain that sparked the entire EVM (Ethereum Virtual Machine) ecosystem. It serves as the primary settlement layer for high-value assets, DeFi protocols, and many NFT standards that have become industry benchmarks (ERC-721 for unique NFTs, ERC-1155 for multi-token, ERC-20 for fungible tokens).

Even as Layer 2 solutions and alternative L1s rise in popularity for speed and cost, Ethereum remains the most secure, decentralized, and battle-tested foundation. Most major projects and bridges ultimately settle or reference Ethereum mainnet.

## Why Ethereum Matters for Pixel Journey Users

- **High-Value Settlement**: Many premium NFT collections and blue-chip projects live here or bridge from here.
- **Standards Leadership**: Understanding Ethereum standards helps you navigate NFTs and tokens across almost every EVM-compatible chain (including future Px tools).
- **Bridge Gateway**: Most cross-chain bridges from WAX or other chains route through or reference Ethereum ecosystem liquidity.
- **DeFi & Innovation Hub**: Home to the deepest liquidity and most mature protocols — great for learning advanced concepts.

## Getting Started on Ethereum (Practical Steps)

1. **Wallet Setup** (Recommended for beginners):
   - **Rabby Wallet** or **MetaMask** (browser extension + mobile)
   - Hardware wallet (Ledger/Trezor) for larger holdings
   - Enable transaction simulation and revoke approvals regularly

2. **Get ETH for Gas**:
   - Buy on centralized exchanges and withdraw to your wallet
   - Use on-ramps in wallets (MoonPay, etc.)
   - Bridge small amounts from other chains if you already hold assets

3. **Explorers & Tools**:
   - [Etherscan.io](https://etherscan.io) — Primary block explorer
   - [Ethplorer](https://ethplorer.io) or [DeBank](https://debank.com) for portfolio views
   - Gas trackers: etherscan.io/gastracker or ultrasound.money

4. **First Transactions**:
   - Start with small test transfers
   - Interact with reputable dApps (Uniswap for swaps, OpenSea for NFTs)
   - Always simulate transactions first

## Layer 2s & Scaling — The Practical Reality (2026)

Ethereum mainnet gas fees can be high during congestion. **Layer 2 rollups** (Optimism, Arbitrum, Base, zkSync, etc.) inherit Ethereum's security while offering dramatically lower fees and higher speed.

**Pixel Journey Tip**: For most day-to-day activities (swapping, minting, bridging smaller assets), use an L2 like **Base** first. Bridge back to mainnet only when necessary for highest security or specific liquidity.

## Safety, DYOR & Common Pitfalls

- **Gas Wars & Fees**: Monitor gas prices. Never set max fee too low or transactions may fail or get stuck.
- **Bridge Risks**: Every bridge has smart contract risk. Use official bridges, start tiny, and understand the withdrawal period (some L2s have 7-day challenges).
- **Scams & Approvals**: Revoke unnecessary token approvals regularly (use revoke.cash or similar). Never approve suspicious contracts.
- **Phishing**: Bookmark official sites. Double-check URLs. Use hardware wallets for significant value.
- **DYOR Always**: The EVM space moves extremely fast. Verify contracts on Etherscan, check team/audit status, and start small.
