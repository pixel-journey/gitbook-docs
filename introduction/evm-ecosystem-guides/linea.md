# Linea — Consensys zkEVM with Strong Security & Ethereum Alignment

**Linea** is a zkEVM Layer 2 developed by Consensys, designed for seamless Ethereum compatibility at the bytecode level while leveraging zero-knowledge proofs for scalability and enhanced security. It emphasizes developer experience, security, and gradual decentralization, making it a reliable choice for projects prioritizing long-term robustness.

Linea benefits from strong backing and integration with tools like MetaMask and other Consensys products.

2. **Get ETH for Gas & Bridging**:

## Safety, DYOR & Best Practices

- **Bridge Security**: Use official bridges and understand any withdrawal mechanics.
- **Contract Verification**: Always verify contracts on Lineascan before interacting.
- **zkEVM Nuances**: While highly compatible, test interactions with new dApps as with any L2.
- **General EVM Practices**: Regular approval management, small test transactions, and strong phishing awareness.
