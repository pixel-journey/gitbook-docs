# AI Geometrics/WAXART Collective

### The WAXART Collective

The WAXART Collective consists of 21 artists from across the Web3+WAX Space, who support each other in releasing and promoting their art released onto the blockchains.



### WAXART Auctions and TG

On the WAXART telegram community channel there are weekly/periodically announced auctions where anyone are welcomed to join to participate!

A number of auctions are listed, and started one at a time, where anyone in the community can pitch in with bids in the chat on each asset.

The auction token used is the $WAXART token on the Wax Network, which is paid to the creators of the items sold.
