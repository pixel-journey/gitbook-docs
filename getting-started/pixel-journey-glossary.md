# 🔢 Pixel Journey Glossary — Your Quick Pixel Dictionary

**New to the pixelverse?** This glossary will help you understand the key terms we use every day. We’ve kept the explanations friendly, short, and educational — with links to deeper pages so you can level up whenever you’re ready.

## Core Concepts

### Pixal
A unique pixel-art **NFT** that serves as your on-chain identity, PFP, and membership card in the Pixel Journey universe. Your Pixal unlocks access to Ingredients, crafting, airdrops, and community perks. Minted starting Spring 2024 on WAX.

→ Learn more: [The Pixal PFPs](season-1/the-pixal-pfps/)

### PXJ
The native **utility token** of Pixel Journey. Earn it through gameplay, crafting, recycling, quests, and staking. Use it to participate in the economy and governance.

→ Learn more: [Kickstarting the PXJ-Economy](season-1/kickstarting-the-pxj-economy/)

### Ingredients
The raw building blocks of the Pixel Journey economy. Collect them, recycle them, and use them in **Crafting** to create new items and collectibles.

→ Learn more: [Recycling & Crafting](season-1/recycling-and-crafting/)

### Recycling
A core game mechanic where you can recycle items or Ingredients to receive resources back. Helps keep the economy balanced and sustainable while giving value to players.

→ Learn more: [Recycling & Crafting](season-1/recycling-and-crafting/)

### Crafting
The heart of creation in Pixel Journey. Combine Ingredients (and sometimes other items) to produce new collectibles, tools, or masterpieces. One of the most rewarding and educational parts of the experience.

→ Learn more: [Recycling & Crafting](season-1/recycling-and-crafting/)

### Rock Council
The governance body of Pixel Journey. Token holders (especially those who stake PXJ) can participate in proposals and decisions that shape the future of the project and its treasury.

→ Learn more: [The Rock Council](season-1/the-rock-council.md)

### KING
A special token in the Pixel Journey ecosystem (often referred to as $KING of Pixals). Has utility and governance connections within the broader project.

→ Learn more: [$KING of Pixals](season-1/usdking-of-pixals.md)

## Technical & Web3 Basics

### NFT (Non-Fungible Token)
A unique digital asset on the blockchain that you truly own. Your Pixal is an NFT. Unlike regular tokens, each one is distinct.

→ Learn more: [What Are NFTs?](education/what-are-nfts.md)

### Wallet
Your personal digital keychain and vault for everything on-chain. It holds your Pixals, PXJ, and other assets. You control it with your seed phrase.

→ Learn more: [Getting Set Up on WAX](education/getting-set-up-on-wax/)

### Seed Phrase
The master backup (usually 12 or 24 words) for your wallet. Never share it. This is the most important security rule in Web3.

→ See safety tips: [Common Pixel Pitfalls](common-pixel-pitfalls.md)

### Staking
Locking your tokens (like PXJ) to support the ecosystem while earning rewards and often gaining governance voting power.

→ Learn more: [Staking & LP-Rewards](season-1/kickstarting-the-pxj-economy/staking-and-lp-rewards/)

### Bridge
A tool that lets you move assets (tokens or NFTs) between different blockchains (e.g. from WAX to Polygon or Base). Essential for multi-chain expansion.

→ Learn more: [Bridges to Polygon, BASE, ETH and beyond](season-1/bridges-to-polygon-base-eth-and-beyond.md)

### DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization)
A community-governed organization that runs on smart contracts and token voting. The Rock Council is Pixel Journey’s version of this.

→ Learn more: [The Rock Council](season-1/the-rock-council.md)

### dApp (Decentralized Application)
An application that runs on the blockchain (like our staking portal, leaderboards, or future miniDApps).
