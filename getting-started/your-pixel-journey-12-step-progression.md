# Your Pixel Journey — 12 Step Progression Path

![Your Pixel Journey 12 Step Progression Path](.gitbook/assets/12-step-progression-path.png)

**From curious explorer to true Pixel Council Member — one quest at a time.**

This visual roadmap shows the complete journey every new explorer takes in the Pixel Journey ecosystem. Each step builds on the previous one, taking you from your very first wallet all the way to becoming a respected member of the Pixel Council.

> **Note for accessibility**: The full detailed explanation of each step is available in the text below the image. The visual is meant as a quick reference and progress tracker.

## The 12 Steps

1. **Create Your First Wallet** — Set up your Anchor, Cloud Wallet, or other compatible wallet.
2. **Join the Pixel Community** — Connect with other explorers on Discord and start your journey.
3. **Learn What Pixals Are** — Understand the soul of the ecosystem.
4. **Obtain Your First Pixal** — Mint or buy your first PFP NFT.
5. **Understand Ingredients** — Learn about the resources needed for crafting.
6. **Master Recycling** — Give old items new life in the circular economy.
7. **The Art of Crafting** — Combine ingredients to create new items and value.
8. **Earn Your First PXJ** — Start earning the native token of the ecosystem.
9. **Stake Your PXJ** — Put your tokens to work and earn rewards.
10. **Participate in Governance** — Have your say in the future of Pixel Journey.
11. **Go Cross-Chain** — Expand beyond WAX using PxPortals.
12. **Become a Council Member** — Reach the highest tier and join the elite.
