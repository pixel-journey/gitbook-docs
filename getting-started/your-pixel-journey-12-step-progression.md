## 🟦 STEP 1: Create Your First Wallet

**Pixel Journey Lesson:** Your wallet is the key to your entire on-chain identity — including your future Pixals, PXJ, and crafted items.

**Web3 Lesson:** Wallets are your personal bank, passport, and vault all in one. You control your keys = you control your assets.

**Action Items:**
- Create a WAX-compatible wallet (start with WAX Cloud Wallet or Anchor — beginner friendly)
- Safely back up your seed phrase (write it down, never share, never screenshot)
- Add a tiny bit of WAX for fees

**→ Go here:** [Getting Set Up on WAX](education/getting-set-up-on-wax/)

**Next Pixel Step:** Once your wallet is ready and secured, move to Step 2.
