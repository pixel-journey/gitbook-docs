# ❓ Common Pixel Pitfalls (And How to Dodge Them Like a Pro)

**New to the pixelverse?** Don’t worry — everyone makes mistakes when they first enter Web3.  
We’ve seen them all. Here’s your friendly guide to avoiding the most common 8-bit traps so you can focus on the fun stuff: collecting Pixals, crafting, earning PXJ, and leveling up your Web3 skills.

---

## 🚫 1. Never Share Your Seed Phrase (The #1 Rule)

Your **seed phrase** (the 12 or 24 words) is the master key to your wallet and everything in it.

**The Mistake:** Screenshotting it, typing it into websites, sending it to "support", or sharing it with anyone.

**The Fix:**
- Write it down on paper (or metal backup)
- Store it in a safe, private place
- Never enter it on any website
- Never share it with anyone — not even Pixel Journey team members

**Pro Tip:** If anyone ever asks for your seed phrase, it’s a scam. Full stop.

---

## 💸 2. Starting Too Big, Too Fast

**The Mistake:** Buying 5–10 Pixals or staking large amounts on day one without understanding the mechanics.

**The Fix:**
- Start small. Get one Pixal. Learn the basics.
- Use the **Getting Started Checklist** and **12-Step Path**
- Treat your first week as a learning adventure, not an investment sprint

Remember: Pixel Journey is designed to be played over months and years. There’s no rush.

---

## ⏱️ 3. Rushing Transactions Without Double-Checking

Blockchain transactions are **permanent**.

**The Mistake:** Sending to the wrong address, wrong chain, or wrong contract.

**The Fix:**
- Always double-check addresses (we provide verified ones in the docs and Discord)
- Start with tiny test transactions when trying something new
- Use the official links from this GitBook or our Discord

---

## 🔐 4. Ignoring Wallet Security Basics

**The Mistake:** Using the same password everywhere, not enabling 2FA where available, or leaving your wallet connected to dApps forever.

**The Fix:**
- Use a strong, unique password for your wallet
- Enable biometric or hardware wallet security when possible
- Disconnect from dApps after use
- Consider a hardware wallet (Ledger, etc.) once you have meaningful value

---

## 🐻 5. Falling for Scams & Fake Support

Web3 is full of scammers pretending to be helpful.

**The Mistake:** Clicking suspicious links, "verifying" your wallet on fake sites, or sending tokens to "recover" something.

**The Fix:**
- Only use links from official sources (this GitBook, our Discord, pixeljourney.xyz)
- Never click random links in DMs
- If something sounds too good to be true (free Pixals, double your PXJ, etc.) — it is
- Ask in Discord before acting on anything suspicious

---

## 🚀 6. Not Joining the Community Early

**The Mistake:** Trying to figure everything out alone.

**The Fix:**
- Join the [Pixel Journey Discord](https://discord.gg/WQ59KEBSMp) right away
- Introduce yourself
- Read the pinned messages and rules
- Ask questions — the community is incredibly welcoming and helpful

The best tips, trades, and support happen in Discord.

---

## 🔄 7. Chasing Every Hype Instead of Following the Roadmap

**The Mistake:** Jumping between projects because of FOMO.

**The Fix:**
- Pixel Journey moves at its own healthy, sustainable pace
- We prioritize quality and education over rushing
- Trust the process — our 1,050+ day journey proves we’re in it for the long haul

---

## 📊 8. Not Understanding What You’re Buying or Staking

**The Mistake:** Staking or buying without reading the docs or understanding utility.

**The Fix:**
- Read the relevant sections in this GitBook before making moves
- Use the **12-Step Progression Path** as your guide
- Ask in Discord if something isn’t clear

Knowledge = power (and safer pixels).
