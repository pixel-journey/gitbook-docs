# ♻️ Recycling & Crafting

A core recurring mechanic in Pixel Journey is **recycling** (blending) of NFTs and pixel collectibles. Most assets are designed to be reusable or upgradable into new things as we progress through each phase. This creates a sustainable, rewarding economy where your early participation continues to generate value.

The Pixal PFPs serve as the primary key to unlock the Pixel Journey economy in Season 1 through their near-exclusive access to PixelPacks. Our $PXJ token is a core resource required at every step of the recycling and crafting routes.

### Why Recycling & Crafting Matters for Your Pixel Journey

Recycling and crafting are not just mechanics — they are the heart of our sustainable economy and hands-on education. By participating, you learn real Web3 skills: understanding burn mechanics, resource management, probability, strategic decision-making, and building long-term value. These skills transfer directly to future phases, PxPackages-powered tools, and the broader Web3 multiverse. Every recycled Pixal and crafted ingredient strengthens the ecosystem while teaching you how tokenomics and NFT utility actually work.

### What You'll Learn Through Recycling & Crafting

- **Sustainable Economy Design**: How burns, faucets, and progressive costs create balance (tied to our yearly New Year’s Eve PXJ mint of ~10% remaining supply to dao.pxj / team.pxj / dev.pxj / defi.pxj / craft.pxj pools).
- **NFT Utility & Blending**: How traits, probabilities, and combinations create rarity and value.
- **Staking & Rewards**: How to put assets to work earning PXJ.
- **Strategic Participation**: Timing, risk management, and community coordination.
- **Foundation for PxPackages**: Early exposure to modular crafting logic that will power future miniDApps and the PxLearn Portal.

### What are Pixel Ingredient Packs?

Pixel Ingredient Packs are the next step toward Pixel Collectibles. They bind the pixel economy together as it rolls out progressively.

Each pack contains 10 Primary colored Pixel Ingredients (randomly red, blue, or yellow). These can later be combined/blended with $PXJ into secondary colors (green, purple, orange). Specific combinations of colored ingredients will be required for future crafts, upgrades, and special blends.

Initially, Pixel Ingredient Packs are obtained exclusively via Pixal PFP recycling. After every ~2,000 recycled Pixals, we will add more packs to TacoSwap NFT Rewards and run special giveaway events. Eventually, up to 20,000 packs will be available through $PXJ in future phases.

### Pixel Journey Tips for Smart Recycling & Crafting

- **Start Strategic**: Recycle common-trait Pixals first to build your ingredient base while preserving rare ones.
- **Stake Everything**: PixelPacks and ingredients earn PXJ — put them to work immediately.
- **Learn Probabilities**: Understand color chances and plan future blends.
- **Community First**: Check the Pixal Cemetery and Discord for collective insights before big decisions.
- **Think Long-Term**: Every craft builds skills and assets that will matter in PxPackages-powered experiences and future seasons.
- **Track Your Journey**: Note what you learn — it prepares you to contribute to PxLearn content later.

This system is the foundation of our sustainable, educational economy. By participating thoughtfully, you're not just collecting — you're mastering Web3 mechanics that will serve you across the entire Pixel Journey and beyond.
