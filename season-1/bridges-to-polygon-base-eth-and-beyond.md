# 🌉 Bridges to Polygon, BASE, ETH and Beyond

Certain series and collectibles from the Pixel Journey (including Pixals) are bridgeable to other NFT-enabled blockchains via the MyCloudWallet Bridge.

This means your assets can move beyond WAX while retaining their utility and history. Bridging opens access to different markets, ecosystems, and features on each chain.

We have already bridged to:

- **Polygon** (view on OpenSea)
- **Ethereum** (view on OpenSea)
- **Base** (view on OpenSea)
- **BNB Chain** (view on Monbase)

All via the [MyCloudWallet Bridge](../introduction/wax-ecosystem-guides/cloud-wallet-bridge/).

Future phases will continue expanding bridging options as new opportunities arise. Mechanics from previous chains (like recycling on WAX) generally remain available even as we explore new ecosystems.
