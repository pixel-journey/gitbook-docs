## Where It All Begins

As we embark on **The Pixel Journey**, our mission is to explore the NFT and crypto space together, creating a shared experience that helps both newcomers and experienced users navigate the vast Web3 landscape — one pixel at a time.

### Our Approach to Seasons

We structure our journey into flexible, milestone-driven phases (originally called "seasons"), each focusing on different blockchain ecosystems and major achievements. Every phase builds upon the previous one, expanding Pixel Journey’s features, economy, collectibles, and — most importantly — your skills and understanding.

**Season 1** has been our extended foundational phase on the **WAX blockchain** — a deep, thoughtful period of building the core of the project. This phase has naturally taken longer than a single year (now over **1050 days strong** since our 2023 WAX launch and Spring 2024 Pixals mint) as we prioritized quality, sustainability, and proper technical maturation, including the major development of our **PxPackages** framework in late 2025–mid 2026 (Alpha miniDApps experimentation followed by a robust shared functionality rebuild). This sets the stage for the standalone PxLearn Portal and future multi-chain adventures.

### What You'll Learn & Master in Season 1

Season 1 is designed as a complete onboarding and skill-building experience:

- **Blockchain Basics & WAX Mastery**: Understand wallets, resources (CPU/NET/RAM), transactions, and why WAX excels for NFTs and education.
- **NFT Mechanics**: Minting, trading, inspecting, and the power of AtomicAssets on WAX.
- **DeFi Fundamentals**: Liquidity pools, staking, swaps on Alcor, TacoSwap, and more.
- **Crafting & Economy Systems**: Recycling Pixals into ingredients, blending, and sustainable tokenomics with yearly New Year’s Eve PXJ mints (~10% remaining supply to dao.pxj / team.pxj / dev.pxj / defi.pxj / craft.pxj pools).
- **Community & Governance**: Discord roles, leaderboards, Rock Council participation, and collective decision-making.
- **Technical Foundations**: Early exposure to modular development via PxPackages.

Every step teaches real, transferable Web3 skills while being fun and rewarding.

### Season 1 – The WAX Blockchain Foundation

Our first phase focused on mastering the WAX ecosystem and laying the groundwork for the Pixel Journey world and economy:

- Learning about WAX & AtomicAsset NFTs and their unique advantages
- Exploring key ecosystem tools (WaxOnEdge, Alcor, NFTHive, NeftyBlocks, WaxDAO, etc.)
- Introducing the $PXJ token with utility, burn/faucet mechanics, and staking
- Building community through events, giveaways, and governance (WaxRocks / PixelDAO)
- Developing core mechanics: Pixal PFPs, Pixel Recycling, crafting, and blending
- Significant technical work on PxPackages (Alpha miniDApps → full rebuild with shared functionality)

### Bridging to New Frontiers

Later in Season 1 we began exploring bridges to other chains (starting with Polygon), setting the stage for future expansion and the multi-chain era.

### Pixal PFPs & Ingredient System

- **10,000 Pixal PFPs** – The first major source of Pixel Ingredients and your gateway into the economy.
- **Recycling Mechanics** – Up to 80% of Pixals can be burned to generate PixelPacks, which unpack into craftable, stakable single-pixel ingredients.

Each step is designed to empower collectors while keeping the economy sustainable and rewarding.

### How Season 1 Prepares You for the Full Journey & PxLearn Portal

Season 1 gives you hands-on experience with the fundamentals that will power everything that follows. The skills, assets, and community connections you build here directly translate to future phases, PxPackages-powered dApps, and the comprehensive educational experiences in our upcoming standalone PxLearn Portal. This is not just a season — it's the solid pixel foundation for a lifetime of Web3 exploration.

### Pixel Journey Tips for Maximizing Season 1

- Start small: Master wallet setup and one marketplace before diving deep.
- Participate actively: Stake, craft, and engage in Discord for roles and rewards.
- Document your journey: Note what you learn — it will help others and prepare you for contributing to PxLearn content.
- DYOR but lean on our guides: They’re written to be the clearest, most welcoming resources in the space.
