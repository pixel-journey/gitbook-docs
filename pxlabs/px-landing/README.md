# 🌐 PxLanding — The Central Pixel Journey Hub

> **⚠️ DEVELOPMENT STATUS (June 2026)**  
> PxLanding is in advanced / near-production state. The core hub experience, PWA features, and integration points are stable. Final polish on onboarding flows, cross-portal deep linking, and seasonal campaign modules is ongoing.  
> **Early access and feedback rounds are active on Discord** — we especially want input on first-time user experience and mobile flow.

**PxLanding** is the official front door and central command center for the entire Pixel Journey ecosystem. It serves as your personalized gateway to every Px dApp, your current progress, news, and quick actions across staking, battling, trading, and learning.

## ✨ Core Experience

- **Unified Dashboard**: See your key stats (balances, staking positions, Arena Power, recent activity) at a glance.
- **Smart Onboarding**: Guided paths that adapt based on whether you're new to Web3, new to WAX, or already deep in the ecosystem.
- **Quick Actions**: One-click access to the most common tasks (stake, swap, vote, claim, bridge).
- **News & Campaigns**: Live ecosystem announcements, seasonal events, and important updates.
- **PWA + Offline Support**: Fully installable with graceful offline behavior.
- **Deep Integration**: Seamless handoff into PxWallet, PxStaking, PxHot, PxMarket, and PxLearn.

## 🔗 How It Connects to Everything Else

PxLanding is the **orchestrator** of your Pixel Journey:

- From here you launch or deep-link into every other Px platform.
- It surfaces personalized recommendations based on your activity in PxStaking, PxHot, or PxLearn.
- Future PxPortals integration will let you manage cross-chain positions directly from the hub.
- Your on-chain achievements and certifications from PxLearn are displayed prominently.

**This is where most new users should start** — it gives context and easy access to everything else in the ecosystem.
