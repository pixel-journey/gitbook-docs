**PxStaking** is the powerful yield engine of the Pixel Journey ecosystem. It offers **8 distinct staking scenarios** ranging from conservative to aggressive, each with different risk/reward profiles, compounding styles, and multipliers tied to your Arena Power, knowledge level, and resource management.

## 📈 The 8 Yield Scenarios

1. **Conservative** — Low risk, stable manual claiming
2. **Balanced** — Moderate risk with optional compounding
3. **Recycling Loop** — Medium risk, full token swap recycling for higher yields
4. **Arena Power** — Medium risk, boosted by PxHot voting accuracy
5. **Aggressive** — High risk/reward with full automation
6. **Gaming-Focused** — Medium risk, boosted by gameplay activity
7. **Bear Defensive** — Very low risk, stable accumulation
8. **Bull Maximalist** — Extreme risk/reward for maximum conviction

Each scenario dynamically adjusts based on your on-chain activity, CPU power-ups, and commitment length.

## 🔗 Pixel Journey Integration

PxStaking is one of the **primary economic drivers**:

- Your performance in PxHot directly increases multipliers here.
- Knowledge from PxLearn improves your effective yield.
- Profits flow into PxWallet and can be redeployed into PxMarket or PxSwap.
- Resource management (CPU/NET) in PxWallet directly impacts your compounding efficiency.
- Future seasons will tie staking positions to governance and exclusive NFT rewards.

**After setting up your wallet and understanding the mechanics, this is where most users spend significant time optimizing yield.**
