# 🔄 PxSwap — Decentralized Exchange & Liquidity

> **⚠️ DEVELOPMENT STATUS (June 2026)**  
> PxSwap is in mid-to-late stage development. Core routing across Alcor, Defibox, and TacoSwap is functional with good rate optimization. Work continues on advanced limit orders, liquidity provision tools, and deeper portfolio integration.  
> **Feedback on swap UX, slippage handling, and mobile experience is welcome** on Discord.

**PxSwap** is the high-performance decentralized exchange interface for the Pixel Journey ecosystem. It finds the best rates across multiple WAX DEXes and provides a clean, fast interface for swapping tokens and managing liquidity.

## 💱 Core Experience

- **Smart Routing**: Automatically finds the best path across Alcor, Defibox, TacoSwap, and other pools for optimal rates and lowest slippage.
- **Fast & Clean UI**: Minimalist yet powerful swap interface with live price impact and route visualization.
- **Liquidity Provision**: Easy tools to add/remove liquidity and earn fees.
- **Portfolio Integration**: See your token balances and quickly swap between positions.
- **PWA + Offline Queueing**: Queue swaps even when offline; they execute on reconnect.
- **Advanced Features** (coming): Limit orders, recurring swaps, and yield-optimized routing.

## 🔗 Pixel Journey Integration

PxSwap is a **key utility layer**:

- Use it to efficiently move between tokens for staking in PxStaking scenarios.
- Recycle rewards or rebalance portfolios from PxWallet activity.
- Support liquidity pools that benefit the broader $PXJ economy.
- Future PxPortals will extend swap capabilities across chains.

**After you have your wallet set up, PxSwap becomes essential for active portfolio management and optimizing your staking flows.**
