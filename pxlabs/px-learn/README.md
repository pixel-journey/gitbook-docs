**PxLearn** is the official interactive academy for the Pixel Journey ecosystem. It teaches everything from WAX fundamentals to advanced DeFi mechanics, smart contract concepts, and Pixel Journey-specific strategies — with quizzes, certifications, voice narration, and adaptive learning paths.

## 🎓 Core Experience

- **Structured Curriculums**: Progressive paths covering blockchain basics, WAX resource management, DeFi & staking, NFT mechanics, and Pixel Journey tokenomics.
- **On-Chain Quizzes & Certifications**: Test your knowledge and earn verifiable achievements/NFT certificates.
- **AI Voice Synthesis**: Listen to lessons hands-free with high-quality narration.
- **Adaptive Learning Paths**: The system recommends what to learn next based on your activity across PxStaking, PxHot, PxMarket, etc.
- **Full Glossary**: Comprehensive, searchable reference for every term in the ecosystem.
- **Offline Support**: Previously accessed lessons and the glossary remain fully readable offline.

## 🔗 Pixel Journey Integration

PxLearn is the **educational backbone**:

- Completing modules increases your knowledge multipliers in staking scenarios.
- Certifications can unlock special roles, airdrops, or access in future seasons.
- Lessons are contextually linked from PxWallet, PxStaking, and PxHot when you encounter new mechanics.
- Your learning progress is visible in PxLanding.

**New users should spend time here early** — understanding the “why” behind every feature dramatically improves long-term success in the ecosystem.
