# 🔥 PxHot — Real-Time Pixal Arena Battles

> **⚠️ DEVELOPMENT STATUS (June 2026)**

**PxHot** is the adrenaline-fueled prediction and battle arena for the Pixel Journey ecosystem. Vote on live or scheduled Pixal matchups, earn rewards for accurate predictions, climb the global leaderboard, and directly boost your yields in PxStaking — all while the rest of your Pixel Journey continues in the background.

## 🎮 Core Experience

- **Live & Scheduled Arena Battles** — Real-time or timed Pixal vs Pixal matchups with transparent, verifiable resolution mechanics.
- **Prediction Voting** — Stake small amounts of $PXJ or resources to back your chosen champion in each matchup.
- **Reward Distribution** — Correct predictions earn proportional shares of the prize pool, plus bonus multipliers based on your current Arena Power and staking tier.
- **Arena Power System** — Your accuracy, consistency, and participation build Arena Power, which directly increases multipliers in PxStaking yield scenarios.
- **Leaderboards & History** — Track your prediction accuracy, win streaks, historical performance, and global ranking.
- **Offline Resilience** — Queue votes even when offline; they sync and resolve automatically on reconnect.

## 🛠️ Key Features (Current Build)

## 📈 Arena Power & Yield Synergy

One of the most powerful aspects of PxHot is how it connects to staking:

- Higher Arena Power = better multipliers in multiple PxStaking scenarios.
- Consistent performance compounds both your direct rewards *and* your long-term staking yields.
- This creates a healthy flywheel: better predictions → higher Arena Power → better staking returns → more resources to participate in PxHot.

This design rewards skill, consistency, and active engagement across the ecosystem rather than pure luck or heavy spending.
