# 🔥 PxHot — Real-Time Pixal Arena Battles

> **⚠️ DEVELOPMENT STATUS (June 2026)**  
> PxHot is in late-stage development. Core battle logic, voting, and offline queueing are functional. Smart contract finalization, reward distribution, and cross-portal state sync are still being hardened.

**PxHot** is the adrenaline-fueled prediction and battle arena for the Pixel Journey ecosystem. Vote on live Pixal matchups, earn rewards for accurate predictions, and climb the global leaderboard — all while the rest of your Pixel Journey continues in the background.

## 🎮 Core Experience

- **Live Arena Battles**: Real-time or scheduled Pixal vs Pixal matchups with transparent resolution mechanics.
- **Prediction Voting**: Stake small amounts of $PXJ or resources to back your chosen champion.
- **Reward Distribution**: Correct predictions earn proportional shares of the prize pool + bonus multipliers from your staking tier.
- **Offline Resilience**: Queue votes even when offline — they sync automatically on reconnect.
- **Leaderboards & History**: Track your accuracy, win streaks, and historical performance.

## 🛠️ Key Features (Current Build)

- PWA-first design (installable, works great on mobile)
- Service Worker with aggressive caching for battle data and assets
- Push notification support for upcoming matches and result alerts
- Background sync for queued votes
- Deep integration with your existing WAX Anchor / PxWallet session
- Demo mode available for testing without real stakes

## 🔗 Pixel Journey Connection

PxHot is not just a side game — it’s a **core engagement loop**:

- Accurate voting increases your Arena Power multiplier in **PxStaking** yield scenarios.
- Rewards feed directly into your $PXJ / $KING balance (visible in PxWallet).
- Battle participation contributes to seasonal achievements and future NFT airdrops.
- Future PxPortals will let you bring champions from other chains into the arena.

**Your primary focus remains on WAX collections and the PXJ economy** — PxHot is the exciting arena where you can test your intuition and earn extra yield on the side.
