# 🏆 Px Achievements — Journey & Progress Recognition

> **⚠️ DEVELOPMENT STATUS (June 2026)**  
> The Px Achievements system is in active design and early implementation. Core achievement categories, tracking mechanisms, and integration with the broader Pixel Journey ecosystem are being defined. Many details around specific achievements and how they tie into badges and rewards are still being refined.  
> **We welcome high-level feedback** on what kinds of achievements feel meaningful and motivating. Join our Discord to share your thoughts.

**Px Achievements** represent meaningful milestones and accomplishments users earn through their journey across the Pixel Journey ecosystem — including the main portals and mini-games.

Achievements are designed to recognize **proof of journey** and **proof of work** — celebrating active participation, learning, engagement, and progression rather than only static ownership.

## 🎯 Core Philosophy

Px Achievements focus on what users *do* and *experience* over time:

- Completing educational content in PxLearn
- Consistent and accurate performance in PxHot
- Active crafting, ingredient development, and staking in PxStaking
- Meaningful trading and market participation in PxMarket
- Exploration and usage of PxWallet and other tools
- Participation in seasonal events and community activities

The goal is to create a rich, rewarding sense of progression that feels earned through engagement with the ecosystem.

## 🔗 Relationship to Badges

Most Px Achievements are symbolized and visually represented through the **Px Badges** system. Earning an achievement typically grants a corresponding badge that can be displayed across portals.

However, not all badges are achievements. Some badges are tied to current ownership or holding status rather than completed actions (see the separate Badges article for more details).

## 🌟 Examples of Achievement Categories

- **Learning Achievements** — Completing courses, quizzes, or certification paths in PxLearn
- **Battle Achievements** — Reaching accuracy milestones, win streaks, or ranking thresholds in PxHot
- **Crafting & Development Achievements** — Reaching milestones in ingredient development and collectible crafting
- **Staking & Yield Achievements** — Sustained participation and performance across different staking strategies
- **Exploration Achievements** — Using multiple portals, discovering features, and engaging with different parts of the ecosystem
- **Community & Seasonal Achievements** — Participation in events, leaderboards, and special campaigns

## 🚀 Long-Term Vision

Over time, achievements are expected to:

- Unlock special themes, cosmetics, or features in the portals
- Contribute to multipliers or benefits in staking and other systems
- Provide visible proof of journey and dedication across the ecosystem
- Help create a layered progression system that rewards both new and long-term users

Achievements are one of the key ways Pixel Journey recognizes and rewards active, meaningful participation.
