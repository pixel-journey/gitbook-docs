# 🎖️ Px Badges — Ownership, Achievement & Status Symbols

**Px Badges** serve as visible symbols of both **achievements** and **ownership/holding status** across the Pixel Journey ecosystem. They act as recognizable markers that users can display and that the system can reference in various contexts.

## 🎯 Two Types of Badges

Px Badges come in two main categories:

### 1. Achievement-Based Badges

These represent completed milestones and accomplishments (see the separate [Achievements](achievements.md) page). Earning an achievement typically grants a corresponding badge. These are **proof of journey** and **proof of work** — they reflect what the user has done and achieved over time.

### 2. Ownership & Status-Based Badges

These badges reflect the user’s **current holdings and status** rather than completed actions. Examples include:

- Holding a certain number of Pixal PFPs
- Maintaining a high staking position or specific staking tier
- Achieving high rankings in PxHot
- Being a top buyer or seller in PxMarket
- High placement on crafting or staking leaderboards
- Other proof-of-ownership or current-status markers

These badges can change over time as holdings and rankings shift.

## 🔗 Relationship Between Achievements and Badges

While most achievements are represented through the badge system, **not all badges are achievements**. Ownership and status-based badges exist independently and serve a different purpose — they highlight current standing and holdings within the ecosystem.

This dual system allows Pixel Journey to recognize both:

- What users have *done* (achievements)
- What users currently *hold and represent* (ownership/status badges)

## 🌟 Examples of Badge Types

- **Journey & Achievement Badges** — Earned through completing learning paths, battle performance, crafting milestones, staking consistency, etc.
- **Ownership Badges** — Based on current holdings (e.g., number of Pixals, staking levels)
- **Performance & Ranking Badges** — Reflecting high placement in PxHot, market activity, or leaderboards
- **Seasonal & Event Badges** — Tied to specific seasons or limited-time campaigns
- **Special Recognition Badges** — For community contributions or unique accomplishments

## 🚀 Long-Term Vision

Over time, badges are expected to:

- Be visible across multiple portals and in user profiles
- Unlock cosmetic themes, features, or advantages in various experiences
- Serve as social proof and identity markers within the community
- Integrate with future governance or reputation systems

Badges help create a rich, layered identity system that reflects both a user’s journey and their current standing in the Pixel Journey ecosystem.
