# 🖥️ For Developers

**Welcome, builder.**

Whether you are taking your very first steps into Web3 or you are a seasoned dApp architect looking for production-grade patterns on WAX and beyond — you are in the right place.

Pixel Journey was born on the WAX Blockchain in 2023. We minted our Pixal PFPs in Spring 2024. For over **1,050 days** we have been building, learning, shipping, and refining in public. Everything we have learned — the hard-won patterns, the mistakes, the elegant solutions — is now being turned into the most welcoming, rigorous, and pixel-perfect developer education experience in Web3.

We believe the best way to explore **all of Web3** is not through scattered tutorials, but through a cohesive, story-driven journey that starts with real production systems (our own) and expands outward.

## 🧰 Your Primary Hands-On Path: PxDev Examples Vault

The single best place to start (or level up) is the brand-new **PxDev Examples Vault** inside PxLabs.

🔗 [Explore the PxDev Examples Vault →](https://docs.pixeljourney.xyz/pxlabs/px-dev-examples/)

This is our growing collection of **170+ educational code repositories**. In their completed form, every repo is a self-contained, production-quality quest that teaches you:

- Exact patterns we use in PxWallet, PxHot, PxMarket, PxStaking, and Px Packages
- Modern Next.js 15 + WharfKit + TypeScript stacks
- Verifiable on-chain mechanics, NFT/PFP systems, DeFi integrations, secure wallet architectures, cross-chain derivation, i18n, PWA resilience, automation, and professional engineering standards
- Why we made every decision — not just how to copy-paste code

All repositories follow our unified **Px Standards**, **Px Templates**, and **Px Design System**. Most are currently in private preview while we finish the final educational layers. Request access on GitHub or reach out on Discord — we happily grant early access to genuine builders.

**https://github.com/pixel-journey**

## 🚀 How to Get Involved

- **Clone & Build**: Start with any quest in the PxDev Examples Vault that matches your current level.
- **Contribute**: Once repos are public, we welcome high-quality PRs, documentation improvements, and new example ideas.
- **Feedback & Early Access**: Join our Discord (Community Contribution channels unlock at level 10+) and tell us what you are building or what patterns you wish existed.
- **Stay Updated**: Follow [@PxJourney](https://x.com/PxJourney) and watch the GitHub org for new public releases.

We are not just building tools for ourselves. We are building the map so that the next generation of pixel-perfect Web3 creators can move faster and with more confidence than we ever could.

If you have questions, ideas, or want to collaborate on something specific, the Discord is the fastest way to reach the team and the community.
