# ⛓️ For Blockchains

Without blockchains, there would be no Web3 metaverse. The Pixel Journey naturally includes deep education about blockchains, networks, tokenomics, bridges, and the wider crypto space as part of our core onboarding and exploration focus.

While our early phases have focused heavily on **WAX** (our strong foundation since 2023, with its powerful NFT standards, low fees, and excellent bridging capabilities), we are already expanding through bridges to Polygon, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, and beyond. Future phases will continue this multi-chain exploration as we leverage PxPackages to build seamless experiences across ecosystems.

We welcome opportunities to highlight and educate about other blockchains along the way. If you're building on a chain you'd like us to feature, collaborate with, or help explorers understand, feel free to reach out!

### Why Blockchains Matter in Your Pixel Journey

Understanding blockchains is foundational to everything we do. By exploring different chains, you learn about unique strengths (speed, cost, security, NFT tooling, DeFi depth), interoperability challenges, and how to navigate the growing Web3 multiverse. This hands-on education directly prepares you for the multi-chain future powered by PxPackages and the comprehensive learning experiences in our standalone PxLearn Portal.

### What You'll Learn About Blockchains

- **Core Concepts**: How different blockchains work, their trade-offs, and why they matter for NFTs and economies.
- **WAX Strengths**: High TPS, carbon-neutral design, AtomicAssets standard, and seamless bridging.
- **Multi-Chain Navigation**: How to move assets safely and understand different ecosystems.
- **Tokenomics & Incentives**: Real examples of sustainable models, including our yearly New Year’s Eve PXJ mint supporting the ecosystem.
- **Future-Proof Skills**: Knowledge that transfers across chains and into decentralized applications built with PxPackages.

### Pixel Journey Tips for Blockchain Explorers

- **Start with WAX**: Master the foundation where our Pixals, economy, and mechanics were born — it’s the perfect on-ramp.
- **Bridge Thoughtfully**: Use official tools and plan around snapshots/rewards when moving assets.
- **Compare Ecosystems**: Notice how different chains handle NFTs, DeFi, and community — this builds real comparative understanding.
- **Stay Curious**: Follow new chain developments and how they might integrate with future Pixel Journey phases.
- **Contribute Knowledge**: Share what you learn in Discord — helping others is part of the journey.
- **Connect to the Bigger Picture**: Every blockchain you explore strengthens your ability to participate in PxPackages-powered experiences and the PxLearn Portal.

**Get in touch:** Create a ticket in our Discord or email support@pixeljourney.xyz if you'd like to collaborate or be featured.

Blockchains are the foundation we all build upon. By exploring them together through the Pixel Journey, we're not just traveling — we're learning, connecting, and helping shape a more educated Web3 future. Over 1050 days strong and growing! ⛓️🛸🧩
