**Pixel Journey is here to help connect this shared metaverse of ours!** Are you building too? 

We actively look for meaningful collaborations with other projects and builders. If your project could fit into the Pixel Journey, here are some of the ways we can work together:

### Collaboration Opportunities
- **Incorporate your project or dApp into the journey** — Feature it in our educational content, PxPackages modules, or future experiences.
- **Run cross-community events** (promotional, educational, or fun) — Co-host AMAs, workshops, or pixel-themed events.
- **Feature your project through trait references in future PFPs or collectibles** — Pixelated tributes and integrations.
- **Create cross-collection blends and NFT crafts** — Blend mechanics and utility.
- **Distribute your tokens or NFTs through our reward pools** (and vice versa) — Mutual growth via staking, airdrops, and PXJ economy.
- **Offer project advising, guidance, and community-backed feedback** — Tap into our 1050+ day experience and engaged community.
- **Help promote your project to new or targeted audiences** — Reach collectors and explorers through our channels.
- **Connect you with new collectors and potential collaborators** — Leverage our network.

**Why Partner with Pixel Journey?**

In a fast-moving Web3 space, we offer a **stable, educational, and utility-focused** home. Our journey started on WAX in 2023, with Pixal PFPs in Spring 2024. We've grown through **1050+ days** of real building, flexible milestone-driven Seasons, and the development of **PxPackages** (Alpha miniDApps to robust shared rebuild in 2025-2026). 

Yearly New Year’s Eve PXJ mints (~10% remaining supply to **dao.pxj, team.pxj, dev.pxj, defi.pxj, craft.pxj** pools) support sustainable growth. We're preparing for the standalone **PxLearn Portal** — a comprehensive educational hub.

If you're building something aligned with **exploration, education, collectibles, or community** in Web3, we’d love to explore a partnership. Reach out via Discord!

### Pixel Journey Tips for Projects
- **Align with education & utility** — Our audience values learning and meaningful mechanics.
- **Think long-term** — Flexible Seasons mean we prioritize quality and sustainability.
- **Leverage PxPackages** — Future modular tools for deeper integrations.
- **Engage the community** — Our Rock Council and holders are active and thoughtful.
