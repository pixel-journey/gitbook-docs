# 🎨 For Artists

Artists are always welcome in the Pixel Journey! Whether you're creating fan art, emojis, memes, promotional graphics, or full collections, we'd love to see your work. This is a space where pixel art and digital creativity are celebrated, supported, and given real utility.

If you're new to WAX or NFTs, our community is happy to offer feedback and help you get started. We're building something together, and your creativity helps make it pixel-perfect.

### Why Artists Matter in Pixel Journey

Art is the soul of our collectibles and economy. By contributing, artists don't just create beautiful pieces — they help build educational experiences, sustainable mechanics, and a vibrant community. Your work can reach new audiences across multiple chains, gain utility through crafting and staking, and become part of the long 1050+ day journey. This collaboration model also supports the broader Web3 education mission as we prepare the standalone PxLearn Portal.

### Get Your Art Featured in Pixel Journey

As we build the Pixel economy, we regularly incorporate new art into collectibles, ingredients, and special drops. Collaborating artists can reach new audiences across the ecosystems we explore while earning from their creations.

**How collaboration typically works:**
- We mint your creations (often in multiple rarity/pixel-resolution tiers for accessibility and collectibility).
- A special ingredient or requirement is created for crafters/collectors, tying your art into the core mechanics.
- ~33% of revenue from related sales goes directly to the collaborating artist.
- The remaining ~67% supports $PXJ buybacks/burns, WaxRocks Council funds, and ongoing project development (including PxPackages and future educational tools).

### Pixel Journey Tips for Artists

- **Start with Community**: Share your work in Discord for feedback — the community is supportive and full of great ideas.
- **Think Utility**: Art that can be crafted, staked, or used in blends gains more engagement and long-term value.
- **Embrace Pixel Style**: Our focus on pixel art creates a cohesive, recognizable brand that stands out across chains.
- **Collaborate Openly**: Reach out with ideas for themed drops, seasonal events, or integrations with PxPackages-powered features.
- **Learn the Ecosystem**: Understanding WAX tools, bridging, and our mechanics helps you create art that fits naturally into the journey.
- **Build Long-Term**: Your contributions can grow with the project over many phases and into the PxLearn Portal era.

If you're interested in collaborating or having your art featured, open a ticket in our Discord or reach out directly. We're always looking for fresh talent to join the journey and help shape the most educational and pixel-perfect Web3 adventure possible.

Your art can become part of something bigger — a living, evolving collection that educates and connects people across Web3. Welcome to the Pixel Journey! 🛸🎨🧩
