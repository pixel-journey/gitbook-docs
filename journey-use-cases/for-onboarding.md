# 🧑‍🎓 For Onboarding

One of our core goals is to make the Pixel Journey a welcoming and effective onboarding tool for Web3, crypto, and NFTs.

### How We Support New Explorers

- Clear guidance and assistance getting started in Web3 (primarily through Discord and the guides in these docs)
- Help with CloudWallet redemption codes for verified new users (when available)
- Showcases of each blockchain's unique perks, tools, and opportunities
- Curated recommendations of projects and services worth exploring
- A safe, supportive community space where newcomers can ask questions and learn alongside others

Our aim is to lower the barrier to entry and help people not just join, but thrive and eventually help others on their own journeys. Education and accessibility are central to everything we build.
