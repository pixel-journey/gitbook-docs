# 🖲️ For Designers

The Pixel Journey is here to help Designers help design (more and better) as well! Design is at the core of everything we build — from pixel art and collectibles to user experiences, interfaces, and future PxPackages-powered dApps.

While some advanced design collaboration features are still in development (and currently discussed mainly in our Community Contribution sections for Discord Server lvl +10+ members), the vision is clear: designers play a vital role in shaping the pixel-perfect experiences that make the Pixel Journey special.

### Why Design Matters in Pixel Journey

Great design turns mechanics into memorable experiences. Whether it's crafting beautiful pixel art, intuitive interfaces for future tools, or educational visuals for the PxLearn Portal, designers help make Web3 more accessible, enjoyable, and pixelicious. Your work can directly influence how newcomers learn and how the community engages with our sustainable economy, governance, and multi-chain explorations.

### What Designers Can Expect

- Opportunities to contribute pixel art, UI/UX concepts, and visual systems that integrate with our Pixals, ingredients, and future collectibles
- Collaboration on educational materials and visuals for guides, the PxLearn Portal, and community resources
- Input on how PxPackages-powered experiences look and feel (as development progresses)
- Recognition and potential revenue share models similar to our artist partnerships
- A supportive community where feedback and ideas are welcomed at all levels

### Pixel Journey Tips for Designers

- **Start Contributing Early**: Even small designs, emojis, or concepts shared in Discord can become part of the journey.
- **Think Pixel-First**: Our aesthetic is rooted in beautiful, functional pixel art — lean into that style for maximum impact.
- **Connect Mechanics to Visuals**: Great design makes complex systems (like recycling, staking, or bridging) feel intuitive and fun.
- **Collaborate with the Community**: Join contribution channels to get feedback and see your ideas come to life.
- **Prepare for PxPackages & PxLearn**: Future tools and the educational portal will need thoughtful design — your skills will be highly valued.
- **Learn the Ecosystem**: Understanding WAX, bridging, and our core mechanics helps you create designs that fit naturally into the Pixel Journey.

These parts are evolving, and we’re excited to grow the design collaboration side of the Pixel Journey alongside our community. If you’re a designer interested in contributing or shaping the visual future of the project, reach out in Discord or via support@pixeljourney.xyz.

Your creativity can help make the Pixel Journey the most pixel-perfect and educational Web3 adventure possible. Let’s design it together! 🖲️🛸🧩
