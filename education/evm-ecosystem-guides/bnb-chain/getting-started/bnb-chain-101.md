# 📝 BNB Chain 101

## Introducing BNB Chain: Speed and Accessibility

BNB Chain (formerly Binance Smart Chain) is a high-performance EVM-compatible blockchain known for its speed, low fees, and broad accessibility. It powers a large ecosystem of DeFi, NFTs, and dApps, especially in emerging markets. For Pixel Journey explorers, BNB Chain offers an excellent, cost-effective environment for experimentation that complements your core WAX activities.

### Key Features

- **EVM Compatible**: Full compatibility with Ethereum tools and smart contracts.
- **Low Fees & High Speed**: Ideal for frequent transactions, trading, and gaming.
- **Native Token (BNB)**: Used for gas, staking, and as the primary currency.
- **Strong Adoption**: Large user base and vibrant ecosystem, especially in DeFi and gaming.
- **Bridge Connectivity**: Excellent bridges from Ethereum and other chains.

### Why This Model Matters for Pixel Journey

BNB Chain's low costs and high speed make it perfect for high-volume activities and testing new mechanics without high fees. While WAX excels at NFT/gaming volume on its own model, BNB Chain provides another accessible EVM playground. Skills in wallets, bridging, and dApp interaction transfer directly, and it prepares you for future PxPortals cross-chain features.

### Current BNB Chain Stats (approximate, late June 2026)

- High throughput with low fees
- Strong DeFi and NFT activity
- Active developer and user community

BNB Chain continues to evolve with upgrades focused on performance and ecosystem growth.

## Getting Started Basics on BNB Chain

1. **Wallet**: MetaMask, Rabby, or any EVM-compatible wallet. Hardware support recommended for larger holdings.
2. **Get BNB**: Buy on exchanges or bridge from Ethereum/other chains.
3. **Gas Management**: Fees are very low — great for frequent interactions.
4. **Explore**: Start with major dApps, marketplaces, and DEXes.

**Pixel Journey Tip**: Use BNB Chain for cost-effective experimentation and high-volume testing. Bridge small test amounts first. Your WAX skills translate well — accounts and resource concepts help with gas and permissions here. Keep primary focus on Pixel Journey and WAX.
