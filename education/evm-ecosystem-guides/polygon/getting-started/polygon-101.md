# 📝 Polygon 101

## Introducing Polygon: Scaling King for EVM

Polygon is a fast, low-cost EVM-compatible Layer 2/sidechain that gives excellent access to the wider Ethereum ecosystem without high fees. It offers strong NFT/gaming support, mature tooling, and broad adoption. For Pixel Journey explorers, Polygon provides a balanced, accessible EVM environment for experimentation and multi-chain growth.

### Key Features

- **EVM Compatible**: Full compatibility with Ethereum tools and smart contracts.
- **Low Fees & High Speed**: Ideal for frequent transactions, trading, crafting, and gaming.
- **Native Token (POL)**: Used for gas, staking, and governance.
- **Strong NFT Ecosystem**: Excellent support for NFTs with marketplaces like OpenSea and Magic Eden.
- **Bridge Connectivity**: Excellent bridges from Ethereum, WAX, and other chains.

### Why This Model Matters for Pixel Journey

Polygon’s low costs and high speed make it perfect for high-volume activities and testing new mechanics. While WAX excels at NFT/gaming volume on its native model, Polygon offers another accessible EVM playground with strong NFT focus. Skills in wallets, bridging, and dApp interaction transfer directly, and it prepares you for future PxPortals cross-chain features.

### Current Polygon Stats (approximate, late June 2026)

- High throughput with low fees
- Strong NFT and gaming activity
- Mature ecosystem with good developer tools

Polygon continues to evolve with upgrades focused on performance and ecosystem growth.

## Getting Started Basics on Polygon

1. **Wallet**: MetaMask, Rabby, or any EVM-compatible wallet. Hardware support recommended for larger holdings.
2. **Get POL**: Buy on exchanges or bridge from Ethereum/other chains.
3. **Gas Management**: Fees are very low — great for frequent interactions.
4. **Explore**: Start with major dApps, marketplaces, and DEXes.

**Pixel Journey Tip**: Use Polygon for cost-effective experimentation, especially for NFT-related activities. Bridge small test amounts first. Your WAX skills translate well — accounts and resource concepts help with gas and permissions here. Keep primary focus on Pixel Journey and WAX.
