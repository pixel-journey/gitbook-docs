# 🔄 DEXes and Swapping for EVM Chains

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Always verify contract addresses and use well-known, audited DEXes. Be cautious of fake tokens and phishing sites. Start with small amounts when testing new liquidity pools or tokens. DYOR.

EVM chains have diverse DEX ecosystems with varying liquidity, fees, and features depending on the chain (Ethereum mainnet vs. L2s like Base or Arbitrum).

## Key DEXes on EVM Chains

Major DEXes exist across EVM chains with strong liquidity on popular pairs. Options vary by chain (e.g., Uniswap on Ethereum and many L2s, PancakeSwap on BNB Chain, etc.).

**Best Practices**
- Compare rates across major DEXes before large swaps.
- Be aware of slippage, especially on less liquid pairs.
- Double-check token contract addresses.
- Use official sites only (verify URLs).
- Start with small test swaps when trying new platforms or tokens.

## Security Tips for DEX Usage on EVM Chains

- Never approve unlimited token spending unless necessary (revoke regularly).
- Be extremely cautious of new or low-liquidity tokens (higher scam risk).
- Use hardware wallets for large holdings when possible.
- Start small when interacting with new pools or tokens on EVM chains.

## Pixel Journey Relevance

EVM DEXes can be useful for efficient token movement and DeFi experimentation. This complements your WAX activities and will become more valuable as PxPortals expands cross-chain capabilities. However, your primary focus and capital should remain on WAX collections, Pixals, and the PXJ economy. Use EVM chains mainly as a complementary, low-cost testing ground.
