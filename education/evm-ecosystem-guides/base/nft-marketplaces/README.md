# 🖼️ NFT Marketplaces on Base — Growing Ecosystem for Collectors and Creators

**Base** has seen rapid growth in its NFT ecosystem thanks to low fees and fast transactions. While WAX remains the leader for creator tools, blends, and high-volume NFT activity (AtomicHub, Neftyblocks, etc.), Base offers excellent options for broader NFT trading, discovery, and community building at a fraction of Ethereum mainnet costs.

This makes Base a valuable secondary environment for Pixel Journey explorers who want to explore different NFT standards, marketplaces, and trading mechanics.

## Key NFT Marketplaces on Base

- **OpenSea** — Major platform with strong Base support for buying, selling, and discovering NFTs.
- **Magic Eden** and other Base-native or multi-chain marketplaces.
- **Emerging Base-specific platforms** — Growing number of specialized NFT projects and marketplaces.

## Getting Started with NFTs on Base

1. **Set up a wallet** (MetaMask or Rabby recommended) and add the Base network.
2. **Bridge a small amount of ETH** or tokens to Base.
3. **Connect to a trusted marketplace** (start with OpenSea or Magic Eden on Base).
4. **Browse collections** and review listings, provenance, and contract details carefully.
5. **Start with small test purchases** to learn the interface and confirm everything works.
6. **For selling or minting**: Follow the specific marketplace’s creator tools and verification process.

## Safety Best Practices

- **Always verify the marketplace and contract addresses** from official sources or explorers (basescan.org).
- **Read all transaction details** before confirming — especially approvals and gas settings.
- **Be cautious with new or unverified collections** — DYOR on rarity, utility, team, and roadmap.
- **Revoke approvals regularly** (use revoke.cash or manual explorer checks).
- **Use hardware wallets** for significant NFT holdings.
- **Watch for phishing sites** mimicking popular marketplaces.
