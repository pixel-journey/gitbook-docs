# 🔍 Explorers and Analytics on Ethereum

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Always use trusted, well-known explorers. Be cautious of fake explorer sites. Verify data across multiple sources when making important decisions. DYOR. Be especially cautious on mainnet due to high-value activity.

Good explorers and analytics tools are essential for safely navigating Ethereum and managing activities alongside your Pixel Journey on WAX.

## Key Explorers and Analytics Tools on Ethereum

Ethereum has excellent block explorers and analytics tools for transaction monitoring, account activity, DeFi positions, and ecosystem metrics (e.g., Etherscan).

**Best Practices**
- Bookmark trusted Ethereum explorer URLs (e.g., etherscan.io).
- Cross-reference important data across multiple tools when possible.
- Use explorers to verify contract addresses and transaction details before interacting with dApps.
- Monitor your own account activity regularly.
- Monitor gas prices carefully.

## Pixel Journey Relevance

Explorers and analytics tools on Ethereum help you verify transactions, monitor activity, and understand DeFi/NFT positions when experimenting on the chain. Strong familiarity with these tools helps you participate more safely while keeping your primary focus on WAX collections, Pixals, and the PXJ economy.

## Security Tips

- Never enter sensitive information on explorer sites.
- Be cautious of any site mimicking official explorers.
- Use analytics to detect unusual activity on your accounts early.
- Monitor gas prices to avoid overpaying or failed transactions.
