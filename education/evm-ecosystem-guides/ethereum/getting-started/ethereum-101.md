# 📝 Ethereum 101

## Introducing Ethereum: The Foundation of Web3

Ethereum is the original programmable blockchain that introduced smart contracts to the world. It powers the majority of DeFi, NFTs, and dApps, and serves as the settlement layer for many Layer 2 solutions. For Pixel Journey explorers, understanding Ethereum provides deep insight into how the broader EVM ecosystem works and prepares you for cross-chain strategies via future PxPortals.

### Key Features

- **Smart Contracts**: Self-executing code that powers decentralized applications.
- **Gas Fees**: Paid in ETH for transaction processing (higher on mainnet, much lower on L2s).
- **Native Token (ETH)**: Used for gas, staking (in Proof of Stake), and as the primary currency in the ecosystem.
- **Layer 2 Scaling**: Solutions like Optimism, Arbitrum, Base, and Polygon reduce fees and increase speed while inheriting Ethereum's security.
- **Mature Ecosystem**: Largest developer community, most audited protocols, and deepest liquidity.

### Why This Model Matters for Pixel Journey

Ethereum's security and decentralization make it the gold standard. While WAX excels at high-volume NFT actions with low costs, Ethereum (and its L2s) offers unmatched trust and infrastructure. Learning gas management, bridging, and dApp interaction here builds skills that transfer to all EVM chains and inform your WAX strategies.

### Current Ethereum Stats (approximate, late June 2026)

- Dominant smart contract platform by TVL and developer activity
- Strong L2 ecosystem with billions in bridged value
- Active governance through Ethereum Improvement Proposals (EIPs)

Ethereum continues to evolve with upgrades focused on scalability, security, and user experience.

## Getting Started Basics on Ethereum

1. **Wallet**: MetaMask, Rabby, or any EVM-compatible wallet. Hardware support strongly recommended for larger holdings.
2. **Get ETH**: Buy on exchanges or bridge from L2s/WAX via official bridges.
3. **Gas Management**: Monitor gas prices; use L2s for most activities to keep costs low.
4. **Explore**: Start with major dApps, marketplaces (OpenSea, Blur), and DEXes (Uniswap).

**Pixel Journey Tip**: Start on an L2 like Base or Polygon for cheap experimentation, then move to mainnet Ethereum for high-value or long-term activities. Bridge small test amounts first. Your WAX skills in accounts and resources translate to understanding gas and permissions here.
