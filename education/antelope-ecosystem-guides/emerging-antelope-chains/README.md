# 🌱 Emerging Antelope Chains

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Emerging chains carry higher risks including smart contract vulnerabilities, lower liquidity, and potential instability. Always DYOR thoroughly, start with very small amounts, and never invest more than you can afford to lose. Be extremely cautious with new or unproven projects.

This section covers emerging or newer Antelope-based blockchains that are gaining traction or have interesting features for experimentation. These chains are at earlier stages compared to WAX, XPR, or Vaulta, offering unique opportunities but also higher risks.

## Why Explore Emerging Antelope Chains?

- Useful for low-cost experimentation and learning new mechanics

## Getting Started Safely with Emerging Chains

### 1. Research Thoroughly
- Understand the chain's consensus, tokenomics, and development team.
- Review audits, community feedback, and track record.
- Be aware of risks associated with newer or smaller ecosystems.

### 2. Use Trusted Wallets
- Anchor is generally compatible with most Antelope chains.
- Enable hardware wallet integration for any significant holdings.

### 3. Start Very Small
- Test with minimal amounts to understand the ecosystem.
- Revoke approvals regularly.
- Monitor for unusual activity.

### 4. Security Best Practices
- Use hardware wallets for larger positions.
- Double-check all contract addresses and dApp URLs.
- Be extra cautious of new or low-liquidity tokens and projects.
- Never share your seed phrase or private keys.
