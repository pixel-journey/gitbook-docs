# 🌱 Emerging Antelope Chains

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Emerging chains carry higher risks including smart contract vulnerabilities, lower liquidity, potential instability, and less mature tooling. Always DYOR thoroughly, start with very small amounts, and never invest more than you can afford to lose. Be extremely cautious with new or unproven projects. These chains are suitable primarily for experimentation and learning, not large positions.

This section covers emerging or newer Antelope-based blockchains that are gaining traction or have interesting features for experimentation. These chains are at earlier stages compared to WAX, XPR, or Vaulta, offering unique opportunities but also higher risks.

## Getting Started Safely with Emerging Chains

### 1. Research Thoroughly
- Understand the chain's consensus mechanism, tokenomics, governance, and development team.
- Review any available audits, community feedback, and development track record.
- Be aware of risks associated with newer or smaller ecosystems (lower liquidity, potential instability, less battle-tested tooling).

### 2. Use Trusted Wallets
- **Anchor** is generally compatible with most Antelope chains and remains the recommended wallet.
- Enable hardware wallet integration (Ledger) for any significant holdings or experimentation.
- Verify chain configuration details before adding to your wallet.

### 3. Start Very Small
- Test with minimal amounts to understand the ecosystem, fees, and UX.
- Revoke approvals regularly after use.
- Monitor transactions closely on explorers.
- Be prepared for potential delays or issues common in newer chains.

### 4. Security Best Practices
- Use hardware wallets for larger positions or higher-risk experimentation.
- Double-check all contract addresses and dApp URLs every time.
- Be extra cautious of new or low-liquidity tokens and projects (higher scam risk).
- Never share your seed phrase or private keys.
- Start with testnet where available before moving to mainnet.

## Step-by-Step: Safe Exploration Workflow

1. Research the specific chain thoroughly (documentation, community, audits).
2. Set up Anchor wallet and correctly add the chain configuration.
3. Acquire a very small amount of the native token for testing.
4. Start with tiny test transactions to understand fees, speed, and UX.
5. Use trusted explorers to verify all activity.
6. Revoke any approvals regularly.
7. Keep detailed notes on your experience for future reference.
