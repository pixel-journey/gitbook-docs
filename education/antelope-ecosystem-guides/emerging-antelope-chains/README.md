# 🌱 Emerging Antelope Chains

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Emerging chains often carry higher risk due to newer codebases, smaller communities, and potentially less battle-tested security. Always DYOR thoroughly, start with very small amounts, and never invest more than you can afford to lose. Exercise extra caution with new or low-liquidity projects.

Emerging Antelope chains represent the next wave of innovation within the Antelope family. These chains often bring new features, different economic models, or specialized use cases while maintaining compatibility with Antelope tooling (Anchor wallet, similar account models, etc.).

## General Recommendations

### Wallet

**Anchor** remains the primary recommended wallet for most emerging Antelope chains, as it supports multiple chains in the family.

### Security Best Practices

- Use hardware wallets for any significant holdings.
- Start with extremely small test amounts.
- Verify all contracts and dApps thoroughly.
- Revoke approvals regularly.
- Be extra cautious of new or low-liquidity tokens and projects.
