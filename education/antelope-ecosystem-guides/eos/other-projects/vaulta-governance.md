# 🏛️ Vaulta Governance

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Governance participation on Vaulta involves voting and understanding proposals. Always DYOR thoroughly on proposals and candidates. Governance decisions can impact the network long-term. Be especially cautious with new or high-value projects.

Vaulta has a decentralized governance system centered around proposals, voting, and community participation, evolving with its focus on regulated Web3 banking and institutional use cases.

## Key Components

- **Proposals and Voting**: Users can participate in governance by voting on proposals using staked tokens.
- **Community and Development**: Governance helps guide the direction of the network, including features, partnerships, and resource allocation, with emphasis on compliance and security.

- Research current proposals and their implications, especially regarding compliance and institutional features.
- Follow official Vaulta governance channels and discussions.
- Participate thoughtfully in proposals that align with ecosystem health and security.
