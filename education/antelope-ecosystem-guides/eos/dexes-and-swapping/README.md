# 🔄 DEXes and Swapping on EOS

**DEXes and swapping on EOS** allow you to trade tokens efficiently within the mature Antelope ecosystem. While WAX has strong NFT-focused DEX activity (e.g., Alcor, TacoSwap), EOS offers liquid markets for broader token trading and DeFi experimentation. This complements your Pixel Journey activities by providing cost-effective ways to test swapping strategies and liquidity provision.

## Key DEXes and Swapping Platforms on EOS

- **Major DEXes**: Explore established platforms for token swaps, liquidity pools, and yield opportunities.
- **Swapping Tools**: User-friendly interfaces for quick trades with low fees compared to Ethereum mainnet.
- **Liquidity Provision**: Earn fees by providing liquidity to pools.

## Getting Started with Swapping on EOS

1. Use a compatible wallet like Anchor.
2. Acquire EOS and any tokens you want to swap.
3. Connect to a trusted DEX interface.
4. Review slippage, fees, and pool details before confirming.
5. Start with small test swaps to learn the interface.

## Safety Best Practices

- Always verify the DEX contract and URL from official sources.
- Read transaction details carefully (especially approvals and slippage).
- Start with small amounts when trying new pools or platforms.
- Revoke approvals regularly.
- DYOR on any yield or liquidity opportunity — even established DEXes carry smart contract risks.
