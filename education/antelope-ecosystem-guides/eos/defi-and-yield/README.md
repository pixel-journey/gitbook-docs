# 💰 DeFi and Yield on EOS

**DeFi and yield opportunities on EOS** leverage its mature ecosystem, REX resource model, and active governance. While WAX excels at NFT/gaming-focused DeFi (e.g., TacoSwap, Alcor farms), EOS offers liquid markets for broader token trading, liquidity provision, and yield farming. This provides Pixel Journey explorers with additional venues for cost-effective experimentation and strategy testing.

## Key DeFi and Yield Platforms on EOS

- **DEXes and Liquidity Pools**: Trade tokens and provide liquidity to earn fees.
- **Yield Farming**: Participate in farms and staking programs for additional rewards.
- **Lending/Borrowing Protocols**: Explore decentralized lending markets.
- **REX Integration**: Combine resource management with DeFi activities for optimized yields.

## Getting Started with DeFi and Yield on EOS

1. Use a compatible wallet like Anchor.
2. Acquire EOS and any tokens needed for pools or farms.
3. Research platforms, APYs, and risks through official explorers and community resources.
4. Start with small test amounts in liquidity pools or farms.
5. Monitor positions regularly and understand impermanent loss or smart contract risks.

## Safety Best Practices

- Verify all platforms and contracts from official sources or explorers (eosauthority.com, bloks.io).
- Read transaction details carefully (especially approvals and pool parameters).
- Start small when trying new protocols or farms.
- Revoke approvals regularly.
- DYOR on any yield opportunity — even established platforms carry smart contract and market risks.
- Use hardware wallets for significant positions.
