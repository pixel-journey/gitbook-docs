# 🗳️ Governance on EOS — Participating Safely in the Antelope Ecosystem

**Governance on EOS** is one of its mature strengths. Token holders can vote on proposals that shape the chain's development, resources, and direction. This offers valuable learning for Pixel Journey explorers interested in decentralized decision-making, while keeping primary focus on WAX.

## How EOS Governance Works

- **Voting with Staked EOS**: Stake EOS to gain voting power. The more you stake, the more weight your vote carries.
- **Proposals**: Community or block producers submit proposals for upgrades, resource allocation, or other changes.
- **Voting Period**: Proposals have defined voting windows. You can vote yes, no, or abstain.
- **Block Producers**: 21 elected block producers run the network; governance influences who gets elected and how resources are managed.

## Getting Started with Governance

1. Use a wallet like Anchor that supports EOS governance voting.
2. Stake EOS to activate voting power (via REX or direct staking).
3. Review active proposals on official explorers or governance portals (e.g., eosauthority.com or community dashboards).
4. Research proposals thoroughly — understand the impact before voting.
5. Cast your vote during the active period.
6. Monitor results and participate in discussions in the EOS community.

## Safety & Best Practices

- **DYOR on Proposals**: Never vote on something you don't understand. Scams or poorly thought-out proposals can exist.
- **Start Small**: Begin with small stakes while learning the process.
- **Secure Your Keys**: Governance involves staking — use hardware wallets for larger amounts.
- **Revoke Approvals**: As always, regularly review and revoke smart contract permissions.
- **Community Verification**: Cross-check proposals in official EOS channels and trusted community sources.
