# ⚛️ AtomicHub on WAX

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> While AtomicHub has been one of the most trusted marketplaces on WAX historically, always verify current status, especially after any ownership or operational changes. DYOR before large transactions.

AtomicHub has long been one of the most popular and widely used NFT marketplaces on WAX. It offers strong support for AtomicAssets, including Pixals, and provides tools for buying, selling, listing, and managing collections.

## What AtomicHub Is Used For

AtomicHub allows users to:

- Buy and sell AtomicAssets NFTs (including Pixals and related collections)
- List and manage their own NFTs
- Browse collections with search and filtering tools
- Access basic creator and collection management features
- View transaction history and collection activity

It has historically been a central hub for the WAX NFT community due to its broad support for the AtomicAssets standard and relatively user-friendly interface.

## Pixel Journey Relevance
AtomicHub has been extensively used by the Pixel Journey community for trading Pixals and related NFTs. It remains a viable option, particularly while PxMarket is still maturing. Many collectors and traders are familiar with its interface.

## Current Recommendation
While **PxMarket** is our top recommended marketplace for Pixel Journey assets going forward, AtomicHub can still be used for broader WAX NFT activity or when specific collections are better supported there.

## Security Tips
- Always double-check collection contracts and listings.
- Be cautious of new or unverified collections.
- Revoke approvals regularly.
- Start small when exploring less familiar parts of the platform.
