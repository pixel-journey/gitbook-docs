# 📝 WAX 101

## Introducing WAX: Carbon-Neutral NFTs and an Innovative Resource Model

WAX has established itself as a leading blockchain for NFTs and digital collectibles, with a strong focus on sustainability and usability.

### Carbon-Neutral NFTs

WAX offsets the environmental impact of NFT creation and transactions, making it one of the more eco-conscious blockchain options for creators and collectors.

### Resource Model (CPU, NET, RAM)

WAX uses a unique staking-based resource system instead of traditional gas fees:

- **CPU**: Used for processing smart contracts and transactions. Stake WAX to gain CPU resources (regenerates over ~24 hours).
- **NET**: Used for network bandwidth and data transfer. Stake WAX to gain NET resources (regenerates over ~24 hours).
- **RAM**: Used for storing account data on the blockchain. Bought/sold instantly (small trade fee). Does **not** regenerate automatically.

**Recommended starting resources** (approximate, at current rates):
- 50–200 WAX staked to CPU
- 10–20 WAX staked to NET
- 10–50 WAX worth of RAM

This setup allows for hundreds of actions per day comfortably. Resources are spent on transactions and regenerate (CPU/NET) or can be managed (RAM).

### Why This Model Matters

The resource system helps prevent spam and network congestion while giving users direct control and skin in the game. It keeps WAX efficient, scalable, and accessible.
