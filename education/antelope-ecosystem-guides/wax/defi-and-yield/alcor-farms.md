# 🌾 Alcor Farms and Yield on WAX

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Yield farming and liquidity provision carry risks including impermanent loss, smart contract vulnerabilities, and changing APYs. Always DYOR thoroughly, start with small positions, and never invest more than you can afford to lose. Monitor your positions regularly.

Alcor offers various liquidity pools and farming opportunities, many of which are relevant to the Pixel Journey economy (especially $PXJ pairs and related tokens).

## What Alcor Farms Offer

Alcor Farms allow users to:

- Provide liquidity to token pairs and earn trading fees
- Stake LP tokens in farms to earn additional rewards (often in $PXJ or other ecosystem tokens)
- Participate in yield-generating activities tied to the WAX DeFi ecosystem
- Support liquidity for important pairs like those involving $PXJ

These opportunities let users earn yield on their tokens while contributing to the overall liquidity and stability of the WAX token economy.

## Key Alcor Yield Opportunities

### $PXJ Related Pools and Farms
Alcor has historically hosted important liquidity pools and farms for $PXJ and ecosystem tokens. These are often used by Pixel Journey participants for earning yield while supporting the token economy.

### Other Major Pools
Alcor supports many other WAX ecosystem tokens with varying liquidity and reward structures.

## Pixel Journey Relevance
Participating in Alcor farms and liquidity pools can be a way to generate additional yield on tokens while staying within the WAX ecosystem. This complements PxStaking and other Pixel Journey-native yield mechanisms. However, your primary focus should remain on core Pixel Journey activities (collecting, crafting, staking NFTs, etc.).

As PxSwap and PxStaking mature, they may offer optimized or safer ways to access similar yield opportunities with better integration.

## Best Practices

- Compare current APYs and risks across available pools.
- Understand impermanent loss, especially on volatile pairs.
- Start with very small test positions.
- Monitor positions frequently — DeFi moves fast.
- Use hardware wallets for larger liquidity positions when possible.
- Revoke approvals regularly.

## Security Tips

- Always verify you are interacting with the official Alcor contracts.
- Be extremely cautious of new or very high-APY farms (often higher risk).
- Never approve contracts you don’t fully understand.
- Keep some WAX liquid for transaction fees.

## Recommendation
Alcor farms can be a useful part of a diversified WAX DeFi strategy. However, they should be approached with caution and used as a complement to (not a replacement for) core Pixel Journey staking and activities. Always prioritize safety and understanding over chasing high yields.
