## Step-by-Step: Safe DeFi Workflow on WAX

1. Research the farm/pool thoroughly (APY history, token risks, impermanent loss potential, team reputation).
2. Verify official platform URLs and contract addresses from trusted sources.
3. Connect your wallet (Anchor recommended, with hardware integration for larger positions).
4. Start with a tiny test position to confirm everything works and understand the mechanics.
5. Review all transaction details carefully before confirming.
6. Monitor your position regularly on explorers and the platform dashboard.
7. Revoke approvals regularly after use.
8. Withdraw or rebalance positions thoughtfully when rates or conditions change.
