# ✍️ PXJ Writers Program

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> The PXJ Writers Program is our own community content initiative. Participation is voluntary and should be done for the love of the ecosystem and learning. Always follow official guidelines when contributing.

The **PXJ Writers Program** is Pixel Journey's own content creation initiative. It allows community members to contribute articles, guides, and educational content about the Pixel Journey ecosystem, WAX, and related topics.

## Purpose of the Program

- Encourage high-quality educational content about Pixel Journey and the broader ecosystem
- Help new users learn through community-written guides
- Reward contributors for valuable contributions
- Build a rich knowledge base alongside our official documentation

## How It Works

Community members can write articles, tutorials, or explainers on topics relevant to Pixel Journey (crafting, staking, tools, strategies, etc.). Contributions are reviewed and, when accepted, may be featured in our documentation or rewarded through the ecosystem.

## Pixel Journey Relevance
This program is directly tied to the Pixel Journey mission of education and community building. It complements our official GitBook docs and PxLearn portal. Contributing to or reading content from the Writers Program is an excellent way to deepen your understanding while helping others on their journey.

## Getting Involved
- Watch for official calls for writers in our Discord
- Follow the guidelines when submitting content
- Focus on helpful, accurate, and well-written pieces
- Topics can include tools, strategies, crafting systems, security, or ecosystem explanations

## Recommendation
The PXJ Writers Program is a great way to contribute to the community and learn at the same time. Even if you don't write, reading community-created content can provide valuable insights and different perspectives on the Pixel Journey ecosystem.

*Educational content only. Always DYOR. Not financial advice.*
