# 🏛️ WAX Governance

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Governance participation on WAX involves voting for Block Producers and understanding the OIG/RFP system. Always DYOR on candidates and proposals. Governance decisions can impact the network long-term.

WAX has a decentralized governance system centered around **Block Producers (BPs)**, the **Office of Inspector General (OIG)**, and the **Request for Proposals (RFP)** system.

## Key Components

- **Block Producers**: 21 active BPs responsible for producing blocks and maintaining the network. Users can vote for BPs using their staked WAX.
- **OIG (Office of Inspector General)**: Oversees the network and helps with governance processes.
- **RFP System**: Community-driven proposals for funding and development on WAX.

## Pixel Journey Relevance
Understanding WAX governance helps you see how the network evolves and how decisions are made. As the Pixel Journey ecosystem grows and we engage more deeply with the WAX blockchain (and eventually other chains via PxPortals), familiarity with governance becomes increasingly valuable.

While most Pixel Journey participants focus on collecting, crafting, and DeFi activities, having a basic understanding of how the underlying chain is governed is beneficial for long-term holders and active community members.

## Getting Started with Governance
- Stake WAX to gain voting power.
- Research current Block Producers and their track records.
- Follow official WAX governance channels and discussions.
- Participate in RFPs when relevant to the ecosystem.

## Recommendation
Governance is an important part of any blockchain ecosystem. While not everyone needs to be deeply involved, staying informed helps you make better long-term decisions about your involvement in WAX and the Pixel Journey.
