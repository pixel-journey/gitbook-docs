# 🌾 XPR Farms and Yield on XPR Network

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Yield farming and liquidity provision carry risks including impermanent loss, smart contract vulnerabilities, and changing APYs. Always DYOR thoroughly, start with small positions, and never invest more than you can afford to lose. Monitor your positions regularly.

XPR Network has growing yield and farming opportunities, often with low or feeless experiences for users.

## Key XPR Yield Opportunities

XPR has platforms supporting liquidity provision, farming, and staking with efficient mechanics.

## Pixel Journey Relevance
Participating in XPR farms and liquidity pools can be a way to generate additional yield on tokens while staying within the Antelope ecosystem. This complements PxStaking and other Pixel Journey-native yield mechanisms. However, your primary focus should remain on core Pixel Journey staking and activities.

As PxSwap and PxStaking mature, they may offer optimized or safer ways to access similar yield opportunities with better integration.

## Best Practices

- Compare current APYs and risks across available pools.
- Understand impermanent loss, especially on volatile pairs.
- Start with very small test positions.
- Monitor positions frequently — DeFi moves fast.
- Use hardware wallets for larger liquidity positions when possible.
- Revoke approvals regularly.

## Security Tips

- Always verify you are interacting with official contracts.
- Be extremely cautious of new or very high-APY farms (often higher risk).
- Never approve contracts you don’t fully understand.
- Keep some XPR liquid for transaction fees.

## Recommendation
XPR farms can be a useful part of a diversified Antelope DeFi strategy. However, they should be approached with caution and used as a complement to (not a replacement for) core Pixel Journey staking and activities. Always prioritize safety and understanding over chasing high yields.
