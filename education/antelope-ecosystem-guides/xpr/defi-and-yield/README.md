## Step-by-Step: Safe DeFi Workflow on XPR

1. Research the farm/pool thoroughly (APY history, token risks, impermanent loss potential).
2. Verify official platform URLs and contracts.
3. Connect your wallet (Anchor recommended).
4. Start with a tiny test position to confirm everything works.
5. Review all transaction details carefully before confirming.
6. Monitor your position regularly on explorers and the platform.
7. Revoke approvals regularly after use.
8. Withdraw or rebalance positions thoughtfully when rates change.
