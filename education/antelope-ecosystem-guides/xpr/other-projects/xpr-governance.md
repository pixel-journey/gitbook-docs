# 🏛️ XPR Governance

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Governance participation on XPR involves voting and understanding proposals. Always DYOR thoroughly on proposals and candidates. Governance decisions can impact the network long-term.

XPR Network has a decentralized governance system centered around proposals, voting, and community participation.

## Key Components

- **Proposals and Voting**: Users can participate in governance by voting on proposals using staked XPR.
- **Community and Development**: Governance helps guide the direction of the network, including features, partnerships, and resource allocation.

## Pixel Journey Relevance
Understanding XPR governance helps you see how the network evolves and how decisions are made. As the Pixel Journey ecosystem grows and we engage more deeply with multiple Antelope chains via PxPortals, familiarity with governance across chains becomes increasingly valuable.

While most Pixel Journey participants focus on collecting, crafting, and DeFi activities on WAX, having a basic understanding of how other chains like XPR are governed is beneficial for long-term holders and active community members.

## Getting Started with Governance on XPR
- Stake XPR to gain voting power.
- Research current proposals and their implications.
- Follow official XPR governance channels and discussions.
- Participate thoughtfully in proposals that align with ecosystem health.

## Recommendation
Governance is an important part of any blockchain ecosystem. While not everyone needs to be deeply involved, staying informed helps you make better long-term decisions about your involvement in XPR and the broader Antelope family.
