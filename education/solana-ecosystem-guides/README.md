# 🟣 Solana Ecosystem Guides

> **⚠️ Important Safety Note**  
> Solana is a high-performance blockchain with its own security considerations. Always DYOR thoroughly, start with small amounts when exploring new dApps or tokens, and never invest more than you can afford to lose. Be especially cautious of new or low-liquidity projects.

Solana offers a fast, low-cost environment that is excellent for experimentation, DeFi, and NFTs. While your primary focus remains on WAX and the Pixel Journey ecosystem, Solana can serve as a valuable complementary chain for testing strategies, accessing different opportunities, and preparing for future PxPortals cross-chain capabilities.

- Future PxPortals integration may connect Solana more seamlessly with WAX and other chains

## General Recommendations

### Wallet

**Phantom** is the most popular and user-friendly wallet for Solana. It offers excellent UX, strong security features, and broad dApp support. Many users also use **Solflare** or hardware wallet integration (Ledger) for larger holdings.

### Security Best Practices

- Use hardware wallets (Ledger) for significant holdings when possible.
- Start with very small test amounts on new dApps or tokens.
- Verify all contract addresses and dApp URLs carefully.
- Revoke token approvals regularly.
- Be extra cautious of new or low-liquidity tokens and projects (higher scam/rug risk).
