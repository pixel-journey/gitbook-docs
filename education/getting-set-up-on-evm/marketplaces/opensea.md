# OpenSea

[OpenSea](https://opensea.io) is the most widely recognized NFT marketplace in Web3, operating since 2017. It supports all major EVM chains and serves as the default discovery and trading platform for most new EVM NFT collectors.

## Supported Chains

OpenSea supports NFTs on Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Blast, and more. You can browse and trade NFTs across all chains from the same account.

## Setting Up Your OpenSea Profile

### Connecting Your Wallet

1. Go to [opensea.io](https://opensea.io) and click **"Login"** in the top right.
2. Select your wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Phantom, etc.).
3. Approve the connection and sign the sign-in message (this is an off-chain signature — it does not cost gas).
4. Your OpenSea account is now active. Your profile URL will be `opensea.io/<your-address>`.

### Personalizing Your Profile

1. Click your profile icon (top right) → **"Profile"**.
2. Click the **edit (pencil) icon**.
3. Add a username, bio, and profile/banner image.
4. Link your email address to receive notifications about offers and sales.

## Buying NFTs on OpenSea

### Browse and Filter

- Use the search bar to find a collection by name.
- On a collection page, use **filters** (left sidebar) to narrow by traits, price range, and rarity.
- Toggle **"Buy Now"** to show only listed NFTs (not all owned NFTs are for sale).

### Making a Purchase

1. Click on an NFT you want to buy.
2. If it has a **"Buy now"** price, click that button.
3. Review the transaction details — this will be a real on-chain transaction.
4. Confirm in your wallet. Ensure you are on the correct network and have enough gas.

### Making an Offer

You can make offers on any NFT, even if it is not listed for sale:

1. Click the NFT and select **"Make offer"**.
2. Offers on OpenSea are made in **WETH** (Wrapped ETH) on Ethereum/L2s, or **WMATIC/POL** on Polygon.
3. Set your offer price and expiry date.
4. Sign the off-chain offer message (no gas required to place the offer).
5. If the seller accepts, the trade executes automatically.

> To place offers, you need WETH (or WMATIC/POL). Wrap your ETH/POL from the wallet section of your OpenSea profile or directly in your wallet.

## Selling NFTs on OpenSea

### Fixed Price Listing

1. Go to your profile and find the NFT you want to list.
2. Click **"List for sale"**.
3. Select **"Fixed price"**, enter your price in ETH (or POL on Polygon).
4. Set a duration (1 day to 6 months).
5. Confirm the listing — this may require an on-chain approval transaction the first time you list a collection. Subsequent listings of the same collection are gas-free (off-chain signatures).

### Auction (Timed Auction)

1. Select **"Timed auction"** when listing.
2. Set a reserve price and duration.
3. The highest bidder at the end of the auction can claim the NFT.

## Fees

OpenSea's fee structure has evolved significantly:

- **Protocol fee**: 0.5% of the sale price, paid by the buyer
- **Creator royalty**: Optional (0% by default on OpenSea as of 2024). Collections can set a royalty and buyers/sellers can choose whether to honour it.

On L2s (Base, Polygon, Arbitrum), gas fees on OpenSea transactions are minimal — often less than $0.01.
