# Magic Eden

[Magic Eden](https://magiceden.io) began as the dominant NFT marketplace on Solana and has since expanded into a **cross-chain platform** supporting Ethereum, Base, Polygon, and Bitcoin Ordinals. This makes it especially useful for collectors who operate across both EVM and Solana ecosystems — including Pixel Journey collectors.

## Why Magic Eden for EVM?

- **One account, many chains** — Trade EVM and Solana NFTs from the same interface.
- **Launchpad** — Magic Eden frequently hosts new collection mints, making it a discovery hub for fresh projects.
- **Rewards program** — The Magic Eden Diamonds program rewards trading activity across chains.

## Supported Chains

Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, Solana, and Bitcoin (Ordinals/Runes). You can switch the active chain using the chain selector in the top navigation.

---

## Connecting to Magic Eden

1. Go to [magiceden.io](https://magiceden.io) and click **"Connect Wallet"**.
2. For EVM chains, connect MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, or Phantom (Phantom supports both EVM and Solana).
3. Sign the off-chain authentication message (no gas required).
4. Use the **chain selector** to switch between EVM chains and Solana.

> Phantom is a great wallet for Magic Eden specifically, because it natively supports both Solana and EVM chains in one extension.

## Buying on Magic Eden

### Browse Collections

- Use the top navigation to filter by chain and category.
- The **"Popular collections"** and **"Trending"** tabs show what is active right now.
- Collection pages include floor price, volume, listed count, and rarity filters.

### Making a Purchase

1. Click an NFT and review its details and price.
2. Click **"Buy now"** for listed items.
3. Confirm the transaction in your wallet (ensure you are on the correct chain with enough gas).

### Making Offers

Magic Eden supports both individual and collection-wide bids on most chains. Offers are typically made in the chain's wrapped native token (WETH, etc.).

---

## Selling on Magic Eden

1. Navigate to your **profile / portfolio**.
2. Select the NFT you want to list and click **"List"**.
3. Set your price in the chain's native token.
4. Confirm the listing (first listing per collection may require an on-chain approval).

---

## The Launchpad

Magic Eden's **Launchpad** is where new collections mint for the first time. To participate:

1. Find the launch on the Launchpad page and note the mint date/time and price.
2. Connect your wallet and ensure you have enough native token for the mint price + gas.
3. When the mint goes live, click **"Mint"** and confirm.

> Popular launches can be competitive and gas-intensive. Be cautious of fake launch pages — only use links from Magic Eden's official Launchpad or verified project channels.

---

## Fees

- **Marketplace fee** — Magic Eden's fee varies by chain and has trended toward low or zero on EVM to stay competitive. Always check the fee shown at checkout.
- **Creator royalties** — Magic Eden has generally supported honoring creator royalties, though enforcement varies by collection.
- **Gas** — Standard network gas applies per chain.

---

## Magic Eden Rewards (Diamonds)

Trading on Magic Eden earns **Diamonds**, the platform's loyalty rewards. These accrue based on your buying and selling activity across all supported chains. Check the rewards tab in your profile to track them.
