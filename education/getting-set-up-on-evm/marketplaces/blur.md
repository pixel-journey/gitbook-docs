# Blur

## What is Blur?

[Blur](https://blur.io) is a professional-grade NFT marketplace and aggregator built on Ethereum, launched in October 2022. It was purpose-built for high-volume NFT traders, aggregating listings from OpenSea, X2Y2, LooksRare, and other platforms so you can see and sweep the best-priced items across all of them in one place.

Blur quickly became the dominant NFT trading platform by volume on Ethereum, largely due to its speed, its zero-marketplace-fee model, and its BLUR token rewards programme.

---

## Key Features

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Aggregation | Pulls listings from all major ETH marketplaces |
| Marketplace fee | 0% (creators can set royalties) |
| Token rewards | Trade and list to earn BLUR tokens |
| Bulk buying | Sweep entire collections in one transaction |
| Portfolio analytics | Real-time floor, volume, and bid-depth data |
| Blend | Built-in NFT lending / buy-now-pay-later protocol |

## Supported Chains

Blur operates primarily on **Ethereum mainnet**. It does not currently support Base, Polygon, or other L2s — it is the go-to platform specifically for blue-chip Ethereum NFTs (Bored Apes, CryptoPunks, Azuki, etc.).

## Buying NFTs on Blur

### Single Purchase
1. Search for a collection using the search bar at the top
2. Click into the collection and browse the **Listings** tab
3. Click any item to view its detail page
4. Review the price and any royalty info, then click **Buy Now**
5. Confirm the transaction in your wallet

### Sweeping a Floor (Bulk Buy)
Blur's defining feature — buy multiple floor items in one transaction:
1. Open a collection page
2. Click the **Sweep** button (broom icon) at the top right
3. Drag the slider or type a quantity to select how many floor items to buy
4. Review the total ETH cost shown
5. Click **Sweep** and confirm the transaction in your wallet

> **Tip:** Sweeping is significantly cheaper per-item than individual purchases in terms of time, and can save gas versus separate transactions.

## Listing NFTs on Blur

1. Connect your wallet and go to your **Portfolio** (top-right avatar)
2. Click the **List** tab to see your unlisted NFTs
3. Select the item(s) you want to list
4. Set your price — Blur shows you the current floor and best offer for context
5. Choose whether to list only on Blur or across multiple platforms
6. Click **List** and sign the listing (no gas required for listings)

---

## Blur Bids (Offers)

Blur has a unique collection-level bidding system:

1. Go to any collection page and click the **Bids** tab
2. You will see a depth chart of active bids at various price levels
3. To place a bid, click **Place Bid**, enter your price, and approve the WETH (Wrapped ETH) spend
4. Your bid competes with others — sellers can accept the highest bid at any time

> Collection bids use **WETH** (Wrapped ETH), not plain ETH. You will need to wrap some ETH first via the prompt Blur shows you, or via [Uniswap](https://app.uniswap.org).

## Blend (NFT Lending)

Blur includes **Blend**, a peer-to-peer NFT lending protocol that lets you:

- **Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL):** Purchase a high-value NFT with a down payment and repay over time
- **Borrow against NFTs:** Use an NFT you already own as collateral to borrow ETH

Blend is advanced territory — thoroughly read the documentation and understand the liquidation risks before using it.

---

## BLUR Token Rewards

Blur distributes its native **BLUR** token to active traders and listers through ongoing seasonal airdrop campaigns:

- **Listing rewards:** List NFTs at or near the floor price to accumulate points
- **Bidding rewards:** Place competitive collection bids
- **Trading rewards:** Buy and sell NFTs through Blur

Rewards are distributed at the end of each season. Points accumulate in real time and are visible on your portfolio page.

## Security Notes

- Blur is a well-audited, widely used platform — but always verify you are on **blur.io** (not a phishing clone)
- Listing signatures are free but binding — only sign listing messages on the real site
- Blur smart contracts have been audited; Blend (lending) carries additional smart contract risk
- Never share your seed phrase with anyone, including "Blur support"
