# Arbitrum

## What is Arbitrum?

Arbitrum is an Ethereum Layer 2 built by **Offchain Labs**, launched in August 2021. It uses **Optimistic Rollup** technology — transactions are processed off-chain, assumed to be valid by default, and posted to Ethereum with a fraud-proof window that allows anyone to challenge invalid transactions.

Arbitrum consistently holds the **highest total value locked (TVL) of any Ethereum L2**, making it the dominant chain for serious DeFi activity. Its NFT and gaming ecosystem has grown rapidly alongside that DeFi base.

## Why Arbitrum?

- **DeFi depth**: Arbitrum is home to many of the most sophisticated DeFi protocols — GMX, Camelot, Pendle, and others draw billions in liquidity.
- **Low fees, high security**: Fees are a fraction of Ethereum mainnet, while security is directly inherited from Ethereum L1.
- **Nitro upgrade**: The Arbitrum Nitro upgrade (2022) made Arbitrum fully EVM-equivalent — meaning any Ethereum smart contract can be deployed without modification.
- **ARB governance**: Arbitrum has a native governance token (ARB) and a DAO that governs the protocol. ARB can be staked and used in governance votes.
- **Growing NFT scene**: While primarily known for DeFi, Arbitrum has seen growth in NFT projects and gaming — particularly gaming projects that benefit from its low fees and high throughput.

## Arbitrum One vs. Arbitrum Nova

Offchain Labs operates two production chains:

| | Arbitrum One | Arbitrum Nova |
|---|---|---|
| Chain ID | 42161 | 42170 |
| Technology | Optimistic Rollup | AnyTrust (lighter security) |
| Security | Full Ethereum security | More trust-dependent |
| Use case | DeFi, NFTs, general | Gaming, social apps needing ultra-low fees |

**For most users and for Pixel Journey, Arbitrum One (42161) is the right choice.**

## Getting ETH on Arbitrum

ETH on Arbitrum One is the same ETH you know from Ethereum mainnet, just bridged over:

**Option 1: Bridge from Ethereum mainnet**
Use the [Arbitrum Bridge](https://bridge.arbitrum.io) to move ETH from Ethereum to Arbitrum One. Withdrawals back to Ethereum take 7 days (the fraud-proof window). For faster withdrawals, use [Across Protocol](https://across.to) or [Orbiter Finance](https://orbiter.finance).

**Option 2: Buy on a centralized exchange and withdraw directly**
Binance, OKX, and several other exchanges support direct withdrawal to Arbitrum One. This avoids bridging fees entirely.

**Option 3: On-ramp directly to Arbitrum**
MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, and Rabby all have integrated fiat on-ramps that can deposit ETH directly onto Arbitrum.
