# Base

## What is Base?

Base is an Ethereum Layer 2 blockchain built and maintained by [Coinbase](https://coinbase.com), launched publicly in August 2023. It uses the **OP Stack** (the same technology that powers Optimism) and regularly posts transaction data back to Ethereum mainnet for security.

Base was designed from the ground up to be the most accessible on-ramp into EVM for new users — especially those coming from a Coinbase exchange account. It has rapidly grown into one of the most active L2s by transaction volume.

## Why Base Stands Out

- **Coinbase integration**: If you already use Coinbase, you can move funds from your exchange account to Base in a few taps with near-zero fees.
- **Very low fees**: Most transactions cost a fraction of a cent. This makes it practical for everyday use, gaming, small NFT trades, and onboarding new users.
- **Growing NFT and creator ecosystem**: Base has attracted a wave of new NFT projects, generative art, and creator communities — including Pixel Journey.
- **EVM compatibility**: Every tool that works on Ethereum works on Base. Your MetaMask wallet, OpenSea, block explorers — all of them work natively.
- **Gasless transactions**: Some Base dApps use Coinbase's paymaster infrastructure to sponsor gas fees for users entirely, making certain interactions completely free.

## Getting ETH on Base

ETH on Base is separate from ETH on Ethereum mainnet — you need to either bridge it or buy it directly:

**Option 1: Bridge from Ethereum mainnet**
Use the [Base Bridge](https://bridge.base.org) to move ETH from mainnet to Base. Note that the official bridge has a 7-day withdrawal delay from Base back to mainnet. For faster bridging either direction, use [Across Protocol](https://across.to) or [Relay](https://relay.link).

**Option 2: Buy directly via Coinbase**
If you have a Coinbase account, tap "Send" and choose "Base network" as the destination. ETH will arrive in your wallet already on Base.

**Option 3: On-ramp within Coinbase Wallet**
Open Coinbase Wallet, select Base network, and tap "Buy". This takes you through a fiat on-ramp that deposits ETH directly onto Base.
