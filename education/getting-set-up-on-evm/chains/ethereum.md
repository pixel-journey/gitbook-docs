# Ethereum

## What is Ethereum?

Ethereum is the blockchain that invented smart contracts and kickstarted the entire EVM ecosystem. Launched in 2015 by Vitalik Buterin and a founding team, Ethereum is the most battle-tested programmable blockchain in existence. Every EVM-compatible chain — Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism — owes its existence to Ethereum and ultimately derives its security from it.

After "The Merge" in September 2022, Ethereum switched from Proof of Work to **Proof of Stake**, cutting its energy consumption by over 99.9%. Ethereum validators stake ETH to propose and attest to blocks.

## The Role of Ethereum in the EVM Ecosystem

Ethereum is the **settlement layer** for most major L2s. When you transact on Base or Arbitrum, those transactions are eventually posted to and verified by Ethereum. This gives L2 users Ethereum-level security at a fraction of the cost.

For NFTs and digital collectibles specifically, Ethereum mainnet carries a cultural weight that no other chain has matched. The most sought-after collections — CryptoPunks, BAYC, Art Blocks — live on Ethereum. Owning an NFT on Ethereum mainnet carries a prestige that comes from the chain's permanence, decentralization, and history.

## Gas Fees on Ethereum

Ethereum mainnet gas fees are meaningfully higher than any L2. At times of peak demand they can reach $50–$100+ per transaction. At quiet times they may be just a few dollars.

Because of this, Ethereum mainnet is generally used for:

- **High-value NFT trades** where the fee is small relative to the asset value
- **DeFi operations** involving large positions
- **Long-term storage** of significant assets (buy once, hold — don't transact daily)

For everyday NFT trading, minting, or gaming activity, L2s like Base or Polygon are far more practical.
