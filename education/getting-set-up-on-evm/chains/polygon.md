# Polygon

## What is Polygon?

Polygon (formerly Matic Network) is one of the oldest and most battle-tested Ethereum scaling solutions, having launched its mainnet in 2020. It operates as both a **sidechain** with its own Proof of Stake validator set and, more recently, as an Ethereum L2 via **Polygon zkEVM** (zero-knowledge proof technology).

The "main" Polygon chain — the one most users, NFT projects, and games operate on — is **Polygon PoS** (Chain ID 137). This is where Pixel Journey is active.

## MATIC → POL: The Token Migration

In 2024, Polygon migrated its native token from **MATIC** to **POL** as part of the Polygon 2.0 upgrade. If you have MATIC, it can be migrated 1:1 to POL via the [Polygon Portal](https://portal.polygon.technology). Most exchanges now issue POL directly. The ticker change to look for on exchanges is now **POL**.

## Why Polygon?

- **Established ecosystem**: Polygon was one of the first chains to achieve mainstream NFT adoption. Reddit Collectible Avatars, OpenSea's early NFT boom, and countless gaming projects launched here.
- **Ultra-low fees**: Fees are typically under a cent. POL (the gas token) is inexpensive and easy to acquire.
- **OpenSea native**: OpenSea was one of the first major NFT marketplaces to support Polygon, and a large share of its NFT volume still flows through Polygon.
- **Atomichub on Polygon**: Atomichub — well known to WAX users — also operates on Polygon, providing a familiar marketplace experience for those bridging from WAX.
- **Enterprise adoption**: Polygon has partnerships with Starbucks, Draftkings, Reddit, and many others, giving it mainstream exposure beyond crypto-native users.

## Getting POL for Gas

**Option 1: Buy on a centralized exchange**
POL is listed on most major exchanges (Coinbase, Binance, Kraken). Buy POL and withdraw it to your Polygon PoS address.

**Option 2: Bridge ETH from Ethereum**
Use the [Polygon Portal](https://portal.polygon.technology) to bridge ETH (or USDC) from Ethereum mainnet to Polygon. The bridged ETH can then be swapped for POL on QuickSwap or Uniswap (Polygon).

**Option 3: Use a gas swap tool**
If you have USDC or another token on Polygon but no POL for gas, many wallets (MetaMask, Rabby) offer a built-in "Get gas" feature that swaps a small amount of token for POL.
