# Optimism

## What is Optimism?

Optimism is an Ethereum Layer 2 built by the **Optimism Foundation**, launched in December 2021. Like Arbitrum, it uses **Optimistic Rollup** technology. What sets Optimism apart is its strong ideological alignment with Ethereum's values — it donates a significant portion of sequencer revenue to public goods funding via **Retroactive Public Goods Funding (RetroPGF)**.

Optimism is also the creator of the **OP Stack** — the open-source framework that powers both Optimism itself and Coinbase's Base chain. This means Base and Optimism are technically closely related and can communicate efficiently via native bridges.

## The OP Stack and the Superchain Vision

Optimism's long-term vision is the **Superchain** — a network of OP Stack chains (including Base, Mode, Zora, and others) that all interoperate seamlessly. As this vision matures, assets and messages will flow between these chains with minimal friction.

For Pixel Journey users, this means Base and Optimism are likely to become increasingly interoperable over time — assets bridged to Base may become easily movable to Optimism and vice versa.

## Why Optimism?

- **Public goods mission**: Optimism channels sequencer profits into funding open-source software and Ethereum infrastructure. This creates a unique community culture around the chain.
- **OP governance token**: OP token holders participate in governance of the Optimism Collective and allocate RetroPGF funding.
- **Low fees**: Comparable to other OP Stack chains — usually a cent or less per transaction.
- **Well-supported ecosystem**: All major DeFi protocols, OpenSea, and most wallets support Optimism.
- **Velodrome and native DeFi**: Velodrome Finance is the dominant native DEX on Optimism, with deep liquidity and ve(3,3) tokenomics.

## Getting ETH on Optimism

**Option 1: Bridge from Ethereum mainnet**
Use the [Optimism Bridge](https://app.optimism.io/bridge) to move ETH from Ethereum to Optimism. As with all optimistic rollups, withdrawal back to mainnet takes 7 days without a fast bridge. Use [Across Protocol](https://across.to) for fast exits.

**Option 2: Buy on a centralized exchange and withdraw directly**
Several exchanges (Coinbase, Binance, Kraken) support direct ETH withdrawals to Optimism — no bridging required.

**Option 3: Bridge from Base**
Because Base and Optimism both use the OP Stack, bridging between them is fast and cheap. If you already have ETH on Base, you can move to Optimism easily via [Across](https://across.to) or [Relay](https://relay.link).
