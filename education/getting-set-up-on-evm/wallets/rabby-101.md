# Rabby Wallet 101

[Rabby Wallet](https://rabby.io) by DeBank is a browser extension wallet built specifically for experienced EVM users. It is compatible with every EVM chain MetaMask supports, but adds several safety and usability features that make it a popular choice among DeFi and NFT users who want more visibility into what they are signing.

---

## Why Rabby Over MetaMask?

Rabby and MetaMask are both excellent wallets. Here is what makes Rabby stand out:

| Feature | MetaMask | Rabby |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction simulation | No | Yes — shows outcome before you sign |
| Risk scanning | Basic | Detailed (flags suspicious contracts) |
| Chain auto-switching | Manual | Automatic when you visit a dApp |
| Pre-sign approval summary | Basic | Detailed breakdown of what you are approving |
| Hardware wallet support | Yes | Yes |
| Mobile app | Yes | No (browser extension only) |

The **transaction simulation** feature is particularly valuable: Rabby shows you exactly what assets will leave and enter your wallet *before* you confirm a transaction. This is a powerful protection against approve-drain attacks.

---

## Installing Rabby

1. Visit [rabby.io](https://rabby.io) — confirm you are on the official site.
2. Click **"Download for Chrome"** (also supports Brave, Edge, Arc).
3. Add the extension. It will open a setup screen.

> Rabby is available as a browser extension only. For mobile use, MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet are better options.

---

## Setting Up a New Wallet

1. Open the Rabby extension and click **"Create new address"**.
2. Back up your **seed phrase** — write it down on paper and store it offline.
3. Confirm the seed phrase.
4. Your wallet is ready.

---

## Importing an Existing MetaMask Wallet

If you already use MetaMask and want to switch to Rabby:

1. Open Rabby and click **"Import address"**.
2. Select **"Import via seed phrase"** and enter your MetaMask seed phrase.
3. Rabby will import your address and full transaction history.

You can also run both wallets simultaneously — they are independent extensions.

---

## Transaction Simulation

When you interact with any contract, Rabby shows a **"What will happen"** preview before you sign:

- Which tokens or NFTs will leave your wallet
- Which tokens or NFTs will arrive
- Any contract the transaction is interacting with, including risk flags
- Whether the contract is verified and has been audited

If the simulation shows unexpected outflows or flags a risky contract, **reject the transaction and investigate before proceeding**.

---

## Adding Networks

Rabby auto-detects the network a dApp is using and switches automatically. You can also manually browse and add networks from the **"Chain Management"** settings. All major EVM chains (Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism) are pre-configured.

---

## Hardware Wallet Support

Rabby connects to Ledger and Trezor hardware wallets. For any wallet holding significant value, combining Rabby's interface with a hardware wallet's offline key storage is an excellent setup.

Connect via **Settings → Hardware wallet** and follow the pairing steps for your device.

---

## Who Should Use Rabby?

- Anyone who regularly interacts with DeFi, NFT mints, or new contracts
- Users who want more clarity on what they are signing
- Anyone moving up from MetaMask who wants more protection without switching to a completely different workflow

If you are just getting started, [MetaMask](metamask-101.md) is a fine first wallet. Once you are comfortable with how EVM transactions work, Rabby is a meaningful upgrade.
