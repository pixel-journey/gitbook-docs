# Coinbase Wallet 101

[Coinbase Wallet](https://www.coinbase.com/wallet) is a self-custody wallet made by Coinbase, one of the largest and most regulated cryptocurrency exchanges in the world. It is a separate product from the Coinbase exchange app — you do not need a Coinbase account to use it.

Coinbase Wallet is particularly beginner-friendly and offers the smoothest on-ramp experience if you want to buy crypto and move it directly into a self-custody wallet.

---

## Coinbase Wallet vs. Coinbase Exchange App

This distinction confuses many new users:

| | Coinbase (Exchange) | Coinbase Wallet |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Centralized exchange | Self-custody wallet |
| Controls keys? | Coinbase holds keys | You hold keys |
| KYC required? | Yes | No |
| Use with dApps? | No | Yes |
| Good for | Buying crypto with fiat | Interacting with Web3 |

For Pixel Journey, you want **Coinbase Wallet** (self-custody). The exchange app alone cannot connect to NFT marketplaces or dApps.

## Installing Coinbase Wallet

### Browser Extension

1. Visit [coinbase.com/wallet](https://www.coinbase.com/wallet) and click **"Get started"**.
2. Select **"Extension"** for your browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Firefox).
3. Add the extension. It will open the setup screen.

### Mobile App

Available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coinbase-wallet-nft-crypto/id1278383455) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.toshi). The developer is **Coinbase, Inc.**

---

## Creating a New Wallet

1. Open Coinbase Wallet and select **"Create a new wallet"**.
2. Choose whether to use a **recovery phrase** (12 words, standard) or **cloud backup** (backed up to your iCloud/Google account, easier for beginners but less sovereign).

> **For maximum security**, use the recovery phrase option and write it down on paper. Cloud backup is more convenient but means your recovery relies on the security of your cloud account.

3. Set a PIN or biometric unlock.
4. Your wallet is ready. Note your `0x...` address at the top.

---

## Connecting Your Coinbase Account (Optional)

If you have a Coinbase exchange account, you can link it to Coinbase Wallet to:

- Transfer assets from your Coinbase exchange balance directly to your wallet
- Buy crypto and send it straight to your wallet in a few taps

This is optional. The wallet works perfectly fine without a linked exchange account.

---

## Adding Networks

Coinbase Wallet comes pre-configured with Ethereum and Base (naturally, since Base was built by Coinbase). To add other chains:

1. Tap the **network icon** at the top of the app or extension.
2. Select **"Add more networks"** or use the search to find Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, etc.

Alternatively, visit [chainlist.org](https://chainlist.org) — it works with Coinbase Wallet just like MetaMask.

---

## Base Network: Coinbase Wallet's Home Turf

Base is Coinbase's own Ethereum L2, and Coinbase Wallet is deeply integrated with it:

- Free onboarding resources for new Base users
- In-app on-ramp to buy ETH directly onto Base
- Automatic gas fee sponsorship for certain Base transactions when using the Coinbase Wallet app

If your primary EVM interest is Base — which is where a significant part of the Pixel Journey NFT activity happens — Coinbase Wallet is an excellent choice.

---

## Connecting to dApps

1. Visit any EVM dApp and click **"Connect Wallet"**.
2. Select **"Coinbase Wallet"** from the list (or **"Browser Wallet"** if Coinbase Wallet is not listed explicitly).
3. Approve the connection.

---

## Who Should Use Coinbase Wallet?

- Complete beginners to EVM / Web3
- Users who already have crypto on Coinbase exchange and want a quick path to self-custody
- Users primarily focused on Base
- Anyone who wants a polished, intuitive mobile experience

Once you are more comfortable with EVM and want more advanced safety features, consider adding [Rabby](rabby-101.md) as a second option for desktop use.
