# Wallets on EVM

Choosing the right wallet is your first step into the EVM ecosystem. Unlike WAX — where your wallet is often tied to a specific app (Cloud Wallet, Wombat) — EVM wallets are general-purpose tools that connect to any EVM chain and any dApp.

## Which wallet should I use?

| Wallet | Best for | Platform | Custodial? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MetaMask** | Beginners, widest compatibility | Browser + Mobile | No |
| **Rabby** | Power users, extra safety features | Browser | No |
| **Phantom** | Users also on Solana | Browser + Mobile | No |
| **Coinbase Wallet** | Coinbase users, easiest onboarding | Browser + Mobile | No |

All four wallets listed here are **non-custodial** — meaning you hold your own private keys. No company can freeze or access your funds.

> **Coming from WAX?** The biggest adjustment is that your EVM wallet uses a **seed phrase** (12 or 24 random words) as its master backup, not a username/password. Guard this seed phrase with your life. Anyone who has it has full access to your wallet.
