# Phantom Wallet 101

[Phantom](https://phantom.app) started as the leading wallet for the Solana ecosystem, but has since expanded to support Ethereum, Base, Polygon, and other EVM chains. If you plan to explore both the EVM and Solana sides of Web3 — both of which Pixel Journey has a presence on — Phantom lets you manage everything from one wallet.

---

## Installing Phantom

### Browser Extension

1. Visit [phantom.app](https://phantom.app) and click **"Download"**.
2. Select your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge).
3. The extension will install and open automatically.

### Mobile App

Available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/phantom-solana-wallet/id1598432977) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.phantom). Search "Phantom" in the app store and verify the developer is **Phantom Technologies Inc.**

---

## Creating a New Wallet

1. Open Phantom and click **"Create a new wallet"**.
2. Set a password to unlock Phantom on your device.
3. Phantom will generate your **Secret Recovery Phrase** (12 words).
4. Write it down on paper and store it securely offline. This phrase recovers your wallet on any device.
5. Confirm the phrase and your wallet is created.

Your wallet will have addresses on both Solana and Ethereum/EVM chains generated from the same seed phrase. They are separate addresses — your Solana address and your EVM `0x...` address are different.

---

## Switching Between Networks in Phantom

Phantom shows a network switcher at the top of the extension. To switch between Solana and EVM:

1. Click the **network/chain icon** at the top.
2. Select the chain you want — Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Solana, etc.

When on an EVM network, Phantom behaves like any EVM wallet and is compatible with MetaMask-enabled dApps (it shows up as an option in most "Connect Wallet" menus).

---

## Adding EVM Networks

Phantom supports Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, and Optimism out of the box. You can enable or manage which networks are visible via **Settings → Networks**.

---

## Connecting to EVM dApps

Most EVM dApps support Phantom natively. When connecting:

1. Click **"Connect Wallet"** on the site.
2. Look for **Phantom** in the wallet list. If you do not see it, some sites use a generic **"Browser Wallet"** option which also works.
3. Approve the connection in the Phantom popup.

## Using Phantom for Both Solana and EVM

This is Phantom's strongest selling point for Pixel Journey users. You can:

- Hold and manage Solana-based NFTs and tokens
- Hold and manage EVM-based NFTs and tokens (Ethereum, Base, Polygon)
- Swap tokens within Phantom on both ecosystems
- Use the built-in browser to discover dApps across chains

One wallet, multiple ecosystems. As Pixel Journey expands to Solana, having Phantom set up early means you are already prepared.

---

## Importing an Existing Wallet

To import from MetaMask or another EVM wallet:

1. Choose **"I already have a wallet"** during setup.
2. Enter your existing seed phrase.
3. Phantom will derive your EVM address from it.

> Note: If you import a MetaMask seed phrase, your EVM address will match exactly. Your Solana address will be newly derived from the same phrase — there will be no existing Solana history associated with it unless you were already using Phantom.

## Tips

- Phantom has a built-in **NFT viewer** that shows your NFTs from Solana and EVM chains in one place.
- The in-app **swap** function works on both chains — useful for getting small amounts of gas.
- Phantom's mobile app is especially polished for viewing your multi-chain portfolio on the go.
