## What is the Ethereum Virtual Machine?

The EVM is the computing engine that runs smart contracts on Ethereum and every EVM-compatible chain. When a chain says it is "EVM-compatible", it means it runs the same software engine.

**This means:**
- Your wallet works on all of them.
- Your address is the same on all of them.
- The NFT standards are the same.
- Once you understand EVM, you understand the whole family at once.

**Why It Matters for Pixel Journey Explorers**: The EVM is the common language of a huge part of Web3. Mastering it opens doors to deeper liquidity, more advanced mechanics, and future PxPackages-powered experiences across chains.

## Gas Fees: How EVM Transactions Work

Unlike WAX's resource model (CPU/NET/RAM staking), EVM chains use a **gas fee** system:

- Every action on an EVM chain costs **gas** — a small computation fee.
- Gas is paid in the chain's **native token** (ETH on Ethereum/Base/Arbitrum, POL on Polygon, BNB on BNB Chain, etc.).
- Gas prices fluctuate based on network demand. When busy, fees rise. When quiet, fees are very low.
- A fee consists of a **base fee** (burned) and a **priority fee / tip** (paid to validators to process your transaction sooner).

### Layer 1 vs Layer 2

| Type | Examples | Fees | Speed | Security |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Layer 1 (L1)** | Ethereum | Higher | Moderate | Highest (settles on its own chain) |
| **Layer 2 (L2)** | Base, Arbitrum, Optimism | Very low | Fast | Inherited from Ethereum L1 |
| **Sidechain / L2** | Polygon | Very low | Fast | Its own validator set + checkpointing to Ethereum |

L2s batch many transactions together and post a compressed proof back to Ethereum. This is why they can be 10–100x cheaper than mainnet while still benefiting from Ethereum's security.

## Your Wallet Address

On EVM chains, your wallet address looks like this:

```
0x71C7656EC7ab88b098defB751B7401B5f6d8976F
```

It always starts with `0x` followed by 40 hexadecimal characters. **This same address is your identity on every single EVM chain.** Your Ethereum address is also your Base address, your Polygon address, your Arbitrum address, and so on.

> Your address is **public** — anyone can look it up on a blockchain explorer. It does not reveal your identity, but it does show every transaction and holding associated with it. This is normal and expected in Web3.

**Pixel Journey Tip**: Keep your address consistent across EVM chains — it makes bridging and managing assets much simpler.

## Smart Contracts & Token Standards

EVM smart contracts are the foundation of everything:

- **ERC-20** — The standard for fungible tokens (like USDC, MATIC/POL, or any project token).
- **ERC-721** — The standard for unique NFTs. Each token has a unique ID and owner. This is the standard behind most NFT collections.
- **ERC-1155** — A flexible multi-token standard that can represent both fungible and non-fungible tokens in a single contract. Used frequently in gaming and collectibles.

## Connecting Your Wallet to a Chain

Most wallets (MetaMask, Rabby, etc.) come with Ethereum and a few major chains pre-configured. To use a chain that is not pre-loaded:

1. Open your wallet and look for **"Add network"** or **"Networks"**.
2. Enter the chain's **RPC URL, Chain ID, currency symbol, and explorer URL**.
3. Alternatively, visit [chainlist.org](https://chainlist.org) — it lets you add any EVM network in one click.

## NFTs on EVM: What's Different From WAX?

Coming from WAX, there are a few key differences to understand:

| | WAX | EVM (Ethereum / Base / etc.) |
|---|---|---|
| **NFT Standard** | AtomicAssets (custom) | ERC-721 or ERC-1155 |
| **Fees** | Resources (CPU/NET/RAM) | Gas (ETH or native token) |
| **Wallet type** | Account name (e.g. `pixeljourney`) | `0x...` address |
| **Marketplaces** | AtomicHub, NeftyBlocks, NFTHive | OpenSea, Blur, LooksRare |
| **Minting cost** | RAM + minimal fees | Gas fees at mint time |

The biggest practical difference: on EVM chains, you **always need a small amount of the native gas token** in your wallet before you can do anything. Keep a small ETH (or POL/BNB) buffer for gas at all times.

## Bridges: Moving Assets Between Chains

Since Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, and BNB Chain are separate chains, moving assets between them requires a **bridge**:

- [**Across Protocol**](https://across.to) — Fast, low-fee bridging between most major EVM L2s
- [**Base Bridge**](https://bridge.base.org) — Official Coinbase bridge between Ethereum and Base
- [**Polygon Bridge**](https://portal.polygon.technology) — Official Polygon bridge
- [**Arbitrum Bridge**](https://bridge.arbitrum.io) — Official Arbitrum bridge
- [**Hop Protocol**](https://app.hop.exchange) — Multi-chain token bridge
- [**LayerZero / Stargate**](https://stargate.finance) — Cross-chain liquidity protocol

> Bridging NFTs between EVM chains is also possible but less common. The Pixel Journey WAX-to-EVM bridge is handled via the [Cloud Wallet Bridge](../../introduction/wax-ecosystem-guides/cloud-wallet-bridge/).
