# NFT Marketplaces on EVM

EVM chains host the largest NFT ecosystems in Web3. Unlike WAX — where AtomicHub, NeftyBlocks, and NFTHive are the dominant platforms — the EVM landscape has several major marketplaces, each with a different focus and chain presence.

This page covers the platforms most relevant to Pixel Journey users.

---

## OpenSea

[OpenSea](https://opensea.io) is the largest and most recognizable NFT marketplace in the world. It supports Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, and many other chains from a single interface.

### Getting Started on OpenSea

1. Visit [opensea.io](https://opensea.io).
2. Click **"Connect wallet"** (top right) and select your wallet (MetaMask, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, etc.).
3. Select the network you want to browse using the chain filter.
4. To buy an NFT: find the item, click it, and click **"Buy now"** or make an offer.
5. Confirm the transaction in your wallet popup.

### Personalizing Your OpenSea Profile

After connecting:
1. Click your profile icon → **"Settings"**.
2. Add a username, bio, and profile image.
3. Add links to Twitter/X or an external site.
4. Your profile page shows all NFTs you hold across connected chains.

## Blur

[Blur](https://blur.io) is a professional-grade NFT marketplace built primarily for traders and power users on Ethereum and Base. It offers:

- **0% marketplace fees** (sellers keep the full sale price)
- **Real-time floor price tracking** and portfolio analytics
- **Bidding pools** — you can place bids across an entire collection at once
- **Blend** — NFT-backed lending protocol for leveraged NFT positions

Blur is most relevant if you are actively trading higher-value NFTs and want the best execution. For casual users and beginners, OpenSea is more intuitive.

- [Blur (Ethereum)](https://blur.io)
- [Blur (Base)](https://blur.io/base)

## Zora

[Zora](https://zora.co) is a creator-focused marketplace and protocol built on its own chain (also OP Stack) with support for Ethereum, Base, Optimism, and Zora Network. It is known for:

- **Free minting**: Many Zora collections are free to mint (you only pay gas).
- **Creator-first economics**: More revenue goes to creators than on traditional marketplaces.
- **Open editions**: Time-limited mints where anyone can mint during a set window.

Zora is a great place to discover indie creators and generative art across the EVM space.

- [Zora](https://zora.co)

## Tips for Buying NFTs on EVM

- Always verify the collection address before buying. Search for the official contract address from the project's verified website or Twitter/X — do not trust addresses posted in Discord or chats.
- Check the **floor price** and **volume** to understand a collection's liquidity. A collection with very low volume may be hard to sell later.
- On Ethereum mainnet, time your transactions for off-peak hours (early morning UTC) when gas fees are lower.
- Use [revoke.cash](https://revoke.cash) periodically to audit and clean up any marketplace approvals you have given — especially if you have used multiple platforms.
- For Pixel Journey NFTs specifically, check OpenSea on Base and Polygon first — that is where our primary EVM activity is concentrated.
