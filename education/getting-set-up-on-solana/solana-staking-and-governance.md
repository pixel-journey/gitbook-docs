# Solana Staking & Governance — Earn Rewards & Participate in the Ecosystem

**Staking SOL** is one of the simplest and most reliable ways to earn passive rewards on Solana while supporting the network's security and decentralization. Governance participation allows token holders to influence protocol decisions. This guide covers staking options, risks, and how it ties into your Pixel Journey multi-chain activities.

> **Safety First**: Use reputable validators and official tools. Never share seed phrases. DYOR on validators and governance proposals. Start small and monitor performance. Staking involves risks like slashing (rare on Solana) or opportunity cost.

## Why Staking & Governance Matter for Pixel Journey Users

- **Passive Yield**: Earn ~6-8% APY on SOL (varies) with low effort, complementing your WAX staking or DeFi positions.
- **Network Support**: Helps secure Solana, benefiting the ecosystem where you may bridge assets or explore NFTs/DeFi.
- **Governance Voice**: Participate in decisions for protocols like Jupiter, Raydium, or Solana itself via tokens or DAOs.
- **Liquidity Options**: Use liquid staking (e.g., JitoSOL) to earn rewards while keeping assets liquid for trading or DeFi.

**Pixel Journey Perspective**: Stake bridged SOL or ecosystem tokens for yield while keeping your core Pixal collection and economy on WAX. Use governance to support projects that align with community values. Combine with bridges for cross-chain yield strategies.

## Staking SOL on Solana

### Native Staking via Wallets
- **Phantom, Solflare, Backpack**: Built-in staking interfaces. Choose validators, delegate SOL, and earn rewards.
- Rewards are auto-compounded or claimable; APY fluctuates based on network conditions.
- **Steps**:
  1. Connect wallet with SOL.
  2. Select a validator (check commission, uptime, and community reputation via explorers or dashboards).
  3. Delegate and confirm transaction.
  4. Monitor rewards and performance in wallet or explorers like Solscan.

### Liquid Staking (Recommended for Flexibility)
- **Jito (JitoSOL)**: Stake SOL to receive JitoSOL, which earns staking rewards + MEV tips. Trade or use JitoSOL in DeFi (lending, liquidity) while earning.
- **Other**: Marinade (mSOL), etc. Check for fees, risks, and liquidity.
- **Benefits**: Maintain liquidity for swaps, DeFi, or bridging back to WAX/EVM.

## Governance Participation

- **Token-Based**: Hold governance tokens (e.g., JTO for Jupiter, RAY for Raydium) to vote on proposals.
- **DAOs**: Participate in Solana ecosystem DAOs via tools like Realms or Squads.
- **Steps**:
  1. Acquire governance tokens via Jupiter or DEXes.
  2. Connect to governance portal.
  3. Review proposals, discuss in communities, and vote.
  4. Stake tokens if required for voting power.

## Safety, DYOR & Best Practices
- **Validator Selection**: Choose low-commission, high-uptime validators with good reputation. Use tools like Stakeview or explorers to research.
- **Risks**: Slashing is rare but possible for validator misbehavior. Impermanent loss or smart contract risks in liquid staking/DeFi.
- **DYOR**: Research APYs, fees, and risks. Monitor via Solscan or dashboards.
- **Security**: Use hardware wallets for large stakes. Revoke approvals. Verify dApp URLs.
- **Diversify**: Don't stake all assets in one validator or protocol.
