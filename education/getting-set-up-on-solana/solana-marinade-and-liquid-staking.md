# Marinade & Liquid Staking on Solana — Earn Rewards While Staying Liquid

**Marinade** is Solana's leading liquid staking protocol, allowing users to stake SOL for mSOL (Marinade SOL) while keeping it liquid for DeFi use. This guide covers advanced features, with safety emphasis and Pixel Journey relevance.

> **Safety First**: Always use the official Marinade site (marinade.finance). Verify URLs. DYOR on strategies. Start small. Monitor for smart contract risks and market volatility. Use transaction simulations.

## Key Advanced Features

### Liquid Staking (mSOL)
- Stake SOL to receive mSOL, which earns staking rewards automatically.
- mSOL can be used in DeFi (lending, liquidity pools, swaps) while continuing to earn rewards.
- Higher APY than native staking due to optimized validator selection and MEV.

### Advanced Strategies
- Use mSOL in liquidity pools on Orca/Raydium for additional yield.
- Integrate with lending protocols like Kamino or Solend for leveraged positions.
- Auto-compounding and rebalancing features in partnered vaults.

### Governance & Rewards
- Participate in Marinade governance with MNDE tokens.
- Earn additional rewards through partnerships and incentives.

## Getting Started with Marinade
1. Connect your Solana wallet.
2. Visit marinade.finance and stake SOL for mSOL.
3. Use mSOL in DeFi platforms for extra yield.
4. Monitor performance via dashboards or portfolio tools.
5. Unstake anytime by swapping mSOL back to SOL.
