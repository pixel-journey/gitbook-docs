# How to Get SOL

Before you can transact on Solana — buy an NFT, interact with a DeFi protocol, or even receive a new type of token — you need SOL in your wallet. Fortunately, SOL is widely available and a small amount goes a long way given Solana's near-zero fees.

---

## How Much SOL Do You Need to Start?

A starting buffer of **0.1–0.5 SOL** is more than enough to cover:

- Dozens of transaction fees
- Rent deposits for new token accounts (~0.002 SOL each)
- A few NFT purchases (plus the NFT price itself)

Unlike Ethereum mainnet where gas fees can be $5–$50+ per transaction, Solana fees are fractions of a cent. You will rarely notice them in practice.

---

## Option 1: Buy on a Centralized Exchange (Most Common)

SOL is one of the top 5 cryptocurrencies by market cap and is listed on every major exchange:

| Exchange | Available In | Direct Solana Withdrawal? |
|---|---|---|
| [Coinbase](https://coinbase.com) | US, EU, UK, + more | Yes |
| [Binance](https://binance.com) | Most regions (not US) | Yes |
| [Kraken](https://kraken.com) | US, EU | Yes |
| [OKX](https://okx.com) | Most regions | Yes |
| [Bybit](https://bybit.com) | Most regions | Yes |

**Steps:**
1. Create an account and complete KYC.
2. Deposit fiat (bank transfer or card).
3. Buy SOL.
4. Withdraw to your Solana wallet address (from Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack).

When withdrawing, **always select the Solana network** on the exchange withdrawal screen. SOL sent on the wrong network is not recoverable without the exchange's support team.

---

## Option 2: In-Wallet Fiat On-Ramp

Phantom and Solflare both have built-in "Buy" buttons powered by fiat on-ramp providers:

- **Phantom**: Tap "Buy" → choose MoonPay, Coinbase Pay, or Transak → buy SOL with a card or bank transfer
- **Solflare**: Similar flow with MoonPay integration

This is the most convenient option for beginners — SOL lands directly in your wallet with no withdrawal step needed. The trade-off is that on-ramp fees (typically 2–5%) are higher than buying on an exchange.

---

## Option 3: Swap From Another Crypto

If you already hold ETH, USDC, or another token on an EVM chain, you can bridge or swap it to SOL:

### Bridge from EVM to Solana

Use a cross-chain bridge that supports Solana:

| Bridge | Supports |
|---|---|
| [Allbridge](https://app.allbridge.io) | ETH, Base, Polygon, BSC → Solana |
| [deBridge](https://app.debridge.finance) | Most major EVM chains → Solana |
| [Wormhole Portal](https://portalbridge.com) | Wide chain support including Solana |
| [Mayan Finance](https://swap.mayan.finance) | Fast cross-chain swaps to Solana |

These bridges convert your EVM tokens (ETH, USDC, etc.) into SOL or Solana-native tokens in one transaction.

### Swap Within Phantom

If you have tokens in Phantom on an EVM chain, use the built-in Phantom swap to move between assets. Switch to Solana in Phantom and use the "Swap" function powered by [Jupiter](https://jup.ag) to swap any Solana token for SOL.

## Option 4: No-KYC Options

If you prefer not to use a centralized exchange:

| Service | What it does |
|---|---|
| [SimpleSwap](https://simpleswap.io) | Swap WAX, ETH, or other crypto for SOL — no account required |
| [ChangeNOW](https://changenow.io) | No-KYC crypto swap with wide support |
| [Jupiter](https://jup.ag) | Best DEX aggregator on Solana — requires existing SOL balance for first use |

For complete beginners with no existing crypto, an exchange (Option 1) or wallet on-ramp (Option 2) remains the most accessible route.

## Coming From WAX?

If you hold WAXP and want to fund a Solana wallet:

1. Find an exchange listing both WAXP and SOL — check [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/wax/markets/).
2. Sell WAXP for USDC or BTC, then buy SOL on the same exchange.
3. Withdraw SOL to your Solana wallet address.
4. Alternatively, use [SimpleSwap](https://simpleswap.io) to swap WAXP → SOL directly with no account needed.

---

## Receiving SOL From Someone Else

If a friend or project is sending you SOL:

1. Open your Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack).
2. Make sure you are on the **Solana** network.
3. Copy your Solana address — it will be a long base58 string, not a `0x...` EVM address.
4. Share this address with the sender.

Always double-check you are sharing the correct Solana address and not an EVM address. They look different and should never be confused.

---

## Tips

- Keep at least 0.01 SOL in your wallet at all times as a minimum gas buffer. Running out of SOL means you cannot send any transactions — including transactions to get more SOL.
- Your first SOL purchase does not need to be large. 0.5 SOL is plenty to explore the ecosystem, cover fees, and buy a few NFTs before you decide how much further to commit.
- Track SOL prices on [CoinGecko](https://coingecko.com/en/coins/solana) or [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/solana/).
