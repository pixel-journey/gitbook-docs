# Solana Governance & DAO Tools — Participate in Ecosystem Decisions

**Governance on Solana** allows token holders to influence protocol development, treasury management, and upgrades through DAOs and voting mechanisms. This guide covers key tools and best practices, with safety emphasis and Pixel Journey relevance.

> **Safety First**: Always use official governance portals. Verify URLs and proposals. DYOR on proposals before voting. Never share private keys or sign suspicious transactions. Use hardware wallets for significant voting power.

## Key Governance & DAO Tools

### Realms (by Solana Foundation)
- The primary DAO framework on Solana.
- Create or join DAOs for protocols like Jupiter, Raydium, or community projects.
- Features: Proposal creation, voting, treasury management, and member management.
- Supports SPL governance tokens for weighted voting.

### Squads Protocol
- Multi-sig and DAO tooling for teams and communities.
- Secure transaction execution and proposal management.
- Useful for Pixel Journey community or partner DAOs.

### Other Tools
- **Token Voting**: Many protocols use their native tokens (e.g., JTO for Jupiter, RAY for Raydium) for governance.
- **Snapshot**: Off-chain voting for some communities (though Solana favors on-chain).
- **Integrations**: Link with wallets like Phantom for seamless voting.

## Getting Started with Governance
1. Acquire governance tokens (e.g., via Jupiter swaps).
2. Connect wallet to Realms or protocol-specific portals.
3. Review active proposals, discuss in communities, and vote.
4. For DAOs: Delegate voting power or create proposals if eligible.
5. Monitor outcomes and participate in discussions on Discord/Forums.

## Pixel Journey Relevance
- Participate in governance for protocols we use (Jupiter, Raydium, Kamino) to support features that benefit multi-chain users.
- Use DAO tools for community initiatives or future PxDAO-like structures.
- Combine with staking for governance power (e.g., staked tokens may grant voting rights).
- Stay informed on ecosystem changes that could affect bridging, DeFi, or NFT tools.

## Best Practices & DYOR
- **Research Proposals**: Read details, consider impacts on fees, security, or tokenomics.
- **Vote Wisely**: Align with long-term ecosystem health over short-term gains.
- **Security**: Use hardware wallets and verify all transactions.
- **Avoid Scams**: Ignore fake governance sites or phishing attempts.
- **Stay Updated**: Follow official channels and our Discord for relevant proposals.

**Pro Tip**: Start by voting on familiar protocols like Jupiter to build experience. Governance participation strengthens the ecosystem we rely on for Solana activities. Always prioritize security and DYOR — your vote shapes the future of tools we use alongside WAX.
