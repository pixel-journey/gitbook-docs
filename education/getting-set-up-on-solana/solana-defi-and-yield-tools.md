# Solana DeFi & Yield Tools — Swaps, Liquidity, Lending & Farming

**Solana DeFi** is one of the most vibrant and efficient ecosystems in Web3, powered by near-zero fees and sub-second transactions. Beyond basic swapping on Jupiter, there are powerful tools for providing liquidity, lending, borrowing, and yield farming that can complement your Pixel Journey activities — whether experimenting with bridged assets or exploring new opportunities alongside your WAX collection.

> **Safety First**: DeFi involves risks like impermanent loss, smart contract vulnerabilities, and market volatility. Always DYOR, start with small amounts, use transaction simulations, and only risk what you can afford to lose. Verify all protocols and contracts from official sources.

## Key DeFi Categories & Tools

### Swapping & Aggregation
- **Jupiter**: The gold standard aggregator (see dedicated advanced features guide for limit orders, DCA, and perps).
- Routes across Raydium, Orca, Meteora, and more for best prices and lowest slippage.

### Liquidity Provision (AMM)
- **Raydium**: Major DEX with concentrated liquidity (CLMM) pools. Provide liquidity to earn trading fees + rewards.
- **Orca**: User-friendly with Whirlpools (concentrated liquidity) for efficient capital use.
- **Meteora**: Dynamic liquidity pools (DLMM) popular for volatile pairs and new launches.
- **Tips**: Choose stable or correlated pairs to minimize impermanent loss. Monitor APYs and rewards via dashboards.

### Lending & Borrowing
- **Kamino Finance** and **Solend**: Leading lending protocols. Supply assets to earn interest or borrow against collateral.
- Useful for leveraged strategies or earning yield on idle assets (e.g., bridged USDC or SOL).
- **Risks**: Liquidation thresholds, interest rate fluctuations — use health factors and monitor positions.

### Yield Farming & Strategies
- **Farm on Raydium or Orca**: Stake LP tokens for extra token rewards (often high APYs but with risks).
- **Kamino**: Automated vaults for optimized yield strategies across lending and liquidity.
- **Jito**: Liquid staking for SOL (JitoSOL) to earn staking rewards + MEV tips while remaining liquid.
- **Other**: Emerging protocols for leveraged farming or options — approach with caution and thorough research.

## Getting Started with DeFi on Solana
1. Connect a secure wallet (Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack with Ledger for larger amounts).
2. Acquire SOL or bridged tokens (via Jupiter or bridges from WAX/EVM).
3. Start simple: Swap on Jupiter, then try providing liquidity on Raydium or Orca.
4. Explore lending on Solend/Kamino for passive yield.
5. Use dashboards like Step Finance, Birdeye, or DefiLlama (Solana section) to track positions, APYs, and risks.
6. Enable priority fees during congestion and always preview transactions.
