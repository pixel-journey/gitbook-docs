## Key Advanced Features

### Real-Time Analytics & Data
- Live floor prices, bid depth, recent sales, and collection analytics.
- Rarity tools, trait filters, and historical data for informed decisions.
- Portfolio tracking and performance metrics directly in the platform.

### AMM Liquidity Pools (TensorSwap)
- Provide liquidity to NFT collections and earn fees (similar to DEX AMMs but for NFTs).
- Dynamic pricing and concentrated liquidity options.
- Earn trading fees on your provided NFTs or tokens.
- **Risks**: Impermanent loss, smart contract risks — monitor positions and start with small amounts.

### Bulk Operations & Efficiency
- Buy/sell multiple NFTs in a single transaction (bulk buy/sell).
- List entire collections at once.
- Advanced order types and sniping tools for active traders.

### Compressed NFTs (cNFTs)
- Native support for ultra-low cost minting and trading of compressed NFTs.
- Ideal for large-scale or high-volume NFT projects.
- Lower fees and better scalability than standard NFTs.

### Governance & Rewards
- TNSR token for governance and trading rewards.
- Incentives for active users and liquidity providers.
