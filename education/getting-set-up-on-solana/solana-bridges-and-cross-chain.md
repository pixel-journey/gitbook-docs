# Solana Bridges & Cross-Chain — Securely Connect Solana to WAX, EVM & Beyond

Bridges allow you to move assets (SOL, tokens, NFTs) between Solana and other blockchains like WAX, Ethereum L2s (Base, Arbitrum), or others. While Solana's speed and low fees make it excellent for on-chain activity, bridging opens up cross-ecosystem opportunities for Pixel Journey users — such as accessing deeper liquidity, new NFT communities, or DeFi yields.

**Important Safety Note**: Bridges are one of the highest-risk areas in Web3. Many major exploits have targeted bridge contracts. Always use official or well-audited bridges, start with tiny test amounts, and only bridge what you can afford to lose. DYOR thoroughly on the specific bridge's security model, audits, and recent incidents.

## Why Bridges Matter for Pixel Journey

- Move assets from WAX to Solana for cost-effective trading, new NFT discoveries, or experimenting with Solana's vibrant ecosystem.
- Bridge from EVM chains to Solana for faster/cheaper interactions or unique opportunities.
- Participate in multi-chain events, airdrops, or communities while keeping your core Pixal collection and economy on WAX.
- Prepare for future PxPortals or cross-chain features.

## Recommended Bridges

### Official & Trusted Options
- **Wormhole** — Widely used for Solana ↔ EVM (Ethereum, Base, Polygon, etc.) and other chains. Supports tokens and some NFTs. Official portal: wormhole.com or app.wormhole.com.
- **Allbridge** — Good for Solana ↔ EVM and other ecosystems; check for current supported routes.
- **Portal Bridge (Wormhole)** — User-friendly interface for many transfers.

### Chain-Specific or Aggregators
- Use official chain bridges where available (e.g., for Base or Arbitrum) combined with Solana endpoints.
- Aggregators or multi-hop tools can simplify routes but add complexity and risk — stick to simple, direct official paths when possible.

## Getting Started
1. Choose source and destination chains (e.g., WAX → Solana or Base → Solana).
2. Visit the official bridge portal and connect your wallet (Phantom/Solflare for Solana side; MetaMask/Rabby for EVM).
3. Select assets to bridge, review fees, estimated time, and any lock-up periods.
4. Approve and confirm transactions on both sides.
5. Verify arrival on the destination explorer (Solscan or Solana Explorer for Solana side).

**Note on Timing & Costs**: Solana side is usually fast and cheap. EVM side may have higher gas or challenge periods (e.g., ~7 days for some optimistic rollups). Always check current conditions.

## Safety Best Practices
- **Verify everything**: Use only official links (bookmark them). Double-check contract addresses and URLs.
- **Start tiny**: Send a very small test amount first to confirm the flow works.
- **Understand the model**: Some bridges are liquidity-based, others use lock-and-mint or burn-and-mint. Know the risks.
- **NFTs**: Not all bridges support every NFT standard; test with low-value items. Compressed NFTs (cNFTs) on Solana may have specific requirements.
- **Revoke approvals**: After bridging, revoke any unnecessary token approvals.
- **Monitor & DYOR**: Use explorers to track transactions. Research the bridge's team, audits (e.g., via Immunefi or official reports), and any past incidents.
- **Avoid hype**: New or unverified bridges often come with higher risk.
