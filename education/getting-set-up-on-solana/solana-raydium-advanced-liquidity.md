## Key Advanced Features

### Concentrated Liquidity (CLMM)
- Provide liquidity within specific price ranges for higher capital efficiency and yields vs. traditional AMMs.
- Narrow ranges for higher fees but require active management.
- Earn trading fees + farm rewards.

### Farms & Yield
- Stake LP tokens in farms for extra RAY rewards (or other tokens).
- High APYs possible but with risks like impermanent loss and reward volatility.
- Check current farms and multipliers on the site.

### AcceleRaytor Launchpad
- Participate in IDOs for new Solana projects.
- DYOR heavily on teams, utility, and tokenomics — many launches are high-risk.
- Use with caution; start small.

### Other Tools
- Analytics for pools, volumes, and performance.
- Integration with Jupiter for best routing.
- Support for various token pairs and strategies.

## Getting Started with Advanced Raydium
1. Connect your Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack recommended).
2. Explore CLMM pools and select suitable pairs (stable or correlated for less IL).
3. Provide liquidity by setting price range and amounts.
4. Stake LP tokens in farms if desired.
5. Monitor positions via dashboards or explorers; rebalance as needed.
6. For launches: Research thoroughly and use small allocations.

## Best Practices & DYOR
- **Impermanent Loss**: Narrow ranges increase fees but risk IL; choose wisely and monitor.
- **Active Management**: Rebalance CLMM positions; track farm rewards.
- **Launchpad Risks**: High volatility and potential rugs — thorough DYOR essential.
- **Security**: Use hardware wallet for significant positions. Revoke approvals. Verify contracts.
- **Diversify**: Don't put all in one pool or farm.
- **Tools**: Cross-reference with DefiLlama, Step Finance, or Birdeye for data.
