# Jupiter Advanced Features on Solana — Power User Tools for Swapping, DCA, Perps & More

**Jupiter** (jup.ag) is the leading DEX aggregator on Solana, and its advanced features go far beyond basic swapping. For Pixel Journey users exploring Solana's fast, low-cost environment, mastering these tools can unlock efficient trading, automated strategies, and leveraged opportunities while keeping everything self-custodial.

> **Safety First**: Always use the official site (jup.ag). Bookmark it. Enable transaction simulations in your wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack). Start with small amounts when trying new features. DYOR on any strategy involving leverage or automation.

## Key Advanced Features

### 1. Limit Orders
- Set a specific price at which you want to buy or sell a token.
- Jupiter monitors the market and executes automatically when conditions are met.
- Useful for disciplined trading without constant monitoring.
- Access via the Limit tab on jup.ag.

### 2. Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA)
- Automate recurring purchases of a token over time (e.g., buy $PXJ or SOL weekly).
- Choose interval (daily, weekly, etc.) and amount.
- Reduces the impact of volatility by spreading buys.
- Great for long-term accumulation strategies alongside your WAX holdings.

### 3. Perpetuals (Perps) Trading
- Leveraged trading up to high multiples on major pairs.
- Cross-margin and isolated options.
- High risk — only use with capital you can afford to lose and proper risk management (stop-loss, position sizing).
- Pixel Journey tip: Treat as advanced experimentation, not core strategy. Start with demo-like small positions.

### 4. Routing & Best Price Aggregation
- Jupiter routes your swap across multiple liquidity sources (Raydium, Orca, Meteora, etc.) for the best price and lowest slippage.
- Real-time price impact and route preview before confirming.
- Supports limit orders and DCA routing as well.

### 5. Token Launch & New Pairs
- Discover and trade newly launched tokens quickly.
- Use with caution — new tokens carry high rug/pull risks. Always check with RugCheck or similar tools.

## Getting Started with Advanced Features
1. Go to [jup.ag](https://jup.ag) and connect your Solana wallet.
2. Switch to the desired tab (Swap, Limit, DCA, Perps).
3. Select tokens and parameters.
4. Review the preview, fees, and impact.
5. Confirm in wallet — enable priority fees for faster execution during busy times.
