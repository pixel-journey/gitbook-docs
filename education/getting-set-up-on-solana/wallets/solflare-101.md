# Solflare Wallet 101

[Solflare](https://solflare.com) is a Solana-native wallet built specifically for the Solana ecosystem. While Phantom covers both Solana and EVM, Solflare goes deeper on Solana-specific features — particularly staking, validator selection, and token management. It is the preferred wallet for users who are primarily focused on Solana.

---

## Installing Solflare

### Browser Extension

1. Visit [solflare.com](https://solflare.com) and click **"Download Extension"**.
2. Add the extension for your browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Firefox).
3. The extension opens the setup screen automatically.

### Mobile App

Available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/solflare/id1580902717) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.solflare.mobile). The developer is **Solflare**.

---

## Creating a New Wallet

1. Open Solflare and select **"Create a new wallet"**.
2. Solflare generates your **Secret Recovery Phrase** — 24 words by default (more secure than 12).

{% hint style="danger" %}
Write the full 24-word phrase on paper and store it offline. Never photograph it or save it digitally. Anyone with this phrase has permanent, irrevocable access to your wallet.
{% endhint %}

3. Confirm the phrase by selecting the words in order.
4. Set a password and your wallet is ready.

---

## Importing an Existing Wallet

To restore from a seed phrase (12 or 24 words):

1. Click **"Access existing wallet"**.
2. Select **"Mnemonic phrase"** and enter your phrase.
3. Set a password.

Solflare also supports importing from a **private key** or connecting a **Ledger hardware wallet** during setup.

## Staking SOL

Solflare has the most feature-rich native staking experience of any Solana wallet:

1. Go to the **"Staking"** tab.
2. Click **"Start Staking"**.
3. Choose a validator from the list. Look for validators with:
   - **High uptime** (99%+)
   - **Reasonable commission** (5–10% is typical)
   - A history of participating in governance
4. Enter the amount of SOL to stake and confirm.

Staking rewards currently run around 6–8% APY and are distributed at the end of each epoch (approximately every 2–3 days). Staked SOL takes one epoch to become active and one epoch to become unstaked after you withdraw.

> Staking on Solana is non-custodial. You are delegating your SOL to a validator — you retain ownership and can unstake at any time. Your SOL never leaves your wallet.

---

## Connecting to Solana dApps

1. Visit any Solana dApp and click **"Connect Wallet"**.
2. Select **Solflare** from the wallet options.
3. Approve the connection in the Solflare popup.

---

## Token & NFT Management

- **Tokens**: The main screen shows your SOL balance and all SPL token holdings.
- **NFTs**: The "Collectibles" tab displays all Solana NFTs in your wallet.
- **Burn spam**: Solflare has a built-in tool to burn unwanted or spam NFTs and reclaim the rent deposit — useful for clearing airdropped junk from your wallet.

---

## Hardware Wallet Support

Solflare is one of the best wallets for using a Ledger with Solana. Connect via:

1. Settings → **"Hardware wallet"**
2. Select Ledger and follow the pairing steps.

All transactions will require physical confirmation on the Ledger device — a significant security upgrade for holding valuable assets.

---

## Who Should Use Solflare?

- Users focused on Solana who want deep staking features and validator visibility
- Anyone who wants to reclaim rent from spam/junk NFTs
- Power users who want granular SPL token management
- Ledger hardware wallet users on Solana

If you are also active on EVM chains, consider using [Phantom](phantom-101.md) alongside Solflare — or use Phantom as your primary wallet and Solflare as your dedicated Solana staking interface.
