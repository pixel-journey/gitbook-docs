# Backpack Wallet 101

[Backpack](https://backpack.app) is a Solana wallet built by Coral, the team behind the **xNFT** standard — executable NFTs that run applications directly inside your wallet. It supports standard Solana functionality (SOL, SPL tokens, NFTs) alongside a unique ecosystem of xNFT-powered apps.

Backpack is geared toward power users and early adopters who want access to the cutting edge of Solana. For beginners, [Phantom](phantom-101.md) is easier to start with.

---

## Installing Backpack

### Browser Extension

1. Visit [backpack.app](https://backpack.app) and click **"Download"**.
2. Install the extension for Chrome or Brave.
3. The extension opens the setup screen.

### Mobile App

Available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/backpack-crypto-wallet-app/id6445964121) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.backpack.mobile). The developer is **Coral**.

---

## Creating a New Wallet

1. Open Backpack and click **"Create a new account"**.
2. Set a username — Backpack uses human-readable usernames as an on-chain identity layer.
3. Back up your **Secret Recovery Phrase** (12 words). Write it on paper, store it offline.
4. Confirm the phrase.
5. Your wallet is ready.

---

## xNFTs: Apps Inside Your Wallet

The defining feature of Backpack is **xNFTs** (executable NFTs). An xNFT is a token that also contains a runnable application. When you hold an xNFT in your Backpack wallet, its app is accessible directly from the "Apps" tab — no browser required.

Examples include:
- Trading interfaces that run inside your wallet
- Games playable directly from Backpack
- Portfolio dashboards and analytics tools

As the xNFT ecosystem grows, Backpack becomes more powerful for users who hold and use them.

---

## Standard Solana Features

Beyond xNFTs, Backpack handles all standard Solana operations:

- **SOL and SPL token management**: View balances, send and receive tokens.
- **NFT viewer**: Browse all Solana NFTs in your wallet.
- **dApp connections**: Connect to any Solana dApp the same way you would with Phantom or Solflare — click "Connect Wallet" and select Backpack.

---

## Usernames and the Backpack Identity System

Backpack assigns on-chain usernames (e.g. `@yourname`) to wallets. This means you can send SOL to a human-readable name instead of a long address — as long as the recipient also uses Backpack.

This is optional and does not affect compatibility with other wallets.

---

## Who Should Use Backpack?

- Developers and early adopters interested in the xNFT ecosystem
- Users who want on-chain identity via Backpack usernames
- Collectors of xNFT-specific applications and experiences
- Anyone who wants to explore the leading edge of the Solana application layer

For everyday NFT trading, minting, and DeFi on Solana, [Phantom](phantom-101.md) or [Solflare](solflare-101.md) remain the more established choices. Backpack is best as a second wallet for users who want access to its unique features.
