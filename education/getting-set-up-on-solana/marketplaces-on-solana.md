# NFT Marketplaces on Solana

The Solana NFT ecosystem is large, fast-moving, and competitive. Unlike WAX — where AtomicHub, NeftyBlocks, and NFTHive are the clear leaders — Solana has two primary marketplaces (Magic Eden and Tensor) and a broader ecosystem of tools for power users and traders.

---

## Magic Eden

[Magic Eden](https://magiceden.io) is the largest multi-chain NFT marketplace, and it started on Solana. It remains the most popular and widely used destination for Solana NFT trading, with the broadest catalogue of collections, the cleanest interface for beginners, and the highest volume.

### What Magic Eden Offers

- **Browse and buy**: Search any collection, filter by traits, and buy at floor or below.
- **List for sale**: List your NFTs in seconds — select the item, set a price, approve the transaction.
- **Offers and bidding**: Place bids on individual NFTs or collection-wide offers (bid on any NFT in a collection meeting your price criteria).
- **Launchpad**: New Solana NFT projects often launch through Magic Eden's launchpad — upcoming mints are listed here.
- **Multi-chain**: Magic Eden also supports Ethereum, Base, Polygon, and Bitcoin Ordinals — your one account works across all chains.

### Getting Started on Magic Eden

1. Visit [magiceden.io](https://magiceden.io).
2. Click **"Connect Wallet"** and select Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack.
3. Browse collections using the top navigation or search bar.
4. To buy: open an NFT listing, click **"Buy Now"**, and confirm the transaction in your wallet.
5. To sell: go to your profile → "My Items" → select an NFT → "List for Sale" → set your price.

### Fees

Magic Eden charges a **2% marketplace fee** on sales. Creator royalties are optional (the platform moved to optional royalties in 2022) — you can set them when listing, but they are not enforced.

## Tensor

[Tensor](https://tensor.trade) is a professional-grade Solana NFT marketplace built for traders. It is to Solana what Blur is to Ethereum — designed for speed, data, and execution rather than browsing.

### What Makes Tensor Different

- **Real-time floor data**: Live floor price charts, bid depth, and collection analytics.
- **AMM liquidity pools (TensorSwap)**: Provide liquidity to NFT collections and earn fees — similar to a DEX but for NFTs.
- **Compressed NFTs (cNFTs)**: Native support for Solana's compressed NFT standard, which allows minting millions of NFTs at negligible cost.
- **Bulk operations**: Buy multiple NFTs in a single transaction, or list your entire collection at once.
- **TNSR governance token**: Tensor has a native governance token with trading rewards for active marketplace users.

Tensor is more data-dense than Magic Eden and has a steeper learning curve. For everyday buying and selling, either platform works well — but active traders tend to gravitate toward Tensor for its analytics and execution tools.

### Getting Started on Tensor

1. Visit [tensor.trade](https://tensor.trade).
2. Connect your wallet.
3. Search for a collection using the search bar.
4. The collection page shows real-time listings, recent sales, floor price charts, and bid depth — more information than Magic Eden by default.
5. Buy at floor, place a collection bid, or provide liquidity to the collection's AMM pool.

## Comparing Magic Eden and Tensor

| | Magic Eden | Tensor |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Beginners, general browsing | Traders, analytics, bulk operations |
| Interface | Clean and accessible | Data-dense, power user |
| Fees | 2% | 1.5% |
| Bid / offer system | Collection-wide offers | Collection bids + AMM pools |
| Compressed NFT support | Yes | Yes (native) |
| Token rewards | ME token (points) | TNSR governance token |
| Multi-chain | Yes (ETH, Base, Polygon + more) | Primarily Solana |

## Other Platforms Worth Knowing

### Exchange Art

[Exchange.art](https://exchange.art) is a curated marketplace focused on fine digital art and 1-of-1 works on Solana. If you are interested in the artistic side of the Solana ecosystem rather than PFPs or trading-focused collections, this is the platform to explore.

### Solanart

[Solanart](https://solanart.io) was one of the first Solana NFT marketplaces and remains active, though volume has largely shifted to Magic Eden and Tensor.

## Tips for Buying NFTs on Solana

- Verify the collection address before buying. Every project should have its official contract address listed on their verified Twitter/X. On Magic Eden and Tensor, also check the **verified checkmark** on the collection page.
- Check **recent sales** and **floor price** before buying. Solana NFT prices can be highly volatile.
- Watch for **compressed NFTs (cNFTs)** — these are a newer format with even lower minting costs. They are fully tradable on both Magic Eden and Tensor but stored differently on-chain.
- Use [HowRare](https://howrare.is) or [Tensor's rarity tool](https://tensor.trade) to check trait rarity within a collection before paying a premium for specific traits.
- Be cautious of NFTs listed far below floor with urgency messaging — verify on the official marketplace rather than clicking links in chats.
