## What Makes Base Special

Base is an Ethereum Layer 2 (L2) blockchain backed by Coinbase, one of the biggest and most trusted names in crypto. This gives it a solid, secure foundation from day one.

**Key Advantages:**
- **EVM Compatible**: Built on Ethereum, so it works seamlessly with the tools, wallets, and projects we already know and love (MetaMask, etc.).
- **Developer-Friendly**: Designed for easy building, which means a growing wave of innovative dApps, games, and NFT projects.
- **Low Fees + High Speed**: Transactions are fast and cost a fraction of mainnet Ethereum — often just a few cents or less. More fun, less friction!
- **Coinbase Backing**: Strong security, liquidity, and mainstream adoption potential.

For NFT collectors and traders, this means:
- Minting and trading feels smooth and affordable.
- On-chain experiences (gaming, interactive collectibles) become practical.
- Easy bridging to and from Ethereum and other L2s.

**Why Base Matters for Pixel Journey Explorers**: Base's low-cost, high-speed environment is ideal for the kind of rich, interactive mechanics we're building with PxPackages — from advanced crafting to real-time community experiences. It's another powerful tool in our multi-chain toolkit.

## How Base Works (Simplified)

Base is an **Optimistic Rollup** on Ethereum. This means:

- It processes transactions off the main Ethereum chain (for speed and low cost).
- It periodically posts transaction data back to Ethereum for security and finality.
- You still benefit from Ethereum's security while enjoying much better performance.

You don't need to understand the technical details to enjoy it — what matters is that it feels fast, cheap, and familiar if you've used other EVM chains.

## ETH on Base

The native token is still **ETH** (same as Ethereum). It's used for:

- Paying transaction fees (very low on Base).
- Interacting with dApps and NFTs.
- Bridging assets between chains.

**Practical Tip**: Keep a small amount of ETH on Base for gas. Most wallets make bridging easy.

## NFTs on Base

Base uses standard EVM NFT standards (ERC-721, ERC-1155), so collections work similarly to Ethereum and Polygon. Marketplaces like OpenSea and Blur support Base, and new Base-native platforms are emerging.

**Pixel Journey Connection**: Our multi-chain collectibles and future PxPackages-powered experiences will feel right at home here — fast interactions, low costs, and easy bridging back to WAX or other chains.
