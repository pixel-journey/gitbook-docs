# Base 101

> **Shared EVM Fundamentals First**: Before diving into Base-specific details, make sure you've read the [EVM Hub](https://docs.pixeljourney.xyz/education/getting-set-up-on-evm) (especially [EVM 101](https://docs.pixeljourney.xyz/education/getting-set-up-on-evm/evm-101.md)). It covers the common concepts (gas, addresses, ERC standards, bridging, wallets) that apply across all EVM chains, including Base. This page focuses on what makes **Base** unique.

**Welcome to Base on the Pixel Journey!** 🚀

Base is a high-performance Ethereum Layer 2 incubated by Coinbase. It brings Ethereum's security and ecosystem with much lower fees and faster speeds — making it one of the best environments for onboarding new collectors and for casual-to-serious NFT collecting.

As part of our multi-chain journey (WAX roots in 2023, 1050+ days strong), Base is a natural extension. With **PxPackages** enabling modular cross-chain experiences, Base offers an excellent low-friction environment for future features and community growth, leading toward the **PxLearn Portal**.

## What Makes Base Special

- **Coinbase Backed**: Strong security, liquidity, and mainstream credibility from day one.
- **EVM Compatible**: Works seamlessly with MetaMask and all Ethereum tools.
- **Very Low Fees & Fast**: Transactions are cheap and quick — ideal for frequent minting, trading, and interacting with dApps.
- **Growing Ecosystem**: Strong NFT and DeFi scene with increasing developer activity.
- **Easy Onboarding**: Excellent for people coming from WAX or new to EVM chains.

**Why Base Matters for Pixel Journey**: Its low cost and speed make advanced mechanics (crafting, interactive collectibles, community events) feel seamless. It’s a great place to test and launch PxPackages-powered experiences.

---

## Base-Specific Practical Notes

- **Native Token**: ETH (same as Ethereum).
- **Marketplaces**: OpenSea, Blur, and emerging Base-native platforms.
- **Bridging**: Use the official Base Bridge or Across Protocol for moving assets from Ethereum or other L2s.
- **Gas**: Very low — one of the cheapest EVM environments.
