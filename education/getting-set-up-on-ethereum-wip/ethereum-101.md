## What Makes Ethereum Special

Ethereum was the first blockchain to bring smart contracts to the mainstream. It powers a massive ecosystem of DeFi, NFTs, DAOs, and decentralized applications.

**Key Strengths:**
- **Pioneer Status**: The original smart contract platform — most of Web3 was built on or inspired by Ethereum.
- **Strong Security & Decentralization**: Battle-tested with the largest developer and validator community.
- **Massive Ecosystem**: Thousands of dApps, the deepest liquidity in DeFi, and the most established NFT infrastructure.
- **L2 Scaling Solutions**: While mainnet fees can be high during congestion, Layer 2s like Base, Arbitrum, and Optimism bring Ethereum's security with much lower costs and faster speeds.

For collectors and learners, Ethereum offers:
- Deep liquidity and mature marketplaces.
- The most battle-tested smart contract standards (ERC-721, ERC-1155, etc.).
- A huge variety of projects and communities to explore.

**Why It Matters for Pixel Journey**: Understanding Ethereum helps you see where many Web3 ideas originated and how our multi-chain vision (WAX + high-performance chains + L2s) creates richer experiences. PxPackages will help bridge and unify these worlds.

## How Ethereum Works (Simplified)

Ethereum uses **Proof of Stake (PoS)** consensus. Validators stake ETH to secure the network and process transactions.

Key concepts:
- **Gas Fees**: Every transaction costs gas (paid in ETH). Fees fluctuate based on network demand.
- **Smart Contracts**: Self-executing code that powers dApps, NFTs, and DeFi.
- **Accounts**: Externally Owned Accounts (EOAs — your wallet) and Contract Accounts.
- **Layer 2s (L2s)**: Solutions built on top of Ethereum that inherit its security while offering lower fees and higher speed (Base is one example).

You interact with Ethereum through wallets like MetaMask by approving transactions.

## ETH: The Native Token

**ETH** is used for:

- Paying gas fees.
- Staking to secure the network and earn rewards.
- Interacting with dApps and NFTs.
- Bridging between Ethereum and L2s or other chains.

## NFTs on Ethereum

Ethereum pioneered the standards that most NFTs use today:

| Standard   | Use Case                  | Notes                              |
|------------|---------------------------|------------------------------------|
| ERC-721   | Unique NFTs (1-of-1)     | Most common for PFPs & art        |
| ERC-1155  | Multi-token (fungible + non-fungible) | Efficient for games & collections |

Major marketplaces: OpenSea, Blur, Foundation, etc.

**Pixel Journey Note**: Many of our multi-chain inspirations and future PxPackages integrations draw from Ethereum's mature standards while bringing WAX-style accessibility and fun mechanics.

## Pixel Journey Tips for Ethereum Explorers

- **Start with an L2 if Possible**: Base or other L2s give you the Ethereum experience with much lower fees while you learn.
- **Use MetaMask**: The most popular wallet for Ethereum and L2s.
- **Watch Gas Prices**: Use tools like etherscan.io/gastracker or your wallet's fee estimator.
- **Security First**: Always verify contracts and use official bridges. Read our security tips.
- **Think Multi-Chain**: Bridges let you move assets between WAX, Ethereum, Base, Solana, and more as PxPackages grows.
- **Learn the Standards**: Understanding ERC-721/1155 helps you appreciate why AtomicAssets on WAX feels so creator-friendly.
- **Join the Community**: Share what you discover in Discord — we're all learning together.
