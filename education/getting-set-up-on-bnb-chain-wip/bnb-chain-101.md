# BNB Chain 101

**Welcome to BNB Chain on the Pixel Journey!** 🚀

BNB Chain (formerly Binance Smart Chain) is a fast, low-cost, EVM-compatible blockchain with a massive DeFi and NFT ecosystem. It offers excellent performance while staying connected to the broader Ethereum-compatible world. As we expand our multi-chain adventure (WAX roots in 2023, Pixals Spring 2024, 1050+ days strong), BNB Chain represents another powerful and accessible environment for collectors, creators, and learners.

Our journey is built on flexible milestone-driven Seasons and the growing power of **PxPackages** (modular multi-chain tools). BNB Chain fits beautifully into this vision, offering high-performance experiences with strong liquidity and community — all while we prepare for the standalone **PxLearn Portal**.

## What Makes BNB Chain Special

BNB Chain is an EVM-compatible blockchain that prioritizes speed and low costs. It uses a Proof of Staked Authority (PoSA) consensus, which allows for fast block times and very low fees.

**Key Advantages:**
- **EVM Compatible**: Works seamlessly with MetaMask and all your favorite Ethereum tools and dApps.
- **Very Low Fees**: Transactions often cost just a fraction of a cent.
- **High Speed**: Fast block times (around 3 seconds).
- **Strong Ecosystem**: Excellent DeFi (PancakeSwap, etc.) and growing NFT scene with deep liquidity from Binance integration.
- **Binance Backing**: Strong liquidity, user base, and mainstream adoption potential.

For collectors and learners, this means:
- Affordable minting, trading, and DeFi participation.
- Easy bridging to and from Ethereum and other chains.
- A vibrant community and many projects to explore.

**Why It Matters for Pixel Journey**: BNB Chain offers great performance and liquidity with EVM familiarity. It’s an excellent environment for testing future PxPackages-powered experiences and reaching a broad audience of explorers.

## How BNB Chain Works (Simplified)

BNB Chain runs parallel to Ethereum but with its own consensus. It processes transactions quickly and cheaply.

You interact with it almost exactly like Ethereum:
- Use MetaMask (add the BNB Chain network).
- Pay fees in BNB (the native token).
- Bridge assets between chains.

It feels fast and affordable while staying connected to the broader EVM ecosystem.

## BNB Token

The native token is **BNB**. It’s used for:

- Paying transaction fees.
- Staking and participating in governance.
- Interacting with dApps and NFTs.

You’ll need a small amount of BNB to use the chain.

## NFTs on BNB Chain

BNB Chain uses standard EVM NFT standards (ERC-721, ERC-1155), so most tools and marketplaces from Ethereum work here too. Major platforms like OpenSea support BNB Chain collections, and there are active native marketplaces as well.

**Pixel Journey Connection**: Many of our multi-chain inspirations and future bridging/crafting mechanics will feel natural on BNB Chain — fast, affordable, and EVM-friendly with strong liquidity.
