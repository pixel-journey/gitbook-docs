**PxWallet** is your command center for the entire Pixel Journey ecosystem. Track balances, manage resources (CPU/NET/RAM), handle multisig, and control every asset across your WAX accounts — all in one beautiful, offline-capable interface.

## 🛡️ Core Capabilities

- **Live Resource Gauges**: Real-time CPU, NET, and RAM visualization with one-click PowerUp actions.
- **Multi-Account Management**: Add, switch, and monitor multiple WAX accounts with permission-level control.
- **Token & NFT Inventory**: Unified view of all your $PXJ, $WPIXAL, $KING, and Pixal collections.
- **Transaction History & Analytics**: Detailed on-chain activity with export options.
- **Offline Vault**: Encrypted local storage for keys and queued actions that sync when online.
- **PWA + Push Notifications**: Installable, works beautifully on mobile, with battle and reward alerts.
