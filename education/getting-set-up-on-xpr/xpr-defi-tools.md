## Safety, DYOR & Best Practices

- Always verify protocols and contract addresses from official sources.
- Start small with new pools or strategies to understand risks (impermanent loss, smart contract risks).
- Use transaction previews and enable wallet simulations.
- DYOR on yields, teams, and tokenomics — high APYs often come with higher risks.
- Revoke approvals regularly and use hardware wallets for significant positions.
- Be cautious with bridges — test small amounts first.
