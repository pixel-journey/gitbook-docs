# XPR Network 101 — Feeless Antelope Basics for Pixel Journey Explorers

**XPR Network** (formerly ProtonChain) is a high-performance, feeless Layer 1 blockchain built on the Antelope (EOSIO) framework — the same tech family as WAX. This makes it immediately familiar if you've used WAX accounts, resources, or NFTs.

## What Makes XPR Network Special

- **Feeless for Users**: Transactions feel free for end users thanks to the protocol design and resource model. No more worrying about small fees for every action.
- **Account-Based**: Human-readable account names (like on WAX) instead of long hex addresses.
- **Built-in DeFi Primitives**: Native support for swaps, lending, and staking directly on-chain.
- **NFT Ecosystem**: Growing marketplace (XPR Market) with easy minting and trading.
- **High Speed & Scalability**: Fast finality suitable for gaming, DeFi, and social features.

**Why It Matters for Pixel Journey**: XPR offers a low-friction environment to experiment with Antelope-native tools, bridge assets from WAX, or explore new DeFi/NFT opportunities while staying in a familiar account model. It's a natural complement to WAX for cost-effective activities.

## Core Concepts

### Accounts & Permissions
- Accounts are human-readable (e.g., `youraccount`).
- Permissions (owner/active) control actions — similar to WAX.
- Easy to create and manage multiple accounts.

### Resources (RAM, CPU, NET)
- Like WAX, you stake or allocate resources for transactions and storage.
- XPR emphasizes feeless UX, so resource management is often abstracted or subsidized for users.

### Tokens & Assets
- Native token: **XPR** — used for staking, governance, fees (if any), and DeFi.
- Tokens follow standard Antelope patterns (similar to WAX tokens).
- NFTs use compatible standards for easy bridging/minting.

### DeFi & Governance
- Built-in swap and lending primitives.
- Governance through staking and voting on proposals.
- XPR Market for NFTs with trading and discovery features.

## XPR Network vs. WAX

| Feature | WAX | XPR Network |
|---------|-----|-------------|
| Fees | Resource-based (CPU/NET/RAM) | Feeless for most user actions |
| Accounts | Human-readable | Human-readable |
| DeFi | Alcor, TacoSwap, etc. | Native primitives + XPR Swap |
| NFTs | AtomicHub, F12Key, etc. | XPR Market |
| Focus | NFTs, gaming, carbon-negative | Feeless UX, DeFi, mainstream adoption |

Both share the Antelope DNA, so skills transfer easily. XPR adds feeless convenience and integrated DeFi.

## Getting Started Basics
1. Create or import an account (via WebAuth Wallet or Metal Pay).
2. Acquire some XPR (via exchanges, on-ramps, or bridging).
3. Explore XPR Market for NFTs or try native swaps.
4. Stake for governance or use DeFi tools.

**Pixel Journey Tip**: Start small, bridge a test amount from WAX, and experiment with the feeless environment. It's perfect for quick interactions while your main collection stays on WAX.
